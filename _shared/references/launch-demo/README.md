# The 187-film study

Before writing any rule, the studio studied 187 product launch and product demo motion-graphics films from YouTube: one card per film with its structure, visual style, how the product UI is shown, motion and transitions, pacing, sound, what to take from it and what to avoid, plus measured numbers (cuts, shot lengths, loudness, tempo, sound events, words per minute).

- **[PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md)**: the rules distilled from all 187 cards, each claim citing the card and the time stamp it comes from. Agents read it before writing a brief, a previs or a shot list for a launch or demo film.
- **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)**: every film with its link, length, type, rating and voice pace, grouped (launch, demo, explainer, brand/ad, agency talks), plus the fifteen to watch first.
- **[cards/](cards/)**: one study card per film (`cards/<youtube-id>.md`), written from [CARD-TEMPLATE.md](CARD-TEMPLATE.md).
- **data/**: the measured numbers per film. `tools/stats.py` prints the medians per group.

What the evidence can and cannot tell: 180 of the 187 films come from one agency (What a Story), so the library shows one house style in depth; the numbers come from automatic tools (see the caveats at the top of the playbook). The films belong to their creators: only notes, links and numbers are here, never their frames, audio or scripts.
