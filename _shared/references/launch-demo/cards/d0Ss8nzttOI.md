# Gataca Studio: 2D character explainer with a 99-steps to 1-step bookend

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d0Ss8nzttOI · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:30
- **Type:** explainer (2D characters + UI) · **Product:** Gataca Studio, a low-code platform for self-sovereign identity: users scan a QR code with a wallet app and are verified with one click
- **Numbers:** 10.0 transitions per min (18 detected cuts, several in a burst at 1:03–1:04) · median shot 2.9 s (longest 26.4 s) · 107 words/min · ~112 BPM · 136 sound events/min · -17.6 LUFS

- 0:35–0:58 User journey: a grid of avatars slides away to one user (0:36–0:38); he scans a QR code on a web page (0:40); a hand holds a phone showing "Wallet connected" (0:43); the admin ticks Required Credentials on a list (0:47); the phone shows the same items as consent toggles (0:51); the page shows a green "Verified" pill (0:55).

- 1:12–1:30 Payoff and CTA: the opening window returns as "1 Step to Onboard", bar full, queue gone (1:13–1:17); "Successfully Onboarded" check (1:21); wordmark with a free-trial pill and URL (1:29).

## Visual style
Flat 2D vector characters on two stages: white with pale line-art rooms, and saturated indigo for product scenes. Indigo, teal and one hot pink-red for problems. Rounded geometric sans, white UI cards with soft shadows, green pills for success.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Simplified redrawn UI in a browser frame; the dashboard keeps a real layout (sidebar, stat tiles, charts).
- Live counters tick while charts draw (0:24–0:32): the product runs while you watch.
- Two-sided demo: the admin's checklist (0:47) mirrored by the user's phone toggles (0:51), cause and effect back to back.
- Large drawn hands hold the phone (0:43, 0:51), so taps feel physical.
- Green status pills are the result of each action.

## Motion and transitions
Masked slides and pans: the avatar grid slides off and the chosen user stays (0:36–0:38); stages swap white ↔ indigo with a wipe. The bravura move is the walk cycle at 1:02–1:04: websites slide under the walking figure like a treadmill, each flipping to verified. Snappy ease-out with small overshoots on pops.

## Pacing
Voice at 107 words/min, but pictures change every ~3 s, so it feels faster than the voice. The dashboard holds longest (about 12 s), kept alive by the counters. Breathes on the logo (0:13) and end card.

## Sound
Bed near 112 BPM, -17.6 LUFS. Single narrator (timbre not checked; the study reads captions and numbers). Script: brand line, a "want to know how" question, the journey in order of use, pains removed, CTA. 136 sound events per minute: pops on callouts, ticks on checks and toggles, swishes on wipes.

## What to take from it
- 0:02 vs 1:13: bookend. The same frame opens as "99 steps" and returns as "1 step".
- 0:24–0:32: counters ticking while charts draw; data that moves without a cursor.
- 0:47 and 0:51: the admin sets a rule, the user's phone shows it, back to back.
- 1:02–1:04: a figure walking across a treadmill of windows, each flipping to "Verified": a run of results in about 2 s.

## Avoid
- Self / Sovereign / Identity Venn circles (0:17): a concept slide with no product.
- Red-crossed icons floating round the character (1:06–1:10): dated trope.
- Cartoon characters: wrong register for a developer tool; take the grammar only.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
