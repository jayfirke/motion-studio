## Visual style
Midnight navy, one purple-to-cyan accent family; glass orbs are the only 3D. Rounded geometric sans, white, at two sizes: single small words in empty space, or a huge wordmark. A recurring layout puts the UI panel in the centre with one short label on each side (0:29, 0:31). Self-aware and fun rather than corporate.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real extension UI, first inside a laptop browser (0:16), then as flat floating panels at readable size.
- The cursor types into search, then clicks to add a coin (0:31).
- Cause and effect in one shot: button pressed, counter increments (0:41–0:44).
- News cards double as content and transition material: they swarm, orbit and fly past camera.

## Motion and transitions
- **Zoom-through** a card swarm with radial blur (12.6 s).
- **Type slam:** heavy motion blur, then RGB echoes settling (13.5–14.4 s).
- **Slab corridor** of vertical colour panels (23.7 s).
- **Wipe into the reveal:** a word smears off, a white disc wipes, a particle burst opens the build-up (32.7–32.8 s).
- Snappy easing that fits about 160 BPM. The carry is an orb or dot that survives between beats (0:01 → 0:04 → 0:26).

## Pacing
One idea per shot; single words hold about 1 s on empty frames. Brisk VO (128 wpm). It breathes on purpose before the gm reveal: about 8 s of slow orbit build-up (0:34–0:41) to set up the joke.

## Sound
A fast electronic track (~160 BPM) that builds into the reveal. Casual, witty VO: problem, product, two features, an overblown setup for a tiny feature ("presenting the GM button"), CTA. Moderate SFX (about 47 events/min), likely whooshes on zooms and hits on slams.

## What to take from it
- Mock-epic reveal: a cinematic build that pays off on one small UI action (0:34–0:44). Real engagement.
- Press plus counter tick in one shot (0:41–0:44).
- Zoom-through swarm to dramatise information overload (0:09–0:13).
- Centre panel with one label on each side, readable on a phone (0:29–0:31).
- An orb that carries across boundaries as a through-line (0:01–0:26).

## Avoid
- RGB-split echo type and heavy smear (13.5–14.4 s): glitch.
- Falling-streak rain, tumbling confetti slabs (0:49), particle bursts (32.8 s).
- Glow on the button press (0:44); use shadow and scale.
