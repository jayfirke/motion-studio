## Visual style
Corporate footage (offices, hands, phones, servers, Alps) cut with front-on and tilted product UI on white. Pastel pink-lavender fields for UI beats; a pink-blue neon gradient only for the opening stroke. White humanist sans captions, two-tier, angled on motion shots.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product UI, full-frame or tilted 15–25°.
- Components lift out of the plane: the "Add member" button and status pills float up in 3D with soft shadows (0:21).
- Cursor present on lists and the white transition (0:14–0:15).
- UI composited onto real monitors and phones in footage (0:06, 0:29), so the product is seen in use.
- Modals pop over a dimmed page (0:21).
- No typing: each UI shot lasts 1–2 s and shows one state.

## Motion and transitions
Fast cutting to the beat: 28 cuts in 48 s. Shots link through motion: the light stroke morphs (curve → signature → button) and the button carries into the monitor footage; captions whip in with motion blur; the triptych panels slide in one after another; tilted UI drifts in the direction of the previous camera move. Snappy ease-out, no bounce.

## Pacing
High energy, a shot every 1.3 s, all held by music; text is one or two short lines per shot. The trust act strings five one-line claims in 7 s. No rest until the logo.

## Sound
Music-led, no narration (no caption words at all). Bed near 103 BPM, -17.0 LUFS; 121 sound events per minute fits cuts landing on beats and small hits on the whips. Genre and SFX not checked by ear.

## What to take from it
- 0:01–0:06: one light stroke morphs from curve to signature to button and then the button is pressed on a real screen; the product's core action as a single carry.
- 0:12–0:13: three footage strips slide in one per beat, one word each; a fast way to land three benefits.
- 0:21: UI button and status pills lift off the tilted page into 3D.
- 0:29: product UI composited into a hand-held phone in footage.

## Avoid
- No voice at all; the owner wants dense narration that explains.
- Generic stock claims over stock footage (server racks for security, Alps for "Swiss Made").
- Node-network brand graphic (0:41): dated corporate cliché.
- UI shots at 1–2 s are too short to read a demo; good for a sizzle, not for teaching.
