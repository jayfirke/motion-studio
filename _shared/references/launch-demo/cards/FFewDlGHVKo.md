## Visual style
Flat 2D with thick black outlines, brand yellow as the hero colour, green and blue accents, confetti dots and small squares scattered on white. Characters are local construction-trade people drawn simply. Type is a bold rounded sans used sparingly (a stat, the wordmark). UI is real: white web app with yellow buttons, and a chat app on outlined phones.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI in outlined phone and tablet frames, or full-bleed for the web app.
- A white glove hand cursor presses the Add Vendors pill: the hand dips, the pill darkens, then the next screen arrives (0:27).
- Forms show the actual inputs: truck plate chips, quantity field, Next button (1:00).
- The chat app carries the story: bot messages appear as bubbles in sequence (0:56, 1:13).
- A character holds the phone in close-up so the hand and the screen read at the same time (0:43).

## Motion and transitions
Two big bursts: yellow bars wiping across with speed dashes (0:07–0:08), and an anime-style blue energy explosion with a shrinking ring and impact star that carries into the UI (0:24–0:26). Otherwise elements pop in with small overshoot and the camera pans between grouped scenes. The logo hub is the carry object that comes back several times.

## Pacing
Moderate: 93 words/min with music gaps between lines, about one new screen every 4 s. The narration is short slogan-like phrases, so the picture often holds after the voice stops. Feels slower than its cut count suggests.

## Sound
Narrator reading short punchy feature phrases; script is benefit slogans rather than sentences. Energetic electronic bed around 136 BPM, fairly loud (-15.6 LUFS). SFX on pops, the hand click and the two transition bursts.

## What to take from it
- Hand cursor press on a real pill button, then the next screen lands (0:27).
- Close-up of a hand holding a phone with real UI, mixing person and product in one frame (0:43).
- A real form card (chips, quantity, Next) shown long enough to read (1:00).
- Tangle untangling into a neat hub as the problem-to-solution turn (0:14–0:18).

## Avoid
- Anime energy burst with lightning streaks (0:24–0:26): glitchy and loud.
- Ringing phone icon grid and confetti dots: generic explainer filler.
- Slogan-only narration leaves dead air; the owner wants denser voice.
