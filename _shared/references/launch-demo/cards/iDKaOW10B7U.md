## What to take from it
- A problem told in five one-object beats in 14 s: a chat line, a map, a calendar, flying coins (0:01–0:14).
- The user's avatar riding the cursor so the viewer knows who acts (0:23.2, 0:31.4).
- A window tilted in 3D to mark "this is the other side's tool" (0:28.7, 0:34.1).
- The honest "works with your current system" beat, shown as logos around the mark (0:42.3).
- A CTA of three short pills that stack over footage in order (0:53–0:57).

## Avoid
- Stock sports footage and the black-and-white echo (0:02.4): close to a glitch.
- Falling coins and alert triangles (0:12): confetti-like.
- The badge in the corner: a corner label.
- Phone mockups for everything: they cost screen space.
