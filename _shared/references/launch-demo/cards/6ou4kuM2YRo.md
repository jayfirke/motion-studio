## What to take from it
- **Real UI with a cursor doing real tasks (0:39–0:54):** a form filled, a modal opened, a table row clicked. It reads as a demo because it is one.
- **Stat pill that becomes the thing it counts (0:19–0:22):** "10 Million Monthly Searches" shrinks into a cloud of search bars.
- **Grid pop-out (0:25):** one card lifts out of the trending grid to make a point about one creator.
- **Bold "Why Us" pivot card (0:10):** a single kinetic headline that turns the argument.

## Avoid
- The sticker or heart burst (0:28), which is close to the banned confetti.
- Decorative black discs at frame edges that do no job.
- Tables held without a highlight become hard to read at phone size.
