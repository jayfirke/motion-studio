## Visual style
Editorial, Swiss-grid minimalism: off-white canvas, very large black geometric sans, flat colour blocks (ochre, butter yellow, red-orange, cornflower blue, forest green), the real product's aubergine sidebar. Big numbers as heroes. No gradients, no glow, no device frames.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI at full fidelity, front-on and flat, cropped tight to the area that matters.
- Small arrow cursor near the action (Connect at 0:16, Validate at 0:37); modals pop in side by side to show a two-step setup.
- Typed instruction in a coaching field followed by a preview of the agent's new reply (0:37).
- Numbers do the proving: a counter that ticks (0:22) and an odometer roll to 83 % (0:46).
