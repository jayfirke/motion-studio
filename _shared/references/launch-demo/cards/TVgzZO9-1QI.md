# 3D Explainer Video for Content Marketing (WriterZen)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVgzZO9-1QI · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:37
- **Type:** explainer (feature tour) · **Product:** WriterZen, an SEO toolkit for topic research, keyword clustering, AI writing and team management
- **Numbers:** 2.5 transitions per min · median shot 15.2 s (shortest 13.0 s, longest 53.2 s) · 98 words/min · ~144 BPM · 118.6 sound events/min · −20.2 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:08 Hook: a glossy 3D pink heart bubble with an "SEO" magnifier, ringed by avatars. It cracks in two (0:06) as the voice jokes about it.
- 0:10–0:16 Problem: a blue glass hourglass surrounded by four juggled tasks, which then scatter.
- 0:18 Solution: the logo on a pale field.
- 0:22–0:30 Overview: a tilted dashboard on royal blue, a row of five module cards, then a loop of spheres (end-to-end).
- 0:34–0:42 Topic Discovery: "Nutri" is typed into the keyword field, and topic result cards fill in.
- 0:42–0:50 Keywords: the cursor clicks Search, a filter slider panel opens, and clusters lay out as cards.
- 0:53 Interrupt: a hard cut to a giant black "Wait" on white.
- 0:58–1:10 AI writing: an outline, then a macro close-up of the Title / Description / Word Count fields (1:02). The cursor opens an "AI Shortcut" menu on "Write for me", text fills the editor, and the cursor clicks Done.
- 1:18–1:24 Teams: add writers in Project Settings, then a projects table with status pills and avatar stacks.
- 1:31–1:37 Wrap: panels slide off left, then the logo, a "Get started today" pill and the URL.

## Visual style
- Hybrid: glossy 3D props (heart, hourglass, spheres, an orange pencil, cubes) with flat, real UI cards.
- Two alternating stages, pale cream-to-sky and saturated royal blue. The stage swap marks a new module.
- Orange props against blue UI. A bold geometric sans; "Wait" fills about 30% of frame height.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product screens, cropped tight and shown large as floating white cards. No device frame.
- A black arrow cursor drives every step: typing (0:34), Search (0:42), "Write for me" (1:06), Done (1:10).
- **Typing, then a cascade:** a short query, then result cards stagger in. The cause is visible and the payoff immediate.
- **Macro field zoom** (1:02): only three labels fill the frame, then the camera pulls back.
- Menus open as real dropdowns, not fades (1:06). One perspective tilt on the first dashboard (0:22); later screens sit flat-on so they read.

## Motion and transitions
- No detected cut for the first 53 s. Props, cards and stages morph, slide and push into each other.
- Only three hard cuts, all as punctuation: "Wait" (53.2 s), into the AI menu (67.9 s), into the team rows (83.6 s).
- Stage colour swaps are wiped by sliding UI panels. Snappy ease-outs; props drift between clicks.

## Pacing
- Fairly dense: 98 words/min, with a new UI state about every 4 s from 0:34 to 1:24.
- A chatty script whose short jokes act as rests. The "Wait" card is a 1-beat breath before the best feature.

## Sound
- A bright bed at about 144 BPM. Quiet at −20.2 LUFS.
- A conversational, playful narrator, for example "wait it doesn't end here".
- SFX likely sit on clicks and card entrances, but can't be confirmed.

## What to take from it
- **Type a query, then the results cascade** (0:34 → 0:38). The core beat for showing Claude Code at work.
- **A hard cut to one giant word as a verbal interrupt** (0:53). A cut used as a hit, before the best feature.
- **A macro push to field level, then pull back** (1:02). It makes a small detail feel important.
- **A cursor-opened menu writing into the doc** (1:06 → 1:10). AI action as visible cause and effect.
- **A stage colour change per module** (0:22, 0:42, 1:23). Chapters without title cards.

## Avoid
- Floating 3D props with no job (pyramids, cubes, the pencil everywhere). This breaks "premium means subtract".
- Heart and hourglass metaphors that delay the product for 16 s.
- A centred logo on a pale field (0:18, 1:35). Tiny UI text on wide shots (0:26).

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Talk to Claude Code:** type the owner's one-line prompt into the real terminal with audible keys. On Enter, the files Claude writes (BRIEF.md, SHOTLIST.md, the previs page) cascade in as cards.

- **Previs Studio tour:** a macro zoom onto one A/B/C option chip, a cursor click, then pull back to the timeline swapping the take.
