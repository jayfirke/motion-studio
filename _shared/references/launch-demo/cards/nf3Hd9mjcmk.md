## Visual style
Two worlds by design: grainy, vignetted black-and-white archival footage for the old way, a bright mid-blue stage with white cards of the real site for the new way. Mint-green buttons, navy text. Clean, flat, front-on; no device frames.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real website UI on cards, front-on, cropped to the part being discussed.
- Typing in the hook (search bar) and in the closing line.
- Cursor fills the calculator, then the result card pops forward out of the page (0:11–0:12): the payoff of the interaction.
- A chat thread builds bubble by bubble (0:17–0:19); the rates table scrolls in place (0:24–0:26).
- End card with a real button and a visible cursor click (0:36).

## Motion and transitions
Circle masks carry between the two worlds: archival footage grows in a circle from the edge, then the circle shrinks away while the next UI card rises from below over it (0:13.7, 0:19.7, 0:22.8). Quick ease-out, nothing floaty. The repeated mask works as a rhythmic device because it alternates with the UI every 3–4 s.

## Pacing
123 words/min in a 40 s spot, a new picture every 3–4 s, four parallel pairs on one sentence pattern: you could do the old thing, or the new thing with the brand. The repetition makes it easy to follow at speed. Only rest is the end card (0:36–0:40).

## Sound
Upbeat bed near 112 BPM, loud and clean at -15.1 LUFS, 127 sound events per minute (clicks and type ticks on UI, swishes on masks). The script is built entirely on the contrast pattern ("You could haggle with every bank"), then a URL.

## What to take from it
- 0:07–0:31: four old-versus-new pairs on one repeated sentence pattern, archival black-and-white for the old way and bright real UI for the new.
- 0:00: frame 0 is an action (typing in a search bar); the hook is a demo.
- 0:11–0:12: cursor fills a form, then the result card pops forward out of the page.
- 0:13.7–0:16: footage circle shrinks while the next UI card rises over it; the carry between worlds.
- 0:36: the cursor clicks the CTA button.

## Avoid
- Gold laurel award badges (0:04): decorative and dated.
- Question-mark field (0:27): a cliché for "confused".
- Circle masks used more than three times start to read as the iris-wipe cliché.
- Archival stock footage of real people needs a clear licence before we borrow the idea with our own material.
