## Visual style
Flat 2D collage of stock footage, flat illustration and real UI. A peach base with big diagonal fields of teal, orange and magenta that re-slice each section. A rounded sans in white or orange pills, never bare. Dot grids and line ornaments sit in the corners. Depth comes from overlapping cards and shadows.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Real screenshots:** cleaned up, in a floating rounded window tilted about 5–10° with a soft shadow, and no device frame.
- **Lifted buttons:** the key button is pulled out of the UI as an enlarged pill on the background, and a black arrow cursor clicks it (1:19, 1:21), so the action reads at phone size.
- **Zoom:** detail first (0:50), then a zoom out to the full screen (1:00).
- **Scrolling:** a long scroll becomes vertical motion blur plus a counter badge.
- **UI as graphic:** fragments become graphics (avatar rows with amounts and checks) instead of full screens.

## Motion and transitions
- **Carry:** the diagonal background re-slices to new colours while the UI window stays put.
- **Distinct transitions:** a slide push (0:20), a speed-line scatter (0:27), a liquid blob (0:37) and a circular wipe (1:39).
- **Easing:** icons and badges pop with light overshoot; windows ease in smoothly.

## Pacing
- **VO:** brisk at 122 wpm, with short verb phrases each matched to an on-screen change.
- **Sections:** the problem section is busy (four visual ideas in 30 s); features run one screen per verb, with UI holds of 5–10 s.
- **No breaths:** the music never stops.

## Sound
- **Music:** upbeat pop-electronic at about 123 BPM.
- **Voice:** a friendly ad read, second person, with rhetorical questions and a playful aside, "did we mention it's the most affordable solution".
- **SFX:** pops and swishes on badges and transitions, with the VO on top.

## What to take from it
- **0:13–0:20:** pull back to a wall of thousands of tiles, then land one stat card, which shows problem scale in one move.
- **1:19 and 1:21:** the button lifted out of the UI as an oversized pill and clicked on the background. A phone-readable click.
- **0:50–1:00:** start inside the UI at a readable crop, then zoom out to the whole screen.
- **1:10:** a counter badge plus a motion-blurred list for "lots of results".
- **1:39:** a circular wipe into a dashboard, kept as the one star transition.

## Avoid
- **Stock footage of people at laptops:** it breaks "real product".
- **Four transition styles in one film,** against our one-star rule.
- **Clip-art icons and a multicolour palette,** beyond our one-accent rule.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
