- **Turn (0:12–0:20):** same framing as 0:04, but the bubble turns teal ("0 KYC Forms") and the laptop shows the logo. A three-circle Venn (0:16) collapses into one ring.

## Visual style
- Classic corporate flat 2D: a purple primary with teal (good) and raspberry (bad) accents, on white or a solid purple field.
- Big-headed characters in a simple line style; the backgrounds are drawn in thin outline.
- UI is redrawn to match the illustration style.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn browser windows and phones held in illustrated hands.
- No mouse cursor: the character's finger and the phone hand do the interacting (ticking boxes at 0:45, scanning at 0:41).
- Numbers count up and bars grow to show the dashboard is live.
- Status pills pop in as payoffs.

## Motion and transitions
- **Colour as story:** the red bubble flips to teal in the same framing (0:04 → 0:12).
- Bold split-screen wipes in purple and white (0:23, 0:35); the detector logs several "cuts" for each.
- The walk cycle carries the camera across windows (0:59).
- **Bookend:** the opening street returns with "99" now "1" (1:13). Clean ease-outs.

## What to take from it
- **Before/after bookend (0:00 vs 1:13):** the same street, "99 Steps" becomes "1 Step". The payoff is a match of the opening.
- **Same-frame state flip (0:04 → 0:12):** keep the framing, swap the red problem bubble for the teal solution one.
- **Counting stats (0:23–0:27):** numbers tick up as the chart grows, so the dashboard feels alive.
- **Walk-past verification (0:59–1:03):** one moving subject triggers a "Verified" pill on each window, a rhythmic run of payoffs.

## Avoid
- Generic big-head corporate characters and Venn diagrams. They date it.
- No real cursor or real UI, so the demo feels illustrative, not proof.
- A pop sound on every pill.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Hook and Results bookend:** open on the slow, manual way (a long checklist of steps), then close the Results chapter on the same frame showing "1 prompt".
- **"Build and critique":** a same-frame state flip, where the critique score chip changes from red 6 to green 8 in place.
- **"Any agent":** a run of agent logos where each gets a "works" check as the camera passes, like the walk-past verification.
