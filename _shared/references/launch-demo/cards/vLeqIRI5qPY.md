## Visual style
Kinetic typography plus real UI. Black, white and brand yellow cycle by feature. One bold geometric sans in caps at about 8–10 % of frame height. Wide tracking for emphasis, tight tracking for statements. Plain macOS browser windows with soft shadows. No illustration, gradients or characters.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI in a plain browser window.
- **Title → window → full frame:** the window docks under its title, then shoves the title out.
- **Count-up** on a real number while the table fills.
- **Typing** into a URL field with inline validation, then a dimmed overlay and a modal (0:28–0:31).
- No visible cursor: the UI moves on the music.

## Motion and transitions
Hard cuts on the beat (31 in 41 s), the burst-of-hits case the house rules allow. The window-pushes-title move links name and UI in one gesture. A letterbox reveal and a word split around a device. Snappy fast-out, hard-stop easing with no bounce. A highlighter swipe on the CTA.

## Pacing
A new card about every beat. Words hold about 0.5 s, windows 1.5–2 s and the logo end about 10 s, so the shot-length variety is strong. The type carries all the meaning; the only rests are the lockup and the offer.

## Sound
Music only, with very dense onsets (216/min) that suggest every type cut lands on a hit. No voice. -18.8 LUFS. The SFX timbre isn't confirmed.

## What to take from it
- **The window pushes its title out** (0:14, 0:19): a carry from chapter name to real UI.
- **Word → full name → UI** three-step rhythm per feature.
- **Count-up on a real number** (0:19–0:21).
- **Type-only problem statement** (0:00–0:06): weight and tracking do the acting.
- **Highlighter sweep** on the one word that matters (0:40).

## Avoid
- No VO at all. The owner wants dense narration: take the grammar, not the silence.
- 40 s of all-caps headlines gets monotonous.
- Scattered dots around "INTRODUCING": near particle bursts.
- 1.5 s UI holds are too short to read at phone size.
