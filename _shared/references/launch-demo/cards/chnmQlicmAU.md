## Visual style
Flat 2D vector with grain: cobalt blue and warm cream, yellow-shirt hero, coral accents. Big diagonal blue slabs and soft blobs as grounds. Labels are small italic all-caps pills set at angles. Real UI screenshots dropped in flat, no device frame.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Per feature: an illustrated metaphor (dial, tag cloud, review bubbles), then the real screen that answers it.
- Real UI, frontal, slid or scaled in as cropped panels; one small cursor on the category tree (0:57), otherwise no clicks or typing.
- Callouts are pills floating over the UI (red revenue pill, 0:39).

## Motion and transitions
Only 11 cuts in 145 s; almost everything is continuous. Carries: a diagonal blue slab wipes across and the next scene is underneath (1:40); a white pill expands to fill the frame (1:47); the dial slides in beside the real tree (0:55); the same three product cards persist from 1:03 to 1:21 while tags orbit them. The rotating panel ring (2:04) is the one 3D-feeling move. Soft ease-out throughout.

## Pacing
Dense words (149 wpm) over slow picture: one 38 s stretch without a cut (0:18–0:56). Something moves every 2–3 s, but layouts change slowly. The repeated sentence shape gives rhythm but is predictable by the fourth pass.

## Sound
Upbeat corporate bed (~123 BPM) under steady conversational VO. 98 events/min points to a pop or tick on almost every element entrance. Loud mix (-15 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- Metaphor-then-proof: a quick illustrated question resolves into the real screen (0:51 → 0:57, 1:51 → 1:58).
- Persistent anchors: product cards stay while criteria tags fly in around them (1:03–1:15), so nothing resets between sentences.
- Diagonal slab wipe that carries colour into the next scene (1:40).
- Rotating ring of real panels around a subject as a feature round-up (2:04–2:16).
- A fake dialog with YES / NO as the problem turn (0:15).

## Avoid
- Grainy stock characters and potted plants: generic agency look.
- Almost no cursor or clicks: still doesn't feel like using the product.
- Six identical "imagine" setups; vary the sentence shape.
- Tilted italic tag pills everywhere read dated.
