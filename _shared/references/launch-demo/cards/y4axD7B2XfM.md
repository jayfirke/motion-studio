# Advanced Animation || Explainer Video for Crypto Exchanges || ETERNA

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y4axD7B2XfM · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:23
- **Type:** explainer (launch ad, 2.5D motion design) · **Product:** Eterna, a hybrid crypto exchange joining centralized and decentralized trading, margin trading and banking
- **Numbers:** 2.9 transitions per min (3 hard cuts; everything else morphs) · median shot 23.4 s (1.97–34.5 s) · 139 words/min · ~99 BPM · 132.5 sound events/min · -15.1 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:08 **Hook (market size):** one glowing coin spins edge-on in a dark blue void (0:01), then sits at the centre of a plexus network of dots and lines (0:05).
- 0:08–0:29 **Problem:** trader-type badges (investor, margin trader) circle a faceted sphere (0:08–0:12); they ride a rolling blue wave (0:15); the frame floods with overlapping app windows to show too many sign-ups (0:19); CEX and DEX coins lost in that maze (0:22); a tangle of toggles and sliders with coins sliding along them (0:26).
- 0:29–0:36 **Solution:** a giant coin rolls in with the brand mark on its face (0:29) and resolves into the logo on black (0:32).
- 0:34–0:57 **Features:** a dark rounded card drops onto blue and fills with the real exchange dashboard (cut 34.5 s, UI at 0:36); CEX/DEX merge in orbit rings (0:39); a candlestick chart sweeps past (0:43); a ring dial with Stocks, Forex and Crypto panels around it (0:50); bars and currency coins rise (0:53).
- 0:57–1:14 **Banking:** a hexagonal mask wipes in a money bag on a string (cut 57.6 s); avatar and bank icon on either side of a dial (1:00); a globe that a hand lifts (1:04–1:07); every icon from the film orbits one core (1:11).
- 1:14–1:23 **CTA:** a coin drops between two hands that close into a handshake (1:14–1:18); wordmark, URL and a blue Register button (1:21).

## Visual style
Dark navy to electric-blue gradients, with teal and red as status colours (green and red badges echo profit and loss). Icons are glossy 2.5D coins and badges with soft inner glow. Type is minimal: small labels on badges and panels, no headline slabs. Depth from bokeh, rim light on spheres and layered translucent windows.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- One real dashboard capture (0:36): a dark card lands first as an empty rounded rectangle, then the UI fades into it, framed as a floating card on blue. Shown once, briefly, no cursor.
- Everything else is metaphor: windows piling up for friction, toggles for complexity, dials for asset classes.
- Market panels (Stocks, Forex, Crypto) float as glass cards around a central dial, the closest thing to a feature map (0:50).

## Motion and transitions
Nearly one continuous camera: the coin is the carry object from 0:01 to 0:32 and returns at 0:46 and 1:14. Shape masks (a hexagon at 57.6 s) replace cuts. Camera pushes through spheres and rings; objects orbit and settle with smooth ease-out. Plexus networks, glow and constant spinning coins date it.

## Pacing
Medium-dense narration (139 words/min), one visual idea per sentence, about 3.5 s each. With almost no cuts, the energy comes from continuous orbiting and pushes, which keeps it flowing but makes every beat feel the same weight. Breath only at the logo (0:32) and the end card.

## Sound
Male narrator with a polished ad-read cadence; the script stacks pain points, then a one-line reveal, then features. Mid-tempo electronic bed around 99 BPM. About 133 events/min: whooshes on orbits, coin clinks, soft hits on reveals; mastered fairly loud (-15 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- An empty dark card lands first, then the real UI fills it (0:34–0:36): a clean way to introduce a screenshot.
- A carry object (the coin) that threads the whole film and returns at key beats (0:01, 0:29, 1:14).
- The "too many apps" pile-up of windows as a fast problem image (0:19).
- Hexagonal mask wipe as a chapter transition instead of a cut (0:57).

## Avoid
- Plexus networks, glows, spinning coins and bokeh (0:05, 0:46): dated crypto clichés.
- Spinning objects break the house rule against spins.
- Only one real UI moment and no cursor: not a demo.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film

3. A carry object: the film's playhead travels through every chapter and returns on the CTA.
