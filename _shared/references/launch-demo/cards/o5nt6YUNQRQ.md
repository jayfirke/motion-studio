## Visual style
A live-action actor against a teal-and-amber office, cut with clean white motion-graphics plates. A heavy condensed display face for punch words, used as a mask. A bold geometric sans for labels. Glossy 3D icons (clock, price tag, scales) sit on pastel blur blobs. The brand yellow appears only on the logo, and blue on the button.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
The UI appears as proof, not as a demo:
- real-looking SaaS dashboards float in 3D around the speaker and whip past camera (0:00–0:02);
- a video-stats card where a magnifier zooms in on the view count (0:27.8);
- comment cards pop in a stagger around his head (0:29.8).

There's no cursor or clicks. The UI is decoration that says "results".

## Motion and transitions
Fast and punchy:
- type-as-mask on the hook word (0:06.4);
- icon pills type their word letter by letter (0:17.5);
- white plates snap in for single ideas;
- a 2 s photo-toss burst (0:34–0:36);
- a blob wipe into the end card (0:42).

The actor's set changes at 0:31 and nobody notices because the cuts are so frequent.

## Pacing
Very dense: one change about every second (median shot 1.0 s, 44 transitions/min), and the voice at 147 words/min. Every spoken claim gets a matching visual on the same word. The only holds are the logo (0:03–0:06) and the end card (about 7 s).

## Sound
A light, bouncy bed (~89 BPM) under an upbeat male actor reading a casual script. The mix is very loud at −10.9 LUFS. The 78 onsets/min are lower than the visual density, so the SFX seem to be a few whooshes and pops rather than one per cut (inferred from the numbers, not heard).

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:02: real UI in the first frame. Dashboards orbit the subject, then whip past camera, so the product world is established before a word.
- 0:06.4–0:07.6: one giant word as a footage window, pulling back to reveal the phrase. Use it once.
- 0:22.3–0:26: a three-beat staccato of icon plus two words, about 1.2 s each, on the beat.
- 0:29.8: social proof as cards popping around the speaker in a stagger.
- 0:34–0:36: a 2 s burst of hits (about 20 cuts) as the energy peak before the close. Bursts are allowed under the house rules.

## Avoid
- The scripted actor-as-client testimonial reads fake.
- Comment cards and stats that look invented. Never fabricate proof.
- Glossy 3D clip-art icons on pastel blobs.
- The blob wipe.
- A −10.9 LUFS mix that is far too hot.
- Text plates on blank white for single words (centered-title feel).

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Hook with real artifacts in orbit:** the terminal sits centred while the real previs page, scorecard, waveform and render frames orbit in z, then whip past camera into the first typed prompt (under 2 s).
2. **Results staccato:** three beats at about 1.2 s each, each with one mark and one measured fact (−14 LUFS, 0 ms sync, 8+ on every score), each landing on a kick.
3. **Any-agent burst:** about 10 agent windows (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot…) tossed in 2 s, with two alternating real clicks stepping down in volume, then a hold on AGENTS.md.
