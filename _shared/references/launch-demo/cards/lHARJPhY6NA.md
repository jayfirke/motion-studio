# FICX No-Code Platform - Product Explainer Video Examples

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lHARJPhY6NA · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:37
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** FICX, a no-code platform for customer self-service journeys, screens and integrations
- **Numbers:** 2.5 transitions per min · median shot 17.4 s (longest 62.2 s) · 126 words/min · ~117.5 BPM · 186.6 sound events/min · -24.0 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:12):** a line-art city in teal and navy. A woman on her phone stands in the foreground while app cards pop up around her (0:02–0:10).
- **Solution (0:12–0:16):** the FICX wordmark in a white card (0:14).
- **What it does (0:16–0:30):** one laptop outline stays put while its screen swaps: a journey flowchart (0:18), five phone UIs (0:22), an integrations dashboard (0:26).
- **Speed (0:28–0:33):** on navy, Gantt bars slide and shorten (0:30).
- **Demo (0:33–0:52):** on teal, a person avatar links by a line to an empty phone (0:34). The phone fills with an "Open An Account" form and a hand cursor taps the button (0:38). A radio list follows (0:42), then a payment screen (0:46), then all five screens line up as a journey strip (0:50).
- **Integrations (0:52–1:00):** a "Success!" phone sends lines out to partner logos that pop in one by one (0:54–0:58).
- **Omnichannel (1:00–1:12):** a central avatar ringed by channel cards (Chat, Bot, Email, SMS, IVR…) (1:02). The ground flips to navy and each card becomes a phone screen (1:06).
- **Loyalty (1:12–1:30):** an agent linked by a dotted line to a phone (1:14). A single line draws and turns a corner (1:19) into smiley faces (1:23) and customer portraits (1:27).
- **CTA (1:30–1:37):** a navy disc with the F mark expands (1:30) into the logo and a "Book your demo today!" pill (1:35).

## Visual style
Flat 2D vector with thin outlines. Teal, navy, ice-blue and white. Line-art characters, a bold geometric wordmark, and placeholder grey bars for UI copy. Drifting dots and open arcs in the background. Almost no depth. The ground colour flips by section.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- The UI is redrawn: generic forms with only a few real labels.
- **Fixed device, swapping content** (laptop 0:18–0:26, phone 0:34–0:46): each new screen reads as progress in one place.
- One visible tap (0:38).
- **Connector lines** explain the relationships: person → phone, phone → logos.
- **Journey strip summary** (0:50).

## Motion and transitions
- **The line as the thread:** connectors draw on and lead the eye, and a free line turns a corner into the next beat (1:19).
- **Ground-colour flips** mark the sections. Only 4 cuts in 97 s.
- A disc wipe with the logo mark (1:30).
- Springy stagger-pops on cards and logos.

## Pacing
An even VO (126 wpm) with a state change every 3–4 s. The phone build is the best-synced stretch, one screen per VO phrase. The 12 s illustrated opener and the loyalty coda are filler.

## Sound
An upbeat bed at ~117 BPM with very dense onsets (187/min), probably a pop on every entrance. A single explainer VO. The SFX timbre isn't confirmed. The mix is very quiet at -24 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **Fixed device, swapping screens** (0:34–0:46), with real UI and a real click per screen.
- **Journey strip summary** (0:50): every step side by side after the walkthrough.
- **Connector lines that carry** (0:54, 1:19) between beats.
- **Same layout, new ground colour** (1:02 → 1:06) to mark a new idea.

## Avoid
- A line-art city and flat characters: dated explainer look.
- Placeholder UI with grey bars.
- Smiley and "Success!" metaphors.
- A single click in a whole demo film.
- A -24 LUFS mix.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Setup:** keep one terminal fixed in frame and advance its content step by step, each step on a real key tap.
2. **Hand-off:** a connector line draws from the approved previs → the engine → the render, carrying into the build chapter.
3. **Previs Studio tour wrap:** a strip of the tour's screens side by side (notes, options, approve).
