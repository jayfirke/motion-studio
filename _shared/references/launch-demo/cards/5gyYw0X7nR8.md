## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified browser windows on white, no device frame; only three screens in the film (1:10, 1:19, 1:24).
- Arrow cursor glides to a nav item or suggestion; no visible press, ripple or squash.
- UI text is unreadable at phone size; the screens prove the product exists rather than demo it.
