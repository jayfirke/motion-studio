- **Type:** launch (event promo with demo) · **Product:** HRS Crew & Passenger Solutions, a platform for stranded airline passengers, crew travel and the operations behind both

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:16):** a "goodbye to…" triplet in kinetic type on black.
  - 0:04: "LONG AIRPORT QUEUES" in heavy caps beside an inset of a real terminal.
  - 0:05–0:07: "CHAOS" with hundreds of glossy teal 3D spheres pouring behind it.
  - 0:11–0:20: a dotted chain of coloured icon nodes grows as the VO lists the fragmented pieces.
- **Problem close (0:16):** a dinosaur icon with "OF THE PAST".
- **Turn (0:21–0:34):** "A SIMPLER", the logo, a full cyan wipe (0:25), and "AUTOMATION" inside a giant cyan circle (0:28).
- **Passengers (0:30–0:52):** travellers with phones, then text left and an iPhone on a cyan disc right, one state per line (cancelled flight, hotel booked, receipt upload).

- **Operations (1:11–1:28):** the real dashboard on an office monitor, then full-frame. The cursor clicks a row and a JFK→BER route and tooltip appear on the map (1:20). "90% efficiency" sits above the KPIs (1:25).
- **Payoff and CTA (1:30–1:50):** "WORK HARDER?", a logo split, a cyan strikethrough through "manual" (1:39), "CUSTOMER" in the circle, then the logo.

## Visual style
Bold kinetic type in a strict black, white and cyan system. One heavy grotesk, all-caps black for punch words and medium sentence case for connectors. The accent colours one word per line. A large cyan circle recurs (behind phones, holding words). Stock footage adds human context. Flat backgrounds swap by hard colour wipes; no gradients.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Phones:** real app screens in clean, upright iPhone mockups on a cyan disc, on the right third, with text on the left.
- **Composited screens:** the UI on phones and monitors in footage (0:57, 1:11), so it lives in a real hand or office.
- **Desktop:** full-frame and flat. The arrow cursor's click (1:20) triggers a row highlight, a map route and a tooltip, with a stat headline above the UI (1:25).
- **States:** each phone beat shows one state, matched to the VO verb.

## Motion and transitions
- **Wipes and masks:** a white bar rising (0:17), a full cyan wipe (0:25), circular masks (0:53), a vertical bar push (0:51).
- **Hard-cut burst:** 7 detected changes in 1.5 s on "CHAOS" (0:05–0:07).
- **Type:** words snap in per word with a quick ease-out, and the strikethrough draws left to right.
- **Carry:** the cyan circle persists and changes role (disc behind a phone, frame for a word).

## Pacing
Dense: a new card every 2 s on median, VO at about 100 wpm in tagline phrases. UI holds run longer (4–15 s), giving the demo room after the fast type.

## Sound
- **Music:** confident corporate electronic at about 108 BPM, lifting at the turn.
- **Voice:** a polished ad read built on triads and repetition, closing on "why work harder when you can work smarter".
- **SFX:** whooshes on wipes, soft hits on punch words, a rattling cascade under the spheres.

## What to take from it
- **0:05–0:07:** one heavy, tactile 3D moment for the problem word, used once.
- **0:39–0:48:** text on the left, the real phone on the right on an accent disc, one UI state per VO verb, swapped in place.
- **1:16–1:20:** the cursor clicks a row, the map draws the route, a tooltip pops: cause and effect in a real desktop UI.
- **1:39:** a strikethrough drawn through "manual" as the voice says it.
- **Overall:** the strict black, white and one-accent system, with the accent on one word per line.

## Avoid
- **Stock footage** of travellers and office workers, and the off-tone dinosaur gag.
- **Colour wipes on almost every card,** so the wipe stops being special.
- **Single big words in a circle at the end,** close to a title on a plain field.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Problems:** a "goodbye to…" triplet in heavy type, each problem word with one physical visual (failed render thumbnails pouring like the spheres).
- **Previs Studio tour:** the page fixed on the right third, swapping one state per VO verb (options, notes, approve) while the text on the left changes.
- **Hand-off:** a strikethrough through "manual" in "no manual hand-off", timed to the voice.
