- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SpcGZLNYztc · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:54
- **Type:** ad / launch · **Product:** a no-code web editor for AR/VR product experiences, published to many channels and SDKs
- **Numbers:** 16.6 transitions per min · median shot 1.33 s (min 0.07 s, max 23.4 s) · 0 words/min (no caption track) · ~112 BPM · 268.4 sound events/min · -13.9 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **0:00–0:09 Problem (live action):** a frustrated developer, intercut about every 1.3 s with macro shots of a code editor and a blinking text cursor (cuts at 1.3, 2.6, 3.5, 4.8 and 6.5 s; a facepalm at 3.5).
  - Bold white caps land over the footage: "ARE YOU TIRED OF" (0:06), then "CODING CHALLENGES / TO CREATE XR?" with the second line in a blue bar (0:08).
- **0:09 Exit:** the headline shears diagonally and slides out in a skewed blue band, leaving a white frame.
- **0:10–0:15 Reveal:** "STEP INTO THE FUTURE OF" with blue speed streaks (0:10). Then a white card on brand blue shows a loader, springs in scale and holds the logo (0:12–0:15).
- **0:15–0:38 Demo (one continuous 23.4 s shot):**
  - The logo card becomes a menu list, and a big blue cursor arrives (0:17). It clicks "3D Models", which fills blue (0:19).
  - The editor opens with a purple chair. The cursor grabs and orbits it (0:21).
  - The camera pushes in; the cursor clicks a swatch and the chair turns navy (0:24–0:26).
  - The camera pulls back as connector lines branch from the editor to output channels, each with a chair thumbnail in context (0:28–0:30).
  - VR/AR tiles drop in (0:32). A "Supports All SDKs" pill rises into an SDK logo grid (0:35–0:37).
- **0:38–0:41 Integrations:** a skewed type wipe brings in "INTEGRATE WITH ALL INDUSTRY LEADERS", with partner logos around it.
- **0:41–0:54 Close:** kinetic type with streaks: "THE EFFORTLESS WAY" (0:44), "THE POTENTIAL" (0:46), then a huge blue "XR" (0:48). The logo with a blinking bar (0:51), then "TRY FOR FREE" and the URL (0:53).

## Visual style
- Brand blue on white and light grey. One heavy all-caps sans (about 8 % of frame height, the final "XR" about 25 %).
- Highlight bars sit behind the key lines. The live action is warm and filmic, the product world clean and flat.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Logo card becomes the UI (0:15–0:17):** the brand carries straight into the product.
- **Big custom cursor:** a solid blue arrow about 6 % of frame height. Each click fills its target blue (0:19).
- **Direct manipulation:** drag to orbit the model (0:21), click a swatch and the colour changes at once (0:26). Cause and effect seconds apart.
- **Push-in on the action:** the camera dollies into the swatch panel as it's used, then pulls back.
- **Branch diagram from real UI:** lines grow out of the editor to every output (0:28), so "publish anywhere" never leaves the screen.

## Motion and transitions
- The signature is the skewed type wipe: headlines shear on a diagonal and leave in a blue band (0:09, 0:38).
- A card scale-spring (0:12), and the card morphs into a menu (0:15).
- The demo is one camera that only pushes, pulls and pans.
- The outro builds word by word, ending on a huge "XR" punch.

## Pacing
- Strong contrast: the problem cuts every 1.3 s, the demo holds 23 s in one take, the outro hits a new word every 2 s.
- About a third of the runtime is product.
- No VO; the text and the cursor carry it.

## Sound
- A punchy ~112 BPM track. 268 events per minute, the densest in this batch: likely a whoosh per wipe, a click per press and a hit per word.
- -13.9 LUFS, on our target. No narrator.

## What to take from it
- **0:00–0:09 fast problem cuts with macro code inserts:** pain in 9 s.
- **0:15–0:17 logo card becomes the first panel:** real carry from brand to product.
- **0:19–0:26 click, instant change, push-in on the click:** the clearest cause-and-effect demo in this batch.
- **0:28 connector lines from the editor to every output.**
- **0:09 / 0:38 skewed headline wipe:** an energetic exit.

## Avoid
- Speed-streak particles (particle ban; templated).
- Facepalm acting.
- A soup of partner and SDK logos.
- Overusing a highlight bar on caps.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Problems:** 1–1.5 s cuts between the owner's hands or face and macro shots of old render frames and terminal errors, with caps headlines arriving, then a skewed wipe to white.
2. **Previs Studio tour:** one continuous 20 s shot. Click an option (it fills with the accent), drag the playhead, click a music swatch and watch the waveform change, the camera pushing in on each click.
3. **Results:** lines branch from the approved spec to the 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 renders, each landing on a click.
