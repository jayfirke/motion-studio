## Visual style
Stock footage carries most shots; real UI sits on white. Two brand colours: Munch red and a lavender-violet gradient for brand moments. Social chrome (LIVE badge, comments, heart and emoji reactions) is rebuilt over footage. Type is rare: heavy rounded logo, one heavy white line at the end.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real web UI, flat and full screen, no device frame. An arrow cursor appears on the project grid (0:22) and the logo tile (0:11).
- Features shown as results: the tracking box is smart crop (1:02); the 9:16 frame lifting out of a blurred source is reframing (0:52).
- Source videos live in white "player cards" with like counters; a card shrinks and splits to show a clip being cut (0:37–0:40).
- The upload → analyse → export chain is never clicked through; the UI gets about four short glimpses.

## Motion and transitions
Hard cuts, one white flash (0:10), dissolves into the UI (0:19–0:22) and card moves on white. The best carry is the reframe: footage scales into a vertical card while its own blurred copy stays behind. The platform burst is a hard-cut stutter.

## Pacing
Dense: 152 words/min of back-and-forth dialogue. Median shot 1.7 s; the longest hold (11 s) is the opening skit. Breathes only at the logo build (1:36) and the end tag (1:50).

## Sound
Upbeat pop-electronic bed (~112 BPM) under two voices: a doubtful creator and a confident narrator who answers each worry in a word or two. 65 events/min fits pops on the icon spill and whooshes on card moves (not checked by ear). Quiet mix, voice on top.

## What to take from it
- Bookend skit: a dull "before" intro in a player (0:00), redone through a REC viewfinder at the end (1:41).
- Objection-and-answer script: each worry gets a one-word reply and a cut, ending on "yes yes yes and yes" (1:31–1:36).
- Reframe as a visible operation: footage shrinks into a 9:16 card over its blurred source (0:52); tracking box in the wide shot (1:02).
- Cursor clicks the logo tile to open the product chapter (0:11).
- Burst then rest: 14 cuts in 1.6 s, then a calm logo build (1:34–1:41).

## Avoid
- White flash (0:10) and dissolves into UI (0:19–0:22): crossfades are banned.
- 3D clay icon spill (0:12): close to a confetti burst.
- Stock reactions (0:45) and emoji overlays (1:31): filler.
- UI glimpses too short to read; never a real walk-through, the same gap the owner saw in our draft.
