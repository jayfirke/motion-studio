## Visual style
Collage: real cut-out photos (copier, chess knight, smiling people) with hand-drawn yellow outlines and marker doodles, on an off-white textured paper ground. Palette is three colours only: violet, sunflower yellow, navy text. Rounded geometric sans for the wordmark. Shapes are chunky and flat, with grain.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- One redrawn UI screen, the goal tree, inside a clean window with a sidebar (0:18–0:19).
- The window grows out of a dot, then the tree expands branch by branch, so the structure of the product is the animation.
- No cursor, no clicks, no typing; the product is a single idea (plans cascade down) shown once.

## Motion and transitions
Everything pops: elements scale in with squash and a little overshoot, animated on twos or threes, which gives the stop-motion jitter the detector reads as constant cuts. Carries are strong: the copier becomes a circle, the circle becomes the whiteboard; a dot becomes the UI window; three shapes squash into the logo. The camera pans along the maze as the knight moves. The road rush at 0:25 is the only perspective move.

## Pacing
Very dense: 144 words/min in 37 s, a new idea every 1.5–2 s, almost no rest until the end card. The final 7 s hold on the end card is long against the speed of the rest.

## Sound
Narration is fast and plain, one short sentence per visual; male or female not checked by ear. Light acoustic-pop bed (~103 BPM), master at -19.5 LUFS. 129 sound events/min fits pops on each element. Not verified by ear.

## What to take from it
- 0:13: the logo built from three simple shapes that squash together, then a quick tilt as the wordmark joins.
- 0:16–0:19: a single dot grows into the app window, then the core feature (a tree) unfolds branch by branch.
- 0:07–0:10: a maze that draws on as the subject moves through it; the problem drawn as a path, not a sad face.
- 0:00–0:04: every object becomes the next object; no hard cuts in the hook.

## Avoid
- Stepped, jittery on-twos motion and bouncy pops: playful, but against the house rule of no bouncy easing on UI.
- Cut-out stock people and a chess knight as metaphors.
- A 7 s static end card.
- Almost no real product interaction.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- Setup chapter: one accent dot grows into the terminal window, then the project folder tree unfolds branch by branch as the VO names each file.
- Hook: the problem as a path that draws on (prompt → render → redo → render) and loops back on itself, then straightens when Claude Code appears.
- CTA: build the mark from simple parts that close together on the impact hit, with no bounce.
