# Best Explainer Videos 2025 (Examples for Startups) / Product Demo Videos

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BLC3H8Fgt8I · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 15:32
- **Type:** agency talk (a countdown compilation of about a dozen SaaS/AI explainers by the agency, Microsoft, Flash Motion, Flix Studios and others) · **Product:** the agency; each clip sells a different SaaS/AI product
- **Numbers:** 18.4 transitions per min (0 to 76/min by section) · median shot 1.21 s (max 72.9 s) · 102.7 words/min overall (voiced films are much denser, sung spots near zero) · ~136 BPM · 47.1 sound events/min · -18.8 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:28 Teaser: 30 transitions in 28 s, one-second slices of every entry (a keyhole, "11+"/"25+" counters, 3D tiles, a send-arrow button, blur-to-sharp type, a tunnel, a typewriter caret, a dotted globe). The host frames the countdown.
- 0:28–2:37 Gamer network (green): glowing nodes and controller icons orbit a logo. Shots of 24–38 s with no detected cut, one continuous camera.
- 2:37–3:57 Atlassian-app vendor (blue): a 3D app icon, then dashboard panels orbiting an orb, in one 73 s take under stat-heavy VO.
- 4:00–5:00 Microsoft-style 3D spot: iridescent shapes cut hard on a sung track (43 cuts in 60 s).
- 5:00–6:00 Creative marketplace (black, neon green): a brief types into a chat and a hand cursor hits Send (5:30).
- 6:00–8:10 Hotel housekeeping SaaS: live action (6:09), then status cards (Room 1001 Updated at 6:48; a green check on 1003 Inspection Completed at 7:27). Ends on a purple Book a Demo pill (8:06).
- 8:10–12:40 Quicker entries: kinetic type, a numbered "07" tag, a hand cursor tapping an AI insights bar under a benchmark row (10:41), a spreadsheet (11:20), a video editor (11:59). 11:00–11:30 has 38 transitions.
- 12:40–15:32 Brand films: a big serif "Elevate", avatar bubbles with a 90 % ring, an illustrated sports spot ending in a 27-cut burst.

## Visual style
Each film has one tight palette (green on teal, electric blue, black and magenta, white and lilac). One geometric sans, set large, with one coloured accent word. Soft depth everywhere: glass cards with background blur, iridescent 3D, vignetted dark grounds.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI: cards with 2–3 lines rather than full screens; a full screen only when it makes the point (spreadsheet, editor).
- A hand-pointer cursor arrives, presses, and the target reacts (5:30, 9:23, 10:41).
- Status changes happen one card at a time, with copy stepping from title to subtitle to a coloured status word.
- Window clusters orbit a hero object in 3D (2:54) instead of sitting in a device frame.

## Motion and transitions
The strongest entries barely cut: the gamer and Atlassian films run 25–73 s without a detectable transition while the camera pushes, nodes grow and panels morph into the next idea. Music-video entries do the opposite with 0.2–0.5 s bursts. Type enters by masks, blur-to-sharp (0:12.6) and a typewriter caret.

## Pacing
Voiced entries talk with no gaps, and a new visual idea arrives every 2–4 s even inside a continuous take. The housekeeping film gives each sentence its own feature card. The rests come from short music-only stretches, never from dead frames.

## Sound
- Upbeat electronic or pop beds at about 136 BPM. Two entries are sung tracks.
- Confident, conversational VOs (male and female) in the order hook → backstory → mechanism → numbers → invitation.
- SFX at 47/min: ticks on card pops, whooshes on pushes, a click under each cursor press.

## What to take from it
- 0:28–2:37: one continuous camera through a node graph with no cuts for 25–38 s. Carry done right.
- 7:27: one card flips to a green check with a coloured status word, a compact "done" beat.
- 10:41: a hand cursor taps a gradient bar and the result appears right beside it, so the cause is visible.
- 0:12.6: a blurred word resolves to sharp type instead of fading in.
- 0:00–0:28: a beat-cut teaser burst before the explanation, as a hook pattern.

## Avoid
- The glowing neon chat-window border (5:30) and orb halos (glow on UI is banned).
- Single centered words on gradients ("Smarter", "Unlock", "Elevate").
- Corner source tags and sung pop beds.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Hook with a 2–3 s beat-cut burst (previs page, critique scores, render strip, Claude Code prompt), then settle into one continuous camera for setup.
2. Build and critique: score cards flip to a green 8+ one at a time, like the 7:27 status cards.
3. Hand-off: a hand cursor presses Approve on the previs page and the spec card slides out of it.
