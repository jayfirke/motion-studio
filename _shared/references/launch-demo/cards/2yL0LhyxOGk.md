# Product Launch Video for Membrain's Elevate (Sales Coaching)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2yL0LhyxOGk · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:10
- **Type:** launch · **Product:** Membrain Elevate, a sales-coaching module that shows rep performance, skill gaps and coaching plans
- **Numbers:** 4.3 transitions per min · median shot 6.3 s (shortest 1.5 s, longest 48.5 s) · 129 words/min · ~112 BPM · 127.6 sound events/min · −15.0 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:09 Hook, stat-led: an "81%" ring draws in a dark tile, which slides left as the claim types on. Then a B&W frustrated-manager photo with an inner-voice line.
- 0:10 A teal-magenta blur floods the frame (the reset).
- 0:13–0:24 Problem stats:
  - "67%" with avatars orbiting it;

- 1:03 Benefit line among avatars and rings, with "your People" in magenta.

## Visual style
- Dark premium SaaS: a charcoal field with soft magenta and teal glows from the corners.
- The signature element is a ring gauge with a magenta-to-teal gradient stroke, at every stat and in the final collage.
- One clean sans in light and regular weights. Accent words change colour, never size. UI is restyled dark to match; light cards appear only in the action burst.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Restyled UI on **steep 2.5D planes** (about 30–45°) with the camera sliding along them. The headline sits on the same plane, so type and UI move as one object (0:31, 0:37).
- A white arrow cursor with a dark outline clicks a ring (0:48), types into a field (0:54) and ticks a task (1:00).
- Popovers land on a tilted parent screen, so context stays visible.
- Stats animate as data (rings draw, a chart climbs, avatars get checks), never as plain typed numbers.

## Motion and transitions
- One continuous camera for 48.5 s: tiles slide aside for text, glows wipe the frame, planes glide past each other.
- Then deliberate acceleration: four hard cuts in 10 s on the action beats.
- Letter-scramble and type-on text suits the data theme. Smooth ease-outs, no bounce.

## Pacing
- Dense: 129 words/min, with four stats in 24 s.
- About 3 s per stat, 5–6 s per tilted feature, then 1.5–2.5 s per action. The wordmark (0:25–0:30) is the one breath.

## Sound
- A bed at about 112 BPM. −15.0 LUFS, right on target.
- A brisk narrator: a stat-question opener, then imperative fragments such as "no more guesswork".
- SFX can't be separated in the data; likely light ticks on gauges and clicks.

## What to take from it
- **Headlines laid in perspective on the UI's own plane** (0:31, 0:37). More premium than overlay captions.
- **Planned acceleration:** a long continuous first half, then 1.5–2.5 s cuts on the action beats (48.5–58.4 s). The energy curve our film needs.
- **One signature data element reused** (the gradient ring at 0:00, 0:13, 1:03).
- **A tagline that builds a word at a time across two holds** (0:25 → 0:28), doubling as the brand hold.

## Avoid
- Corner glow blobs pushed too far, which drift toward the banned glow look.
- The stock face-palm photo (0:07).
- Tilt so steep that the UI can't be read (1:00 Tasks). Fails 360 px.
