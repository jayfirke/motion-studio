# Best SaaS Explainer Videos: Hotel Management App / Product Teaser Video (Motion Graphics Animation)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U3IfeAqciL0 · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 2:13
- **Type:** explainer (product teaser with UI demo) · **Product:** FCS1, cloud hotel-operations software (jobs, cleaning, maintenance, incidents)
- **Numbers:** 14.5 transitions per min · median shot 2.71 s (max 28.2) · 120 words/min · ~99 BPM · 137 sound events/min · -22.7 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:09):** a live-action triptych of hotel staff. A rhetorical question lands on the caption "Guests..." over a blurred face that racks into focus (0:05).
- **Logo (0:09–0:13):** a white disc with a dot, then a ring opens like an iris onto the app icon.
- **Solution (0:13–0:30):** a thin "Job Order" bar grows into the desktop table. A Create Job modal tilts in 3D (0:19), then the phone "My Jobs" list (0:25).
- **Scale (0:29–0:36):** an aerial triptych of hotels, then "We've got you" (0:36).
- **Modules (0:41–1:31):** the logo with four orbiting 3D icons, then one sentence and one UI moment per module:
  - Jobs: phone form with a manager pointer (0:47) and a guest QR request on a phone over a real room (0:52).
  - Cleaning: tasksheet to phone with a green toast (1:04).
  - Maintenance: tilted calendar (1:20).
  - Incidents: orange "New Incident" toasts, then a desktop table (1:26–1:31).
- **Extras (1:37–2:05):** feature pills, a flag globe ("20+ Languages", 1:42), a 3D cloud, an API logo ring (1:53) and staff portraits.
- **CTA (2:10):** icon, URL, "Book a Demo" pill.

## Visual style
UI-centric 2.5D on a deep violet-black radial ground with purple bokeh. There is one violet accent plus small green and orange status colours. A rounded sans splits headline words by colour ("We've" violet, "got you" white). Glossy clay 3D icons float at depth. Live footage appears only in three-panel splits.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Redrawn UI**, clean and simplified, in frameless floating windows and phone screens.
- **The pointer is a person:** a round avatar chip labelled "Manager" or "Staff" glides to a button, and the button presses (0:19, 1:15).
- **Extreme macro:** at 1:13–1:15 the camera is so close on a tilted form that the fields read as fat white stripes. A "Coffee maker" tag chip drops in and a checkbox ticks.
- **Toasts as proof:** coloured notification pills pop beside the phone (1:04, 1:26).
- **Depth of field:** blurred UI sits behind one sharp card (1:59).

## Motion and transitions
- **Strong carry:**
  - a title bar expands into a whole window (0:12.9–0:13.5);
  - a task card lifts out of the desktop tasksheet and becomes the phone list (1:00–1:03);
  - a violet disc wipe opens a section (0:56);
  - the module icons recur as a hub (0:41, 1:09).
- Slow dolly-ins and 3D tilts land with soft eases. Short flashes come in clusters (0:05, 1:13), with long internal-motion holds between them (max 28 s).

## Pacing
Moderate at 120 words/min, with one feature per sentence. Screens hold 3–6 s while elements move inside them, so it feels busy without hard cuts. It breathes at "We've got you", on the aerials and at the CTA.

## Sound
- A ~99 BPM upbeat corporate electronic bed.
- Very dense SFX (137/min): pops on every entrance and taps under avatar clicks.
- A quiet master (-22.7 LUFS).
- A conversational, question-led script; the voice gender wasn't checked.

## What to take from it
- **0:12.9–0:13.5:** a single title bar grows into the full app window, an elegant carry from nothing into UI.
- **1:00–1:03:** a card is pulled from the desktop view and lands as the mobile view. One action, two devices, no cut.
- **0:19 and 1:15:** a pointer with a role label clicks inside a tilted form, so both the cause and the actor are visible.
- **1:13:** a macro on a form field as a tag chip drops in. The scale change makes a dull form exciting.
- **0:05:** a punchline caption on blurred footage that racks into focus.

## Avoid
- Clay 3D icons orbiting a logo (0:41), a flag globe and a partner-logo ring. Generic agency stock.
- A violet bokeh glow on every shot.
- SFX on every entrance tips toward "game" sound. The master is too quiet.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Previs Studio tour:** start on the page's title bar alone and expand it into the full Previs window as Sarah names it.
- **Hand-off:** a pointer chip labelled "the owner" clicks Approve. The approved card lifts out of the page and lands in the terminal or engine panel.
- **Build and critique:** a macro on one score field ticking to 8, then a pull back to the whole scorecard.
