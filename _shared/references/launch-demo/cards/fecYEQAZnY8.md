- **One place (0:21–0:23):** "Manage Sales & Support from one place".
- **Close (0:24–0:31):** two chat bubbles bump together into the logo mark. In the tagline "Your unfair Support advantage", "Support" swaps to "Business". A "Try it now!" pill.

## Visual style
Minimal and light: a pale cool-grey ground, navy type, and one mint brand accent. A medium geometric sans, left- or right-aligned (never centred), with a thin mint underline. Hand-drawn doodles and a few dots. The dashboard is a crisp white card with a slate sidebar and soft shadow.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **The real dashboard**, cropped to the meaningful tiles.
- **No cursor, no clicks:** the UI proves itself with live data. The donut sweeps, lines draw, and counters tick.
- **3D swing-in** that settles flat, one strong move per screen.
- **A push-in** on a single stat tile.
- **A card flip:** the analytics card turns over to reveal the next feature on its back.

## Motion and transitions
- Only 2 cuts in 31 s. The rest is type-on, backspace, a push, a flip and one whip-pan.
- The typing caret is the connective tissue: text types, deletes, and the logo types in.
- Crisp moves with soft landings; the whip-pan is the only fast move, on the beat.

## Pacing
Light and rhythmic: headlines hold about 2 s and UI moments 3–5 s. With no narrator, the music and type cadence set the timing; the counters give constant small change.

## Sound
- A ~99 BPM dance-pop track with a vocal hook.
- Moderate SFX (78/min): typing clicks under type-ons, swishes on the swing, flip and whip, ticks under counters.
- -13.9 LUFS, right at our target.
- No VO.

## What to take from it
- **0:04–0:07:** a headline types, backspaces, and the product name types in its place with a blinking caret. A terminal-native reveal.
- **0:16.7:** the dashboard card flips to show the next feature on its back. One object carries two beats.
- **0:19–0:20:** a big live counter ticking up as proof the product is live.
- **0:13–0:16:** a push into one stat tile while its number counts.
- **0:26–0:28:** a one-word swap inside the tagline.

## Avoid
- Hand-drawn squiggles and scattered dots.
- A song with lyrics; it would fight Sarah.
- No visible cause (no cursor or click). Fine for 30 s, too thin for a demo film.
- A cute two-bubble logo bump.
