# affable.ai "Skye" launch film (made in 7 days)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mS9MbNq4FLg · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:23
- **Type:** launch · **Product:** Skye, an AI assistant inside affable.ai for finding influencers, writing outreach and reviewing content
- **Numbers:** 13.0 transitions per min · median shot 3.8 s · 6 words/min (no voice) · ~152 BPM · 248 sound events/min · -13.6 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:06 Hook: "INTRODUCING" in bold white, a giant outlined echo of the same word drifting behind it (0:02); the gradient logo mark pops in (0:05).
- 0:08–0:17 First demo: a browser window grows from a thin bar; camera pushes onto the prompt field, cursor clicks, a plain-language query types in (0:14), cursor hits send, and a grid of influencer cards scrolls up (0:16–0:17).
- 0:19–0:40 Feature 1, recommendations: scrambled-letter title "BETTER RECOMMENDATIONS" (0:29); window reopens, cursor clicks "Upload Moodboard" (0:27), thumbnails slide under the field (0:31), results update and a chat side panel appears (0:36).
- 0:40–0:46 Feature 2: "AUTOMATED WRITER" over a huge outlined "AUTO" (0:40); outreach inbox with a drafted message bubble (0:43).
- 0:47–0:52 Feature 3: "CONTENT REVIEW" title, then a blurred grid behind a sharp approval panel showing a creator's post (0:50).
- 0:54–1:04 Feature 4: "MANAGE BRAND REPUTATION", then "RISK MONITORING" stacked five times with the middle copy lit in gradient (0:57); "Skye is analysing" bar over blurred posts with one card in focus (1:00).
- 1:04–1:23 Close: letters type out "MAKE EVERY CAMPAIGN A BREEZE WITH SKYE" and the line slides left as it grows (1:11–1:14); two-line "Experience the future of influencer marketing" (1:18); logo end card (1:21).

## Visual style
Near-black stage with slow blurred orbs (teal, magenta, amber) and tiny specks. Light lavender UI windows pop off the dark. Titles in wide, tracked, all-caps sans with an orange-pink-blue gradient; huge outline type behind them gives depth without boxes.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product UI in a minimal browser frame (three dots, no chrome), centered with a thin coloured edge shadow.
- Big black arrow cursor, scaled up for legibility; it moves, presses and the UI reacts (send arrow at 0:14, "Upload Moodboard" at 0:27).
- Typing: the query appears letter by letter in the prompt field (0:14).
- Push-ins: camera zooms from the full window to the prompt field and back out to the results grid.
- Focus by blur: the grid is blurred and one panel or card stays sharp (0:50, 1:00).
- Windows open from a horizontal sliver that expands vertically (0:22).

## Motion and transitions
Every UI section is bracketed by a black title card; titles enter with letter-scramble and per-letter type-on, leave by sliding off or dropping below frame (1:04). Windows scale in from small and grow; result grids scroll fast with motion blur (0:16). The orbs and specks keep drifting under everything, which carries the eye across cuts. Snappy ease-out on UI, slightly springy scale on windows.

## Pacing
Fast and rhythmic, cut to a 152 BPM track: a title roughly every 8–10 s, each followed by 4–8 s of UI. No voice, so the only reading load is the 2–3-word titles. The end runs long (about 19 s of type and logo) and loses energy.

## Sound
Music only: a fast bed at about 152 BPM, mastered loud (-13.6 LUFS). The captions hold only music and applause tags, so there is no narration. 248 sound events per minute is mostly the track's percussion; the title cards are timed as if each lands on a hit (genre and SFX not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- 0:12–0:17: push onto the prompt field, cursor clicks, the query types in, send, then the result grid scrolls in; one clean cause-and-effect demo loop.
- 0:22: a window that opens from a sliver; fresh way to start a screen without fading.
- 0:50 and 1:00: blur the whole UI except one panel to say "look here", no boxes or arrows.
- 0:40: giant outlined word behind the title for depth with one colour.
- Big readable cursor (about 3 % of frame height).

## Avoid
- No voice at all: the owner wants dense narration; this one tells nothing beyond four labels.
- Scrambled letter effects and echo-stacked "RISK MONITORING" (0:57): glitchy, close to banned tricks.
- 19-second type-heavy ending with no product.
