## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:02):** over-the-shoulder on a monitor running old grey accounting software, with a loading spinner and a stalled progress bar composited in. The VO opens on a precise pain: "3 minutes to generate a balance sheet report and then it crashes."
- **Problem scale (0:02–0:10):** a man at a laptop (0:02). The footage shrinks into a rounded card on mint (0:03), and more cards of frustrated workers drift in as a collage (0:05). A faint clock face rises behind them with sweeping red hands (0:07–0:09).
- **Turn (0:10–0:15):** an overhead desk card moves to the clock centre (0:10), then grows to full frame (0:13.9). In the footage the man switches on a desk lamp, the "Introducing" beat. The camera pulls back to an office monitor (0:14).
- **Product (0:15–0:23):** the logo builds on the monitor (0:15–0:16), and the dashboard appears on the same screen. The cursor clicks the top-right button (0:17), and a "Customize Your Dashboard" modal shows an area chart (0:19–0:20). The finished dashboard fills with charts (0:21–0:22).
- **Wipe + CTA (0:23–0:29):** big blue chevrons from the logo slash diagonally across the frame (0:22.7–0:23.1), leaving the mark (0:24) and then the wordmark with its tagline (0:25–0:29).

## Visual style
Live action plus UI: stock office footage in rounded cards on mint or cream, and the product UI composited onto real monitors. Brand blue, with red used only for the clock hands (the pain). A clean geometric wordmark. Cards at several scales with light parallax. The VO carries the words; there's almost no on-screen text.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Screen replacement on a real monitor** for both the before (0:00) and the after (0:15). The same framing makes the contrast instant.
- The **pain is shown as UI:** a spinner and a stalled bar.
- **Click → modal → chart** (0:17–0:20): the chart is already there, which proves "instant".
- The UI fills the monitor rather than floating, so it reads as real.

## Motion and transitions
Footage → card → collage (0:02–0:05). One collage card grows into the next scene (0:13.9). A slow clock under faster card moves. A brand-chevron wipe into the end card. Only 6 cuts, but the motion never stops. Smooth, no bounce.

## Pacing
Tight: pain in 2 s, problem 8 s, turn 5 s, product 8 s, logo 6 s. A sparse VO (54 words) leaves the picture to do most of the work. The roughly 5 s logo hold is long for a 29 s film.

## Sound
A driving ~129 BPM bed with dense onsets (178/min), likely clock ticks and card hits. A calm VO in short sentences. -15 LUFS. The SFX timbre isn't confirmed.

## What to take from it
- **A measurable pain as the first line**, shown on a real screen (0:00).
- **Before / after in identical framing** (0:00 vs 0:15).
- **A collage card that grows into the next scene** (0:10–0:14).
- **One click that pays off instantly** (0:17–0:20).
- **Brand-mark wipe** as the single transition into the end card (0:22.7).

## Avoid
- Stock footage of frustrated office workers.
- The clock-face metaphor.
- A 5 s logo hold in a 29 s film.
- About 2 s per UI state: too short for our demo chapters.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Hook:** open on our own measurable pain, the owner's real draft on screen with a number in the VO (the time it took, or the renders thrown away).
2. **Results:** before / after in the same framing, with the old showreel shot swapped in place for the new one and no layout change.
3. **Hand-off:** the approved previs panel grows to full frame and becomes the engine's first scene, as at 0:13.9.
