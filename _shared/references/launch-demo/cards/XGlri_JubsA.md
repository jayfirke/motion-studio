# Kitaabh: a SaaS product demo in 40 seconds (agency sample)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XGlri_JubsA · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:39
- **Type:** demo (short UI-led product spot) · **Product:** Kitaabh, accounting software with a customisable reporting dashboard
- **Numbers:** 3.1 transitions per min · median shot 19.3 s (max 34.4) · 94.8 words/min · ~123 BPM · 143.0 sound events/min · −20.9 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:04):** stock footage of hands typing on a laptop with a generic dashboard. Blue pill questions pop around the screen one by one ("Customisable reports?", "Dynamic dashboard?").
- **Logo (0:04–0:08):** at 0:04.2 the footage shrinks into a rounded card on blue. The logo mark builds from stacked chevrons (0:05), then the wordmark (0:07).
- **Dashboard (0:09):** the real dashboard on a blue field: stat tiles in ₹, a bar chart and a line chart, with the cursor near the date filter.
- **Customise (0:10–0:26):** one "Customise Your Dashboard" modal: categories on the left, live preview in the middle, a Visualisations list on the right. The cursor clicks a new chart type about every 1.6 s and the preview re-animates each time:
  - Expenses: Table (0:10), Column with bars growing (0:12–0:14), Pie (0:15–0:17).
  - Sales: Table (0:18), Doughnut (0:20).
  - Payment: Table (0:22), Area chart (0:23), with a hover tooltip on the April peak (0:25).
- **Build (0:26–0:30):** the area chart lifts out of the modal while the dashboard behind it blurs. The cursor drags it into place (0:28), and the finished four-widget dashboard holds (0:30).
- **Close (0:31–0:39):** the logo mark alone, then the wordmark and a URL pill held about 5 s.

## Visual style
- A single cobalt blue as both background and accent, with white UI panels.
- The real product UI with its own fonts and colours, no restyling.
- One small agency badge in the top-left corner throughout.
- Minimal: no characters, no icons beyond the logo.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Real UI, one window, one cursor:** every change has a visible cause: the cursor clicks a list item, then the chart morphs.
- Charts animate in on each switch (bars rise, pie wedges sweep), so each click pays off in under a second.
- A hover tooltip shows the data is live (0:25).
- **Lift-out and drag (0:26–0:28):** the widget pops forward over a blurred background, then is dragged into the dashboard.
- The camera drifts in slightly toward the active area; there are no device frames after the opening shot.

## Motion and transitions
- One cut in 39 s. The footage-to-card shrink (0:04) is the only scene change. Everything after happens inside the UI, carried by the cursor and chart morphs.
- Fast ease-out chart builds, smooth slow camera drift.

## Pacing
- A UI state change every ~1.6 s for 16 s straight.
- The voice is sparse (95 wpm) and asks question-and-answer lines while the cursor does the talking.
- The only breath is the 5 s logo hold at the end, which is a little long.

## Sound
- Upbeat ~123 BPM bed with 143 events/min, likely a click on each selection and soft swooshes on chart builds.
- One calm narrator with short lines.
- Mastered quiet at −20.9 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **Click-to-morph rhythm (0:10–0:26):** the cursor clicks, the chart re-animates, every 1.6 s. This is the clearest "looks like a demo" grammar in the batch.
- **Lift-out over a blurred page, then drag into place (0:26–0:30):** this makes the end state feel built by the user.
- **Hover tooltip as proof the data is live (0:25).**
- **Footage shrinking into a card (0:04):** a carry from a human context into the product frame without a cut.

## Avoid
- Stock "hands on laptop" footage with a generic screen. It is weak next to the real UI.
- A 5 s static end-card hold.
- The −20.9 LUFS master.
- The persistent agency badge would count as a corner label under our rules.
