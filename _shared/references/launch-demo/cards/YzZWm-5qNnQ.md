# Brand Video for Enterprise AI Solutions: BlueVerse (LTIMindtree)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzZWm-5qNnQ · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:28
- **Type:** brand film · **Product:** an "agentic AI ecosystem" brand from an IT-services company. No product is shown.
- **Numbers:** 20.5 transitions per min · median shot 1.31 s (min 0.08 s, max 16.8 s) · 97.8 words/min · ~117.5 BPM · 91.0 sound events/min · -22.0 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **0:00–0:20 Hook (the world is changing):**
  - A mirrored skyline at dusk (0:02), night-earth city lights (0:05), swirling blue light currents (0:09).
  - A fisheye city curling round a sun (0:13).
  - A silhouette running down a white tunnel toward light (0:16), landing on the "left behind" line.
- **0:20–0:24 Problem:** a woman and a robot dog in a glass atrium; the caption says pilots are not enough.
- **0:24–0:40 Promise:** a woman walks through pastel portal arches as the company name appears (0:24). Glass molecules (0:27), a ribbon sculpture (0:31), a squishy cube (0:35), then a wall of 3D bulbs (0:38).
- **0:42–0:47 Reveal:** a figure on a mountain under the Milky Way, then the "BlueVerse" wordmark on a nebula (0:46).
- **0:49–1:20 Benefits as anaphora:** three "Out-" lines (outrun, outdo, outgrow), each on its own cosmic plate.
  - A comet (0:49), a hyperspace warp (0:51–0:55), constellations (0:57).
  - A ringed planet (1:00), dust (1:04), a fluid swirl (1:08).
  - A river of light dots (1:11), a red nebula (1:15), a woman in projected light (1:19).
- **1:22–1:28 Close:** the logo lock-up over stars, then a black end card with the URL.

## Visual style
- A CG and stock mood-board: hyper-saturated glass, plastic and nebula renders, mixed with live-action people.
- No single palette. Each shot has its own colour world, held together only by dark exposure and the white captions.
- One spaced, all-caps sans. Every caption is a two-line stack, with the setup in light weight and the key phrase in bold, placed in shifting positions.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
None: no UI, no cursor, no screen. The product exists only as a name and metaphors, the opposite of what the owner asked for.

## Motion and transitions
From the cuts sheet:
- A light-line glitch frame (0:21) and a smeared chromatic pass (0:23).
- A zoom-through from molecule to molecule: five changes in 3 s (0:27–0:30).
- A hyperspace warp with a lens flare (0:51–0:55).
- Captions blur in and track slightly tighter.
- Camera moves are slow pushes and orbits; the energy comes from the plate changes.

## Pacing
- 98 wpm of poetic, fragmentary copy, with a caption on nearly every phrase.
- About 1.3 s shots in the middle act, with a 16.8 s stretch in the cosmic section.
- It breathes at the reveal (0:46, about 3 s with no caption change).

## Sound
- ~117 BPM cinematic electronic that builds into the reveal. Only 91 events per minute, the lowest here: few SFX, the music carries.
- A manifesto-style script that lands on the brand name twice. The frames can't confirm the voice's gender.
- -22 LUFS. The music tail runs over the logo.

## What to take from it
- **0:05–0:16 light/bold caption stacks:** each VO phrase appears as two lines, the stressed words bold. Good on-screen grammar for a dense VO, if set bigger.
- **0:49–1:15 three-line anaphora:** three benefits with the same opening word, each on its own plate, give the cut a rhythm to follow.
- **0:27–0:30 zoom-through chain:** push through one object into the next, five times in 3 s. A good burst of hits.
- **0:46 held reveal:** after 1 s shots, the name holds about 3 s. The contrast makes it land.

## Avoid
- No product: it fails the "looks like a demo" bar completely.
- Nebulae, hyperspace, lens flares, the chromatic smear and the glitch frame (all banned).
- Captions at about 2 % of frame height in thin spaced caps, unreadable at 360 px.
- Unrelated stock-model shots, and a -22 LUFS master.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **"Any agent" chapter:** a three-line anaphora with one plate per line, each showing a real agent window (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor), the key word bold in a light/bold stack at least 5 % of frame height.
2. **Results:** a zoom-through chain into five finished renders (about 0.6 s each), then a 3 s hold on the best one before the CTA.
