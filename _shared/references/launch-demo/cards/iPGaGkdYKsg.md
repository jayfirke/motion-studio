## Visual style
Very pale blue and white with soft blue light pools; glossy blue glass orbs hold icons; white pills with soft shadows carry labels. One blue accent throughout. A rounded geometric sans: big gradient numerals for stats, small medium-weight labels elsewhere. Photographic avatars in circles and a rendered humanoid "AI agent" as the mascot.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Conversations are abstracted: voice-wave pills and chat bubbles link avatars; no app window during the first minute.
- The real dashboard appears only once, for about 6 s near the end (1:03–1:08), on a 3D-tilted plane, with no cursor and no interaction.
- Integrations are a static card of partner logos (0:50).
- Nothing is clicked or typed anywhere in the film.

## Motion and transitions
A single continuous canvas: the camera drifts and pushes while elements pop, scale and orbit; only two cuts in the first 6 s. One streak whip (0:27). The best structure is the orb pair: Coverage vs Cost at 0:11 returns as CSAT vs Cost at 1:09, closing the loop. Easing is smooth and floaty.

## Pacing
Even: 122 words/min and a new element about every 3 s, but motion intensity never changes; the stats run (0:53–1:02) is the only accelerating stretch.

## Sound
Mid-tempo electronic bed (~129 BPM) under a single, calm corporate narrator. 130 sound events per minute against almost no cuts points to a pop or whoosh on nearly every element (not checked by ear). Mix at -18.7 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- Stats beat: one giant number per 3 s, each with a small pill label (0:53–1:02). Fast proof that reads at phone size.
- Bookend: the trade-off orbs at 0:11 return with a new label at 1:09, so the end answers the opening.
- Voice made visible: waveform pills link speaker avatars to the agent (0:21).
- Problem as a falling chart: bars drop into blur under the question (0:08).
- One accent, one light: the glass orbs keep a consistent soft key light (0:11, 0:24).

## Avoid
- Rendered humanoid robot as the AI (0:18): uncanny and generic.
- Honeycomb of social icons (0:31) and greetings globe (0:40): stock clichés.
- Streak whip (0:27).
- The real product appears for only 6 s, tilted, with no cursor; it never feels like a demo.
- Uniform pale blue with no contrast moments; it floats instead of hitting.
