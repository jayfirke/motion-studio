## Visual style
Light grey ground with a subtle blob texture, product greens, HubSpot orange as a second accent, dark navy for the phone. Faithful redraw of both apps in macOS-style windows with traffic-light dots. The only headline is "HOW?"; all other text is UI text.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn UI in floating windows; the camera stays fixed and the windows move, scale and slide.
- Lift-out zoom: the key card leaves the page, scales up with a shadow, holds while the VO lists its contents, then drops back (0:12, 0:20–0:25). This is the film's main device.
- Toggle switch flips, then the tile pops out with a check (0:04–0:07): a satisfying setup beat.
- A live call timer ticking in real time (00:03 → 00:11) across 8 s of the film gives the in-call section urgency.
- Split screen (CRM left, phone right) to show two apps talking.

## Motion and transitions
Zero cuts in 64 s. Windows slide in and out, cards lift and land, the dialer slides from the right and back, the workflow window replaces the calendar by a blank-window morph (0:49). Smooth, snappy ease-out, no bounce. Everything shares one grey stage, so every move reads as continuous.

## Pacing
VO at 124 words/min, casual and quick, with slangy asides; a new UI state every 2.5–3 s. The lift-out card holds about 5 s so its details can be read. Breathes only on "HOW?" and the logo.

## What to take from it
- 0:20–0:25: lift the key card out of the page, hold it big while the VO reads its fields, then set it back. The cleanest way to make a dense UI readable at phone size.
- 0:04–0:07: toggle on, tile pops out with a check: setup shown as one satisfying action.
- 0:33–0:41: a real-time call timer ticking while context pops around the call; the clock sells "in the moment".
- 0:33: two apps side by side to show the hand-off between them.

## Avoid
- Floating triangles and plus signs in the margins (0:01, 0:09): decorative confetti, close to the banned particle look.
- Avatars, hearts and check badges swarming around the phone (0:41): busy and dated.
- Infinity-loop road with riding avatars (0:57): a generic "automation" metaphor.
- -21.2 LUFS is quiet; our mix targets -14.
