## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:06):** on cornflower blue, a social comment card types "Modern Accounting software that's keyboard centric?" (0:00–0:02). Emoji counters tick up (0 → 4 → 12 → 46) as replies stack ("No where to find.", "2024 and Still can't find it"), and the card grows downward with each reply.
- **Reveal (0:06–0:11):** a photo of a man at a laptop slides in on lavender (0:06.2). The layout turns white as the wordmark types on beside it with the tagline (0:07–0:10).
- **Feature 1 (0:12–0:17):** on yellow, a lightning bolt flashes, then "Lightning fast entries". A Create Payment form fills fast: ledger rows come from a dropdown and amounts appear row by row, with no cursor (0:14–0:17).
- **Feature 2 (0:17–0:27):** on green, "Bulk entries taking time?" beside a growing stack of paper invoices with a spinner (0:18), then a "NO MORE!" pill. Live hands type on a real laptop with the Bulk Payment Voucher comped onto the screen (0:20.5–0:22). The voucher table tilts toward camera, a bank dropdown opens, and Submit is pressed (0:24–0:27).
- **Close (0:27–0:32):** the UI shrinks into a blue frame (0:26.8), then the logo mark builds big, followed by the wordmark and a URL pill.

## Visual style
Flat colour-blocked grounds, one per section: blue for the problem, yellow for speed, green for bulk, white for the brand. White UI cards with one brand blue. Photography and live hands are used sparingly. A friendly geometric sans with short slides.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Rebuilt, real-looking UI** on floating cards with no browser chrome.
- **No cursor in the form beat:** fields fill on their own with dropdowns flashing open. Leaving the mouse out sells "keyboard-first".
- **A real laptop with real hands typing** and the UI on its screen: the one moment of physical cause.
- **A tilted table** with a dropdown chip opening on one row.
- **The problem as a community thread** with live reaction counters.

## Motion and transitions
- The ground floods to a new colour per chapter while the UI card stays centred, so the layout carries across.
- The comment card grows rather than cutting, and counters tick.
- The table shrinks into a framed thumbnail before the logo.
- Quick, clean ease-outs with little overshoot.

## Pacing
Punchy: a new idea every 3–5 s. Sparse VO (72 words/min) leaves room for visual jokes. The busiest 4 s is the form filling (0:14–0:17).

## Sound
- A ~123 BPM bright pop-electronic bed.
- Extremely dense SFX (208/min), most likely typing ticks and counter blips.
- A quiet mix (-23.7 LUFS).
- A friendly VO in short slogan lines.

## What to take from it
- **0:00–0:06:** the problem told as a comment thread whose counters climb as replies stack. Fast, human, funny.
- **0:14–0:17:** fields filling with no cursor at all to sell keyboard speed. The missing mouse is the demo.
- **0:20.5–0:22:** real hands typing on a real laptop with the UI on screen: physical cause for the speed claim.
- **0:12–0:27:** one ground colour per chapter while the UI stays centred. A cheap, clear chapter system.

## Avoid
- The lightning-bolt glow flash (0:12) and the infomercial "NO MORE!" stamp.
- An invented social thread that could pass as a real post.
- A quiet master, and synthetic typing blips (we want real key taps).
