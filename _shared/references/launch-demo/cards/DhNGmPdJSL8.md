## Visual style
- Bright and airy: a soft sky-blue blurred gradient everywhere, with white frosted cards and one cobalt accent.
- The brand mark is a circle split by a crescent. Its geometry drives the closing transition.
- Mixed media in the problem act, then isometric diagrams, then light UI. Rounded geometric sans.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real-looking light UI, tilted in 3D about 10–15° and floating without device frames.
- The camera glides along it; a large blue arrow cursor clicks clear targets (Ask, Upload) after typed questions.
- Small blue chapter labels sit left of the screen.
- A results panel or chart answers each click.

## Motion and transitions
- Continuous camera through 3D space; the first 36 s has no hard cut.
- The demo screens slide in on the same tilt, with blank-blue breaths between them (0:43).
- A logo-shape mask wipe closes (1:17).
- Gentle ease-in-out, airy rather than punchy.

## Pacing
- Sparse voice: 78 wpm, 130 words in about 85 s.
- Screens hold 4–5 s each (median 4.3 s).
- Music carries the gaps.
- It breathes well but sags in the middle, where several tilted screens look alike.

## Sound
- Mid-tempo (~99 BPM) bed, mixed loud (−14.9 LUFS).
- 131 events/min suggests frequent UI ticks and swishes under the sparse voice.

## What to take from it
- **Tilted-UI demo with a big readable cursor (0:52–1:09):** the camera glides along a 3D-tilted screen, the cursor clicks, and the answer appears. It reads as a demo without device frames.
- **Side chapter label beside the UI (0:48, 1:05):** a short label sits next to the screen it explains.
- **Stack-building diagram (0:27–0:31):** layers drop into place to explain the architecture in one image.
- **Logo-geometry mask wipe into the end card (1:17):** the brand shape does the transition.

## Avoid
- Binary-digit rain and torn-newspaper stat collages. Both feel dated and clichéd.
- Floating social-post cards, which have no product in them.
- Several near-identical tilted screens in a row (0:39–1:00).
- Voice this sparse would not fix the owner's "slow" complaint.
