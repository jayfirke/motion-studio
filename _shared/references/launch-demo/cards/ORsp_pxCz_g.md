## What to take from it
- **Orbit diagram (0:17):** the agent with its capability icons on rings, the whole product in one image.
- **Monitor push-in (0:33–0:42):** live action pushes onto the monitor, then the same UI fills the frame.
- **Agent card inside a real app (1:31):** a small card grows into a panel where the work already is.
- **Word-by-word kinetic line (2:34):** each word brightens as the voice says it.
- **2 s cut burst after long holds (2:16):** this gives the ending its lift.

## Avoid
- The 19 s cursor-free drifts. They are slow, and there is no visible cause, which is exactly the owner's complaint about our draft.
- Redrawn UI where the real product exists.
- Noun-list narration.
- Light-stream and sonar glow effects.
