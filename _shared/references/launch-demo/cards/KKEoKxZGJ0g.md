## Visual style
Strict monochrome lilac: every footage clip is tinted purple, UI is white cards on lilac with violet charts, one coral accent in diagrams. Typography is a rounded geometric sans: bold white headlines layered over giant outlined (stroke-only) words and numbers that run off the frame. Footage lives inside pill and circle masks. Soft drop shadows, glassy rims, lots of air.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn UI as clean cards: a full dashboard (0:17), a map view (0:47), a scenario planner (1:00).
- Cards are pulled out of the UI and orbit a 3D product render (0:27–0:30), so data sits on the object it describes.
- One card template re-used with the product swapped on the beat (0:34–0:37).
- No cursor, no clicks, no typing in the sampled frames; the UI is presented, not operated.

## Motion and transitions
Word-by-word hits on the beat: a single bold word lands, the next word joins on the next beat, and a huge outlined numeral rises behind them (0:39–0:50). Footage is masked into pills and circles that scale up into the next shot. The orbit is the recurring device: pills around the globe, then around the man, then cards around the product. Snappy ease-out on type, softer drift on the cards.

## Pacing
Fast for an explainer: a new shot every 3 s, almost 14 transitions per minute, and voice at 103 words/min. Stats act is the fastest stretch, with a word or a number every beat. The only rest is the logo mark alone (1:09).

## Sound
Loud, punchy master (-15.3 LUFS). Male narration, confident and promotional, written in short claims with one "here's the kicker" turn. Upbeat pop-electronic bed (~118 BPM) that the word hits follow. SFX not verified by ear; 83 events/min fits hits on each word and soft whooshes on masks.

## What to take from it
- 0:39–0:50: word-by-word headline on the beat, with a giant outlined number rising behind it; two text levels only, very readable.
- 0:27–0:30: data cards orbit the 3D product so the numbers belong to an object.
- 0:34–0:37: one card layout held while the content swaps on the beat; reads as a system, not a slideshow.
- 0:00–0:14: one strict brand colour across footage, UI and type gives a premium, unified look.

## Avoid
- Stock footage of factories, scrap heaps and shoppers as filler between UI beats.
- No visible interaction: no cursor or press, so the product never feels used.
- Stressed-man-with-question-marks problem shot (0:14): cliché.
- Stat claims (40 %, 73 %) without source on screen; fine for them, not for our film.
