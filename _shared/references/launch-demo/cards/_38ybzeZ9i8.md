## Visual style
A single-hue world: lavender ground, violet icons, white cards, soft wide shadows. Depth from z-layered cards with background blur (0:12) and 3D-tilted app windows (0:30, 1:09). Type is a small rounded sans, one short line per beat, set in pills or under objects.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
Detailed, product-accurate UI recreations rather than screenshots:
- the check designer at a 3D tilt with a visible arrow cursor dragging an asset into place (0:30–0:34);
- labelled callouts on thin leader lines pointing at the exact feature (0:34);
- the dashboard tilted with KPI tiles detaching toward camera (1:09);
- a payee-side mobile flow (1:06, 1:13–1:16).

There are no zoom-ins on pixels: the camera stays wide and the UI parts come to the camera.

## Motion and transitions
Only two detected cuts in 85 s (0:04.5 and 1:06). Everything between is one 61 s continuous animation on the same lavender ground: the cloud, nodes and cards morph and reposition instead of cutting. Elements pop with a gentle scale-up and settle. The cloud logo is the carry that comes back at 0:16, 0:55 and 1:23.

## Pacing
Calm but never empty: a measured 123 words/min, something changing every 3–4 s inside the long shot, and the opening stat landing inside 2 s. The densest stretch is the feature run (0:30–0:48).

## Sound
A bright, quick electronic bed (~144 BPM) under a single narrator who reads an ad script. With 145 onsets/min, the mix is likely full of small pops and ticks on each card arrival, but that's inferred from the numbers, not heard. At −15.2 LUFS it is close to our −14 target.

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:05: a stat hook in a pill with a donut counting up over real footage, so the problem lands in 2 s.
- 0:09–0:13: a swarm of question cards at different depths, blurred, the camera slowly drifting. The problem is shown as noise.
- 0:30–0:34: a tilted app window, a cursor drag, then three leader-line callouts naming what you just saw.
- 1:09: KPI tiles lift off the dashboard toward camera, so the proof is literally pulled out of the product.

## Avoid
- The fully monochrome lavender gets monotonous by 0:50.
- The puffy 3D cloud and the speaker-cone rings (1:02) feel stock.
- Centered text on a blank gradient (1:18).
- Third-party client logos used as decoration (0:05).
