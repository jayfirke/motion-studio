## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:16):** a phone on a dark bedside with alert bubbles floating up (0:02), then hands on the phone (0:05–0:08). A businessman in the street follows, with teal alert toasts tracked into the air beside him ("40% drop in Net Profit Margin...", 0:12–0:15).
- **Reveal (0:16–0:22):** a dark teal frame, then a macro on the app header ("Connect to your data on the move") pulling back to the full phone with "Ask Lumin" and Nudges (0:17–0:20).
- **Context (0:22–0:25):** night street and a tram commuter with floating chart cards. "Run Your Business Anytime / Anywhere" sits in the lower third, with the last word swapping.
- **Demo (0:25–1:05):**
  - **Typed question:** "What are the top three regions by Net Profit Margin?" types into a search bar (0:28), and a bar chart answers (0:32).
  - **Nudges:** the list rises with the word as giant letters behind the phone (0:35), into details, charts and a "Model Interpretation" card (0:38–0:45).
  - **Voice question:** "What will the Net Profit Margin be this year?" fills a speech bubble as the mic pulses (0:48). The app's spoken answer plays while its sentence highlights in step and a waveform runs along the card (0:52–0:59).
  - **Recommendations:** suggested questions, plus Teams, Outlook, Slack and SharePoint (1:05).
- **Recap (1:09–1:16):** the phone as a hub with Cognitive Search, Auto Insights and Advanced Analytics cards fanning out.
- **CTA (1:19):** logo, App Store badge, URL.

## Visual style
Two grounds: saturated aqua for demo beats, deep teal-navy for reveal and recap. Footage is natural, with UI toasts comped in perspective. The UI has a dark header over white cards. Feature words appear as giant pale letters behind the phone instead of title cards. Drifting translucent squares and a thin arc decorate.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **A real-looking app on a frameless phone**, cropped tight. Cards break out of the phone and float beside it.
- **No cursor.** Actions show as typed queries and as a pulsing mic while a speech bubble fills word by word.
- **The spoken answer made visible:** a waveform runs while the sentence highlights in sync with the app's voice.
- **Macro → pull-back** reveal of the header (0:17–0:20). Charts draw on as screens arrive.

## Motion and transitions
- Few cuts: the phone slides, scales and rotates in one 2D space as cards detach.
- Live footage leads into UI through a teal flash or through toasts tracked onto the footage.
- The giant word is masked by the moving phone, so title and UI share one stack.
- Smooth ease-in-out; the Q&A holds 24 s on internal animation.

## Pacing
Dense words (138/min) but sparse cuts (median 4.6 s): the voice hurries, the picture stays readable. It breathes during the app's spoken answer (0:52–0:59). The end recap is rushed (three labels in about 6 s).

## Sound
- A ~112 BPM soft corporate electronic bed.
- Moderate SFX (91/min): bubble pops, toast whooshes, typing ticks.
- A smooth narrator, plus a second synthetic-style voice for the app's answer.
- A quiet master (-23.5 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- **0:48–0:59:** the question fills a bubble, then the answer highlights in sync with the spoken reply over a running waveform. Ideal for showing an agent talking back.
- **0:17–0:20:** a macro on the header pulling back to the whole screen.
- **0:35:** the feature name as giant letters behind the device, masked by it.
- **0:12–0:15:** alert toasts tracked into the air beside a real person.
- **0:28–0:32:** a question types, then the answer chart replaces it.

## Avoid
- Drifting squares and the decorative arc.
- Stock businessman footage.
- A quiet master.
- An unreadable 6 s feature-label recap.
- Saturated cyan full-frame grounds.
