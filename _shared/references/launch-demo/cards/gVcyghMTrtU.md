## What to take from it
- **One key word scaled and coloured in a kinetic line** (0:01, 0:55).
- **Live counters on a real dashboard** (0:47–0:52).
- **Zero-cut continuity** across the whole film.
- **Visible button press on the CTA** (1:04–1:07).
- **Model logo ring** (0:41) as the any-model proof in one frame.

## Avoid
- Speed lines, shard bursts and a sparkle on "AI": near the banned bursts.
- Isometric cities, servers and locks: stock AI iconography.
- A feature list with no task shown end to end, the same weakness as our draft.
- UI holds too short to read.
