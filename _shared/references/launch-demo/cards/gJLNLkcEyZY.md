## Visual style
Two grounds with a strict job each:
- **Light grey with soft coral and blue blur blobs:** ideas and metaphors in thin-line icons, photo avatars in circles, dotted connectors.
- **Dark navy with the same blobs:** real product screens.

The one brand blue sits on buttons and the sidebar. Type is barely used outside the UI and the logo.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
Real app screens, on phone and web together:
- dashed callout rectangles name the regions of a phone screen (0:24);
- alerts arrive as notification cards that pop beside the UI (0:37), so the signal reaches the product first;
- a button press opens a menu, then a side list, in a quick cascade (0:40). The press is shown by the button highlight, with no visible cursor;
- the web app and phone overlap in depth, showing the same account on both (0:35).

## Motion and transitions
Dotted lines are the connective tissue: they link avatars, carry the clock out of frame, draw the path the cards travel on, and seem to turn into the wordmark. Light-to-dark hard cuts (0:21.3, 0:34.0, 0:41.3, 0:48.6) are the only real cuts and mark idea versus product. Elements scale up with a soft settle.

## Pacing
A steady 121 words/min, and something new every 2–3 s even inside 10 s holds. Concept beats are short (2–3 s each); product beats hold longer so the UI can be read. The logo and CTA get about 7 s.

## Sound
A mid-tempo bed (~99 BPM) under a confident narrator. The captions mark crowd applause twice (about 0:11 and 0:20, estimated from word position), so a stadium-cheer stinger seems to punctuate the sports metaphor. 140 onsets/min suggests busy small SFX on pops (inferred, not heard).

## What to take from it
- 0:21.3 / 0:34.0 / 0:41.3 / 0:48.6: light means the idea, dark means the real product, so the viewer always knows which they are looking at.
- 0:24: dashed callout boxes around regions of a real screen, labelled in two words each.
- 0:40: press → menu → side list cascade in about a second, the UI reacting in layers.
- 0:27–0:30: event cards along a dotted path to an outcome, a timeline read at a glance.
- 0:55: the end card is a hub of the product's own content around the logo, not a blank background.

## Avoid
- Grawlix speech bubbles (0:04), cartoonish.
- The stock cut-out man cheering (0:48).
- The orbiting star with scattered triangles (0:32), which reads as confetti.
- Crowd-cheer stingers. The house rules ban game-like sound.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Idea/product ground rule:** problems and the hook live on a light, quiet ground with line marks, and every real screen (terminal, Previs Studio, renders) sits on the dark ground. Each switch is a hard cut on a beat.
2. **Hand-off as a dotted path:** a note pinned on the previs timeline travels along a dotted line through approved-video-spec.json to the engine card, with a soft whoosh along the line.
3. **CTA hub:** the studio mark in the centre, ringed by the real artifacts the film showed (previs page, scorecard, render frame, spec file), which then fold in as the button grows.
