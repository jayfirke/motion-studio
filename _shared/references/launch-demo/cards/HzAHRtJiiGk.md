## Visual style
Flat colour grounds that change per phrase: mint, lime, pale mint, grey-teal, charcoal, white. Small centred sans type in dark text, with a mint highlighter bar behind the key words ("it reads", "next update?", "winning pitch"). The UI is redrawn, white cards with traffic-light dots and mint pills. One orange for negative sentiment.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Bar lists with percentages that count up (0:32, 0:46.5).
- A split bar with two round thumb icons, 54 against 46, where the numbers tick toward the final values (0:33.6, from 48 and 50 in the frames).
- Chips that select (Confused is highlighted in the Points of Interest panel at 0:21).
- A leaderboard table for the end result (0:47.7).
- No cursor and no typing: the UI animates itself.

## Motion and transitions
Hard colour cuts at the pace of the words, a flat ground swapped instantly. Words slide or pop in; two moments scramble letters (0:12, 0:13.4). The brain made of avatars pops in piece by piece. The film carries only the highlighter bar (it re-appears under "it reads" and "winning pitch") and the pill chips across scenes.

## Pacing
Very fast: median shot 1.4 s, a new frame every 1–2 s, no voice, so the words are the voice. A joke ("it reads everything but minds. Maybe in the next update?") gives the one beat of relief at 0:38–0:41.

## Sound
No voice. Loud, bright bed (-13.1 LUFS, about 112 BPM) and the most sound events per min of the batch (180.9). With one word per shot, most of those events are probably a tick or whoosh on each word change (inferred, not heard). It is a sound-per-word film; our rule is fewer sounds than beats.

## What to take from it
- One idea per word at the problem: "Sales teams / sick of dealing / objections / that's too late?" with the sentence readable at a glance (0:01–0:10).
- A highlighter bar behind the key words instead of a glow (0:36, 0:42).
- The product's moment list shown as ranked bars that count up (0:32).
- A joke line placed right after a feature claim as a breather (0:36–0:41).
- A leaderboard as the closing "result" table (0:47.7).

## Avoid
- Scrambled letters on charcoal (0:12, 0:13.4): a glitch.
- Flat colour swaps every word (mint, lime, teal): reads as a strobe and loses the one-accent rule.
- Stock footage of stressed agents (0:05, 0:09, 0:14).
- A sound on every word change.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Problems chapter: one word per beat, "Thin synth. No voice. Basic.", on a single calm ground with a highlighter bar under the last word, instead of changing colour every word.
2. Results chapter: ranked bars that count up for the critique scores (hook, readability, motion, sound) with the winners highlighted.
3. A joke beat after the biggest claim, delivered as one line on a clear ground.
