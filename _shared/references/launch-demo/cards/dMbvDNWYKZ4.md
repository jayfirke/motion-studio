# DialWorks: music-only "stomp" brand launch, kinetic type over footage and UI

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dMbvDNWYKZ4 · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:15
- **Type:** launch (stomp: music + kinetic type, no voice) · **Product:** DialWorks, a sales-readiness platform that trains reps on realistic calls, scores them and reports on reps and hiring candidates
- **Numbers:** 8.8 transitions per min (19 detected cuts, many in 2–6-frame bursts during whips) · median shot 5.4 s (longest 22.4 s) · ~5 words/min (no narration) · ~118 BPM · 93 sound events/min · -11.0 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:05 Hook: a question in large type on white, key words in green, a phone icon above (0:02); the answer line follows, "speed" in green (0:05).
- 0:05–0:22 Problem: a live-action rep in a headset at a laptop with a small video-call inset; stacked caption bars build line by line (0:08–0:11); a green card about preparing for real questions (0:14); three objection bubbles pop around the rep, too expensive, missing feature, too early (0:17–0:19); a close shot with a "battle tested" line (0:20).

- 0:41–0:58 Benefits: a diagonal wipe reveals hands on a laptop showing the same dashboard, a line about saving trainers' hours sliding off (0:42–0:44); dashboard left, three-line headline right (0:49); a detail card pops out of the table (0:52); a blurred streak whip (0:52); a performance-data line builds on white (0:55–0:58).
- 0:58–1:15 Close: tilted candidate report (1:01); confetti around "All-in-one platform" (1:04–1:07); wordmark, hashtag, URL on green (1:11).

## Visual style
Forest green and white with a lime highlight on key words. Bold rounded sans, accent word heavier and green; small caption bars stacked over footage. Diagonal green shapes, dot grids and squiggles as decoration. Warm natural-light stock of one rep.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real-looking UI in a browser frame, entering on a 3D tilt and settling flat (0:24–0:27).
- Stat tiles change value across shots (0:36–0:46), so the dashboard feels live.
- A detail card pops off a table row (0:52) as the zoom.
- The same dashboard on a real laptop in footage (0:42) links UI and use.
- No cursor, clicks or typing: a gallery of screens, not a demo.

## Motion and transitions
Music-cut and fast: diagonal panels wipe between footage and UI (0:22, 0:42); text slides in and out sideways; a word swap on the beat (0:30–0:33); a streak whip (0:52). Snappy ease-out. Carries come from the diagonal panels and the green field.

## Pacing
No voice: type and music do the talking. Each text card holds 2–3 s, about one phrase per bar. UI shots hold 3–5 s. The first 22 s are problem footage; the product arrives at 0:24.

## Sound
Stomp-and-clap track near 118 BPM, mastered loud at -11.0 LUFS. Claps and stomps hit text changes; most of the 93 sound events per minute are in the track, plus swishes on wipes. No VO.

## What to take from it
- 0:30–0:33: a word swap on the beat ("months" drops, "days" lands in the same slot): before/after in one line.
- 0:36–0:46: stat tiles that change value between shots.
- 0:17–0:19: objection bubbles round the person; the problem in their own words.
- 0:42: a diagonal wipe that reveals the same dashboard on a real laptop.

## Avoid
- Confetti around "All-in-one platform" (1:04): banned.
- Streak blur whip (0:52): close to a glitch.
- UI gallery with no cursor or interaction: the "not a demo" problem.
- Squiggles, dot grids and stacked caption boxes: decoration with no job.
- -11 LUFS master: too loud for our spec.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film

- Problems chapter: the owner's real notes on the old draft pop as bubbles round the old render.
- Build and critique: score tiles change value between shots (6 → 7 → 8+) as the loop runs.
