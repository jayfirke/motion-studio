# The Unreleased Product Launch Video

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pQkbEUbLxbE · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:30
- **Type:** ad (launch teaser) · **Product:** affable.ai, an influencer-marketing platform for finding creators, running campaigns and tracking sales
- **Numbers:** 24.2 transitions per min · median shot 1.96 s (0.79–7.8 s) · 22.2 words/min (no narration: the captions are only vocal stingers in the music) · ~103 BPM · 134.9 sound events/min · -13.6 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:03 Hook: outlined caps text on black poses a question. Three green and white hashtags stack in its place (0:03).
- 0:03–0:06 Stock footage of a selfie under a two-line bold caps title (0:04), then a red-tinted frame with an outline box sweeping across (0:06).
- 0:07–0:13 Problems: DM bubbles pop beside a woman at a laptop (0:07), then her head in her hands under a heavy caption about one-by-one messages (0:09). A tense close-up with a translucent spreadsheet overlaid and a three-line caption (0:11).
- 0:13 Turn: a cut to peach with a big pink "Or..." and teal and orange diagonal shapes.
- 0:14–0:16 An Influencer Discovery screen with an arrow cursor (0:14), then a pink bridge word (0:15).
- 0:16.6–0:24.4 The demo, 7.8 s with no cut:
  - A Create Campaign modal fills its name, dates, mention chip and hashtags (0:17–0:18).
  - The cursor clicks the purple Create button.
  - The campaign page slides over the modal (0:19) and the influencer table scrolls in (0:20).
  - The page tilts onto a teal diagonal and the cursor ticks a row checkbox (0:22–0:23).
  - The camera zooms into the stat strip (posts, stories, 2.4 % engagement, 1.5 M reach, $38k EMV) as the cursor clicks Send Mail with a purple ripple (0:24).
- 0:24.4–0:27 Features stack line by line in two columns on a teal and orange diagonal split.
- 0:27–0:30 End card: logo, Get a Demo and Free Trial pills, and the cursor presses Get a Demo.

## Visual style
Two acts, two looks. Problem: stock footage, heavy caps captions, tinted overlays. Solution: a warm peach ground with big flat orange and teal triangles and circles, white UI cards with soft shadows, and pink for the bridge words.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product UI floating as a card on peach, no device frame.
- An arrow cursor is always present in the demo stretch, and every step is caused by it: fill, Create, select, Send Mail.
- Form fields populate in sequence and chips pop in.
- A press is a purple circular ripple under the cursor on the button (0:24).
- The zoom turns a dense table into a readable stat row at the moment of the click; a tilt onto a diagonal adds depth without 3D.

## Motion and transitions
- The problem act hard-cuts on the beat (3.3, 6.7, 8.4, 10.6, 12.3, 13.1 s).
- The solution act carries: modal → page → table → zoom in one move, each card sliding over the last. Diagonal shapes wipe across (0:15, 0:24).
- Captions slam in; UI moves are quick ease-out of about 0.3–0.4 s.

## Pacing
Very fast: a new state every 1–2 s. Problems get 2 s each; the demo gets the longest unbroken stretch (7.8 s) with a state change every 1–1.5 s inside it. The end card holds about 3 s.

## Sound
- A hip-hop or trap beat at about 103 BPM with vocal stingers (a laugh and short shouted exclamations) placed on the cuts.
- No voiceover.
- Very dense SFX (135/min): whooshes on caption slams and wipes, clicks on each cursor press. Mastered loud (-13.6 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- 0:16.6–0:24.4: a 7.8 s single-take demo where a cursor fills a form, clicks Create, the next page slides over and a zoom lands on the stat row as Send Mail is pressed.
- 0:24: a click ripple timed with a camera zoom, so the press and the punch-in are one moment.
- 0:13: a cut to a single turn word to switch from the problem act to the product act.
- 0:24.4–0:27: features stacking line by line in two columns, a fast catalogue beat before the CTA.
- 0:27–0:30: the cursor presses the CTA pill on the end card, so the film ends on an action.

## Avoid
- Stock-footage acting (head in hands, a tense close-up): it reads as cheap.
- Vocal stingers and a game-like beat; a mix louder than -14 LUFS.
- Dated outline-box and hashtag openers, and three-line captions over busy video.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Build and critique: one unbroken 6–8 s take where the cursor fills the brief, clicks Generate, the previs page slides over, and the camera punches into the score row as Approve is pressed.
2. Any agent: features stack line by line in two columns (Codex, Cursor, OpenCode... on the left; HyperFrames, Remotion... on the right) as a fast catalogue beat.
3. CTA: end on the cursor pressing a real button on our end card, rather than a static logo hold.
