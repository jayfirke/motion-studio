## Visual style
Cool corporate: stock office footage, graded teal and warm, with clean light-blue motion-graphics plates between. White rounded cards, blue pills, line icons, green check badges. Labels are small; there is almost no large type.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI, two ways: screen-replaced into laptops in live footage (0:24, 0:37, 0:46), and as floating windows on a flat plate (0:53–1:03).
- Cursor present in most UI shots: moves to a filter, then opens a dropdown (0:57, 1:00). No visible press feedback.
- Focus by depth: one panel pops forward sharp while the rest of the screen blurs back (0:43, 0:50).
- A pill tag names each module (Imaging, Remote Control, MDM) so the viewer knows where they are.

## Motion and transitions
Mix of soft drifts and a few loud moves: a radial zoom blur (0:17), panels sliding laterally, a blob morph at the outro (1:15). Inside motion-graphics plates, pills slide in on fast ease-out and checks pop in sequence. The cut sheet shows the same laptop shot reused with the progress bar advancing, which works as a time-lapse.

## Pacing
Moderate voice (103 wpm) with room to breathe; 4 s median shot. The module tour (0:50–1:03) is the tightest stretch: one module every 3–4 s with a cursor action each.

## Sound
Bright, quick corporate electronic bed (~152 BPM) with steady percussion. Friendly, clear VO in short, plain sentences. About 110 events/min: pops on pills and checks, swooshes on window moves.

## What to take from it
- Problem pills slide in half-cut, then all get a green check in sequence (0:29–0:33): a quick before/after without a cut.
- Progress counter advancing on the same shot as a time-lapse of work done overnight (0:20–0:23).
- One panel pops forward sharp while the screen behind blurs (0:43).
- Module tag pill + cursor opening a menu, one module per 3–4 s (0:53–1:03).
- URL in a search-bar pill as the CTA (1:11).

## Avoid
- Radial zoom blur (0:17) and stock-office footage: dated.
- Icons floating around a worried face (0:05): cliché problem shot.
- Agency outro and blob morph.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Problems chapter: pain-point pills (thin sound, no voice, slow cuts) slide in and each gets a check when the new pipeline lands.
2. Build-and-critique chapter: one render-progress shot that advances while critique scores tick up, as a time-lapse.
3. Previs Studio tour: a small tag pill names each area (Timeline, Options, Notes) while the cursor acts in it, 3–4 s per area.
