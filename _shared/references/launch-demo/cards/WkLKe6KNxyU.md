## Visual style
Premium flat 2D with soft gradients: near-white background, blue as the lead colour, peach and coral as secondaries, floating blue dots for depth. Rounded friendly people, avatars in circles with soft shadows. Type is small, thin and sparse (one short label per idea, often under 3 % of frame height). Depth comes from blur on foreground dots and slightly extruded 3D bars and cylinders.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- No real UI. The product is suggested by redrawn cards: a community post (0:17), dashboard tiles with pie and line charts (1:05), a table with a highlighted row (1:13).
- No cursor; a magnifier sweep is the only "action" on the data (1:13).
- Data movement shown as arrows and flying cards between people and stores (0:49), and lines pulling into a cylinder (0:57).

## Motion and transitions
The whole film is one shot: every object turns into the next (laptop → people → avatar dots → network → city → hub → rings → data cards). Nothing cuts; the carry rule is followed absolutely. Movement is slow and floaty, soft ease-in-out, with constant gentle drift on background dots. The rings at 0:45 act as a breath and a reset between the story and the product.

## Pacing
Medium: a new image every 4 s, steadily, matched to a measured narration at 134 words/min. Never rushes and never rests long, except the logo hold. The steady tempo reads as calm and corporate; it lacks spikes.

## Sound
Narrator in a calm, warm documentary tone; script is benefit-led and metaphor-heavy. Soft corporate electronic/piano bed around 108 BPM. The 114 events/min count suggests light sparkle and swish SFX on each morph, sitting low.

## What to take from it
- Single continuous shot where each object becomes the next (0:05–0:41): the strongest carry example in the batch.
- Benefit row: icon circles land one at a time along a line, each with a small caption, as the voice lists them (1:17–1:25).
- Magnifier sweep over dashboard cards as a "look closer" beat (1:13).
- Concentric rings as a breathing reset between chapters (0:45).

## Avoid
- No real UI, no cursor, no click: a story about a product, not a demo.
- Thin small labels that would fail the 360 px phone test.
- Uniform floaty easing throughout makes it feel slow; the owner already called our draft slow.
