## Visual style
Mixed-media collage: paper texture, torn paper scraps, tape, photographic cutouts (ledger, hand, retro computer) layered with flat shapes (circles, sunbursts, shards). Colour moves by era: cream, peach, orange, violet, then clean white for the present and the product. A thin serif-numeral year ruler at the bottom ties every era together. The product UI is clean and modern, a deliberate contrast to the collage.

## Motion and transitions
One continuous 20.5 s shot until the logo. The year ruler is the carry: it scrolls left as each object swaps, so time literally drives the cuts. Objects replace each other in place on the same circle; backgrounds snap colour on the beat; speed shards give the 1990s jump energy. The orange paint drip (0:14) is the turn from problem to product. Easing is punchy but not bouncy.

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:14: a scrolling ruler at the bottom that carries the whole story; each object swaps on its own circle as the ruler advances. Carry without a single cut.
- 0:12: the old screen on a new monitor, a before-state in one image.
- 0:16–0:19: hover-only tooltips on charts to show the UI is live without clicking.
- Era-coded background colours that snap on the beat: an energy curve built from colour.

## Avoid
- Paint-drip wipe (0:14): dated and decorative.
- Orbiting icons and app-badge bursts (0:05, 0:08): close to the banned particle burst and stock explainer grammar.
- Speed shards streaking past (0:06): a transition effect for its own sake.
- 71 words/min is too sparse for the owner's dense-narration bar (fine at 26 s, wrong for a 2-minute launch).
