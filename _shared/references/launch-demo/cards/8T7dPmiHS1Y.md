# Best SaaS Explainer Video Example - Sogage: A One-Stop Solution for Gaming Community Management

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8T7dPmiHS1Y · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:12
- **Type:** explainer / demo · **Product:** Sogage, one dashboard for every player conversation about a game, with alerts, replies, labels and AI summaries
- **Numbers:** 5.8 transitions per min · median shot 6.0 s (longest 30.8 s) · 130.8 words/min · ~112 BPM · 157.3 sound events/min · -22.3 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:07):** a mint and coral mesh gradient. White chips of platform logos and feature labels orbit a ring, then burst outward as more platforms join (0:05).
- **Solution (0:07–0:14):** the logo mark alone (0:08), then the platform icons fall into orbit around it (0:11).
- **Feed (0:14–0:21):** tilted post cards fly in from the icons (0:14) and land as the real dashboard in 3D perspective (0:17). The cursor scrolls the feed (0:20).
- **Alerts (0:21–0:27):** the dashboard blurs, and a notification panel of custom-filter alerts pops forward sharp (0:23–0:26).
- **Collaborate (0:27–0:38):** one post isolates and enlarges (0:29). The cursor clicks reply and a reply modal opens (0:32). It clicks the label icon and a label dropdown drops (0:35).
- **AI insights (0:38–0:52):** the cursor clicks a coral toolbar icon to open a menu with Sentiment Analysis and Summarization (0:41). An analytics dashboard tilts in (0:44), then explodes into chart cards floating at different depths: lines, a word cloud, bars, a donut (0:47–0:50).
- **Benefits (0:52–1:02):** outline icons: a gamepad, a "Productivity" speedometer, "Scale Up Engagement" bars.
- **CTA (1:02–1:12):** a ring zooms through (1:02) to the lockup with a coral "Sign Up For Free Trial" button (1:05).

## Visual style
Real UI floating on an animated mesh gradient in the logo's two hues. White UI, a teal sidebar, coral actions. A friendly rounded wordmark. Depth comes from perspective tilt, parallax layers and strong background blur. No characters, no footage.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Real UI tilted in perspective** with no device frame.
- **One cursor click per feature, each opening something** (0:32 modal, 0:35 dropdown, 0:41 menu).
- **Blur-and-lift focus pull:** everything defocuses except the active panel, which scales forward (0:23, 0:29).
- **Exploded dashboard** (0:47–0:50).
- No typing: the reply field stays empty.

## Motion and transitions
Icons → cards → dashboard (0:11 → 0:17) carries the opener into the product. Blur-and-lift is the main transition inside the UI (only 7 cuts in 72 s). A ring zoom-through into the CTA. Smooth ease-out moves with a gentle 3D drift and small dropdown pops.

## Pacing
Brisk: 131 wpm, a feature about every 5 s, each with one click. Each UI state holds about 3 s, enough to see the click land but not to read the post text. The benefits section slows into generic icons, and the end card holds about 7 s.

## Sound
A light upbeat bed at ~112 BPM with dense onsets (157/min), likely ticks on the cards and dropdowns. A quick, straight VO. A quiet mix at -22.3 LUFS. The SFX timbre isn't confirmed.

## What to take from it
- **Click → panel → next** rhythm (0:32, 0:35, 0:41): the simplest demo grammar with a visible cause.
- **Blur-and-lift focus pull** (0:23, 0:29).
- **Exploded dashboard** (0:47–0:50) so each widget reads at phone size.
- **Background from the brand's own two colours** (throughout).
- **Opener objects become the UI** (0:11 → 0:17).

## Avoid
- Outline benefit icons after a good demo (0:52–1:02).
- A long end card and a quiet mix.
- A mesh gradient behind a lone logo: close to "title on a gradient".
- A reply modal that opens empty, a missed typing moment.
