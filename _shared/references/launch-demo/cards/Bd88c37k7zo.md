## Visual style
Light-gray or single-hue grounds (violet, teal, orange), one accent, pill labels, glassy cards with soft shadows. Geometric sans, medium weight, short lines; words land one at a time. Depth comes from layered cards at different blur levels, not 3D scenes. Characters always sit inside a UI frame.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn vector UI, simplified to one task per shot and magnified 3–4× (4:33).
- Hand cursor presses a bevelled glass Play pill (cut sheet).
- Query bars stack in perspective with depth of field, as if typed in turn (8:18); dropdown chips line up at a tilt (8:50).
- Chip grids build tag by tag, then a full-height brand-blue slab sweeps them off (about 1:10).

## Motion and transitions
Continuous transforms instead of cuts. The blue slab wipes, becomes the 3D logo block, then reveals a laptop (about 1:30). A dial hand sweeps to mean lost time; big letters part to reveal an object. Smooth ease-out, short holds. Cut bursts only at outros: 645.3–645.8 s and 763.7–764.1 s are runs of 0.03–0.1 s flash cuts.

## Pacing
VO films change visual state every 2–3 s and breathe only on logo cards. The music-only Google section (3:50–5:10) feels slow and vague by comparison, proof that narration drives pace.

## Sound
- **Music:** corporate electronic at about 123 BPM, steady, little drop.
- **VO:** brisk benefit stacks, each film opened with a question or a pain line ("Are you a B2B company ready to supercharge your growth?").
- **SFX:** about 91 events/min: soft whooshes on slab wipes, small ticks on chips, swells into logos, all under the voice.

## What to take from it
- Slab wipe that becomes the logo block, then reveals the device (about 1:10–1:30): carry, not crossfade.
- One UI panel at 3× scale filling the frame so it reads on a phone (4:33).
- Prompt bars stacked in depth, one per question (8:18).
- Final action: a cursor clicks a URL pill (9:23).
- A benefit line arriving word by word beside one symbolic object (1:20).

## Avoid
- Glow orbs and light streaks (9:55), mascot comedy (7:14), wall-of-faces stock avatars (1:53).
- Flash-cut stutters into end cards (645 s, 764 s), which read as glitch.
- Music-only stretches with no VO (3:50–5:10).
