## Visual style
Royal blue and white, green and coral for status. Soft glass cards with big blurred shadows, 16–24 px corners, lots of white space. Copy lives inside the UI; the only large type is the end tagline. Live footage only at the open.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Mostly redrawn, simplified UI components (calendar, chat, booking card) rather than full screens; one real dashboard capture tilted in 3D (0:37).
- Each feature is a single component that lives for 4–8 s and changes state: dates fill, an avatar is dragged onto a slot (0:25), a message bubble arrives (0:56).
- Cursor appears on the website builder (1:07) and the Book Now press (1:11); otherwise actions happen by themselves.
- Integrations shown as logos plugging into the product with animated connector lines (0:21, 1:15).

## Motion and transitions
Continuous camera on a soft field: cards slide, tilt and fan out; long shots (up to 16 s) with motion inside. Carries: the logo coin-flip wipe (0:16), a motion-blur whip (0:28), the logo becoming the node between host and guest (0:44), the end ring scaling to fill (1:26). Smooth ease-in-out, gentle 3D tilts of 10–20°.

## Pacing
Steady explainer tempo: 141 wpm voice, a new component every 4 s, each feature a 10–20 s chapter announced by the narrator. No dead time, but no fast bursts either.

## Sound
Light acoustic-pop bed (~89 BPM) under a friendly, conversational VO (the captions show music swells between phrases). About 92 events/min: probably soft pops and swishes on card entrances. Loud master (-14.5 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- Live action dissolving into floating UI cards around the user, then into the product mark (0:00–0:06).
- Logos plugging into the product with flowing connector lines (0:21) — a clean way to show "works with any agent".
- One component per feature that changes state on screen (0:25 avatar drag onto a date).
- Tilted card listing what the guest receives, item by item (1:00).
- A ring of module icons filling label by label as a product map (0:10–0:13).

## Avoid
- Confetti on the Book Now press (1:11) — banned.
- Coin-flip logo wipe (0:16) and motion-blur whip feel template-like.
- Redrawn UI where real screens exist; our house rule wants real captures.
