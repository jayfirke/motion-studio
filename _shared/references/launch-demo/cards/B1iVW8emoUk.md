## Visual style
A talking head is the spine: medium-close, warm office, handwritten whiteboard words echoing the script. Graphics are flat: one warm orange radial stage, white rounded cards, faithful app UI, pill tags. One accent colour shared by tags, stage and logo.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn but faithful cards, one per step of the user journey, in order: quiz, profile, request, tip.
- Context swap: the same kind of tip shown in Slack (0:47), in Teams (1:12) and floating over an office (0:51), proof that it lives where people already work.
- Typing in one prompt field with a cursor (0:43): request appears, reply follows.
- Labels overlaid on live action: avatar pills next to the speaker (0:02), a card over a worker (0:51).
- Browser bar unfolds into a full page for the CTA (1:30–1:32).

## Motion and transitions
Hard cuts between presenter and B-roll; a radial bloom (0:18) and slides onto the orange stage for UI. Cards rise softly; quiz options tick in one by one. The presenter's own gestures (pointing, the blurred turn at 1:16) supply the energy. Carries: the orange field, and the presenter as anchor between sections.

## Pacing
Brisk, conversational voice (129 words/min) with rhetorical questions. Presenter shots hold 5–25 s; the UI section is the dense part, a new card every 3–4 s from 0:34 to 0:54. Breathes on the logo bloom and end card.

## Sound
Light upbeat bed near 89 BPM, -18.5 LUFS, under a dominant on-camera male voice talking to the lens, pitch-style. 93 sound events per minute: soft pops for tags and cards; the voice carries most of the energy.

## What to take from it
- 0:02: label pills popping beside the speaker exactly when the role is named.
- 0:35–0:47: the user journey as a chain of single UI cards, one every 3–4 s.
- 0:47 and 1:12: the same output in two host apps, proof of "works where you work".
- 1:30–1:32: a browser bar that unfolds into the full page with the cursor already on the CTA.

## Avoid
- Stock B-roll of smiling teams (0:11, 1:07, 1:24): generic.
- Presenter before a whiteboard of buzzwords: dated, and our film has no host.
- Radial orange bloom behind the logo mark (0:18): close to "title on a gradient".
