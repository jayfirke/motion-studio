# Munch (first draft): stock creators in viewfinder frames, skeptic-and-answer script

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sEp_fRv2Ow0 · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:52
- **Type:** explainer / ad · **Product:** AI clip extractor that turns long videos (podcasts, webinars, streams, sport) into trend-matched social clips
- **Numbers:** 18.1 transitions per min (41 detected) · median shot 2.2 s (0.07–13.8 s) · 144 words/min · ~112 BPM · 71 sound events/min · -21.5 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:10 Hook: a creator in a purple-lit room framed as a camera viewfinder (REC dot, 4K badge, running timecode) greets the viewer (0:02), then pulls a sceptical face when a second voice says he hates editing (0:07).
- 0:10–0:20 Name: dip to black, a white speed-line starburst, a grey ring irises open on a collage of floating thumbnails with the red logo tile among them (0:11–0:14).
- 0:16–0:28 How it works: floating search-query cards and a Trending tag (0:16); over-the-shoulder shot reveals the collage is the creator's monitor (~0:20); the "My Projects" grid (0:21); a podcast clip in a tilted player card (0:25); a clip tagged with Topics, Content and Themes pills (~0:28).
- 0:28–0:58 Inputs: rapid stock clips, each in a format frame: car selfie with a red record button (0:30), streamer with a progress bar (0:35), hockey in a player card (0:39), a podcaster whose viewfinder shrinks into a full YouTube page (0:49–0:58).
- 1:00–1:24 Features: smart crop as a red box tracking one speaker (1:03, 1:07); trim as a red bar under a clip (1:17); platform badges popping round a clip (1:21); five rapid "yes" cuts (1:26).
- 1:28–1:52 Close: logo letters build from red tiles (1:31), a second sceptic reacts (1:36–1:40), logo and URL (1:50).

## Visual style
Live-action stock footage dominates; graphics are a light layer on warm pale grey: white rounded cards, one raspberry-red accent, small sans, coloured pill tags. A fake camera HUD frames every talking head so stock reads as "raw footage waiting to be edited".

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Almost no real product: one project grid (0:21) and the logo.
- Edits drawn as one-colour overlays: crop box locked on a face (1:03), trim bar (1:17), topic pills (~0:28).
- Format change shown by the container: viewfinder → YouTube page → TikTok badge while the footage stays (0:49–0:58).
- No cursor, clicks or typing anywhere.

## Motion and transitions
Fast cutting joined by signature moves: dip to black plus starburst (0:10–0:14), a ring iris, an over-the-shoulder push that turns the collage into the creator's screen (~0:20, the best carry here), and the viewfinder shrinking into a web page (0:53). The "yes" run at 1:26 is a burst of hard cuts on each word.

## Pacing
144 words/min shared by two voices, a new clip every 2 s. The sceptic-line-then-answer structure gives a question beat and a payoff beat. Almost nothing holds over 4 s except the 13.8 s collage.

## Sound
Upbeat bed near 112 BPM, mixed low (-21.5 LUFS). Two voices: an enthusiastic host and an interrupting sceptic, jokey and conversational. 71 events/min for 41 cuts: sound sits on transitions, not UI.

## What to take from it
- ~0:20: the over-the-shoulder carry where a floating graphic collage turns out to be the screen someone is looking at.
- 0:49–0:58: keep the content, change the container (viewfinder shrinks into a web page).
- 1:03, 1:17: edits drawn as single-colour overlays (crop box tracking a face, trim bar); readable on a phone.
- 1:26: one word per hard cut, the burst-of-hits exception done right.

## Avoid
- Almost no real product UI: exactly the "not looking like a demo" complaint.
- Stock creators and staged sceptic reactions eat screen time the product should own.
- Speed-line starburst and ring iris: template wipes. Mix far under -14 LUFS.
