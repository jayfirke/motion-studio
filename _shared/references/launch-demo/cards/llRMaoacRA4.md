## Visual style
Two worlds: deep navy for brand and AI, airy pastel gradient for features. One glossy glass orb as the AI character. Geometric sans; two-tone headlines with key words in brand blue. White rounded cards with chat bubbles, avatars and status chips.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn but faithful fragments (chat, ticket, review, audit, insights), one card per feature rather than a full app.
- Fixed template: headline top, bullets left, card right; each bullet appears exactly when the card shows that capability.
- Messages pop in one by one with avatars; outcome chips ("paid", "Ticket", "Needs human help") slide in as the result.
- One cursor click in the film, on Send (0:35).
- Content swaps in place; the frame never resets.

## Motion and transitions
The orb carries: it wipes contents (0:25), blooms into white to change worlds (0:13) and ends inside the CTA button. Features keep the layout and swap content, so the eye stays parked. Headlines type or scramble in. Soft, quick easing; small overshoot on chips.

## Pacing
Sparse VO (85 words/min, 82 words in all): the screen carries the detail, the voice only the headline. Each feature gets 6–8 s, five in 35 s. Bullets land every 1–2 s, busy without talk.

## Sound
Light electronic bed near 129 BPM at -15.9 LUFS. Calm female narrator, one short sentence per feature. About 120 sound events per minute: soft pops on each bullet, message and chip, whooshes on orb moves; these small hits drive the rhythm.

## Avoid
- Glossy glowing orb is close to the glow ban; keep any carrier matte.
- Five features on one template feel like slides; vary camera or shot length.
- One real click in 58 s; the UI mostly animates itself.
