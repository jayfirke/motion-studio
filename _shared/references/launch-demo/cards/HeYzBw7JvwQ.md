## Visual style
White canvas with violet-blue blur blobs at the edges, real product screens as rounded white cards with feather shadows. Third-party apps keep their own colours, so the integration story reads without labels. The UI carries all the words; there are no headline cards except the logo.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
Real UI captured from the product:
- floating in z-depth with real defocus on the far layers (0:10, 0:39);
- two cursor styles: a soft white circle for taps (0:36) and a classic arrow for the publish click (0:59);
- the click leads straight into a push-in on the result (insight card → chart → table, 0:36–0:45);
- a spotlight by dimming, where everything else greys and one card rises with its value tag (0:56). There's no glow, just contrast;
- integrations shown by the partner's real logo flying into the product's board (0:48).

## Motion and transitions
Only four detected cuts in 69 s; the first 26 s are one continuous move. The camera pushes through layers instead of cutting, with defocus rising and falling as planes pass. Easing is slow and floaty. The hard cut to the release note (0:57) is the final punctuation.

## Pacing
Sparse narration: 90 words/min and long music-led stretches (0:07–0:15 and 0:33–0:46 have few words). Shots hold about 12 s on average while the camera keeps moving. The rhythm comes from the camera, not from the edit.

## Sound
A light, bright electronic bed (~123 BPM) carries most of the film, under a narrator with short punchy lines and one casual reaction aside. The mix is quiet at −21.3 LUFS. 109 onsets/min suggests soft whooshes and ticks on card arrivals, but that's inferred from the numbers, not heard.

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:05: the problem as a chorus. A face ringed by real-channel messages and badged app icons tells the pain in one frame.
- 0:10–0:13: app icons in deep z-space funnelling toward one inbox counter, the aggregation shown as motion.
- 0:36–0:45: tap → dive. The circle cursor taps "Analyse further", and the camera pushes through the card into the chart, then the table. The click causes the camera move.
- 0:56: the dim-and-lift spotlight, one card raised with its number while everything else greys.
- 0:59: the last product action is a real publish click, which closes the loop the hook opened.

## Avoid
- The AI lightbulb over plexus network lines (0:33), a stock cliché.
- Sparse 90 words/min narration and long music-only stretches, which is exactly the "slow" the owner rejected.
- The −21 LUFS mix.
- Most UI text is too small to read at phone size.
