## Visual style
Violet-on-black 2.5D with soft bokeh; warm natural-light footage only at the open. The UI is white cards with violet pills and small green and orange chips. Results get a big white modal with a large tick and two numbers. A small rounded sans; most text lives inside the UI.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Redrawn UI** on frameless phone screens and desktop windows, sometimes side by side so the same record shows on both (1:08).
- **Pointer = arrow plus person:** an arrow cursor with an avatar badge and role label ("Staff", "Manager"). It taps a field and the field fills (0:53.6).
- **Zoom-to-card:** a push-in until one list item fills the frame (1:19).
- **Outcome cards:** each flow ends on a white "Completed" modal with timing numbers.
- **System link:** a room card with partner-system bubbles around it shows the PMS sync (0:52).

## Motion and transitions
- Violet panel wipes carry live action into the brand world (0:12.8).
- Inside the UI, transitions are camera pushes and pans across one big canvas (longest hold 20.8 s).
- Elements rise with short eased slides; badges pop with slight scale.
- **Anchor screen:** the same phone order screen returns at 0:41, 0:59 and 1:13 in a new state each time.

## Pacing
Steady at 111 words/min: about 5 s and one screen state per sentence, with no long pauses. The stock ending (1:35–2:05) slows the last third.

## Sound
- A ~99 BPM light corporate bed that swells where the VO pauses (0:24, 1:05).
- Moderate SFX (78/min): soft taps and pops.
- A warm, benefit-first hospitality script.
- A quiet master (-20.5 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- **0:46 and 1:30:** each feature ends on an outcome card with a tick and two hard numbers, a result rather than just a screen.
- **0:53.6:** an arrow cursor carrying a role avatar, so we know who clicks.
- **0:41 → 0:59 → 1:13:** one recurring screen that changes state each time. Continuity makes the walkthrough one story.
- **1:08:** desktop and phone side by side, showing that the same record syncs.
- **0:12.8:** colour panel wipes as the single bridge from footage into UI.

## Avoid
- A closing stack copy-pasted from the sister film (against "no repeated shots").
- Stuttered stock footage, and avatar faces orbiting a phone (0:25).
