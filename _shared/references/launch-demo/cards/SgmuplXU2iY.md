## Structure (beats with times)

- 0:08–0:10 Wow: a fly-through of a 3D tunnel whose walls are phones and tablets showing books and video. Two words wait at the vanishing point, then a device whips out on a blue trail.

- 0:16–0:18 Product: the card unfolds into the real Library (thumbnails with language flags). The glove taps a lecture.
- 0:19–0:21 Feature: the thumbnails detach and scatter in 3D around "Multiple Languages", each with a different flag.
- 0:21–0:24 Publish: the list view tilts in, one row lifts out and magnifies, then becomes a "Distribute To Youtube" button under the glove.
- 0:25–0:29 Summary: a blue sparkle leads a three-verb line built a word at a time.
- 0:30–0:33 CTA: logo, URL typing on, about a 3 s hold.

- Glass icons, light-mode real UI with large radii, soft shadows.

- **Row lift-out:** one list row detaches and magnifies to become the subject (0:23).
- **Object morph chain:** bar → dot → card → full UI (0:12–0:16), with nothing cut.
