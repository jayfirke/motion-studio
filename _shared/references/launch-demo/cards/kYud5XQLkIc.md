## Visual style
Luxury restraint: cream and warm charcoal, one muted gold accent, lots of empty space. Always two text levels: a tiny letter-spaced caps eyebrow, then a light sans line with one or two words in italic. Photos and UI sit in soft blobs, circles and squircles. Fits the jewellery brand with no sparkle effects.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real web UI (ring builder, product page, dashboard), flat and frontal, entering by rising from the bottom edge of the frame.
- One cursor click on a diamond-shape option in the builder (0:31); otherwise screens are browsed by drift and crop.
- UI sits beside people: a client avatar bubble next to the builder (0:31), a person at a monitor before the dashboard (1:05 → 1:13).
- No zoom callouts or highlights; the copy line next to the UI does the explaining.

## Motion and transitions
Slow and graceful: 10 cuts; most changes are masked reveals inside one shot (up to 19.6 s). Carries: a self-drawing gold ellipse (0:08), a wavy lens that changes what is inside it (0:35), panels sliding apart (0:52), UI rising from below, a word typing on (1:25). Soft ease-in-out; motion is felt more than seen.

## Pacing
Unhurried by design for a booth loop: each line holds 4–6 s so passers-by can read it. With no voice, all information is in two-line captions, so it explains less than a narrated film.

## Sound
No speech. A quiet, gentle bed (~103 BPM) at -27.9 LUFS; the 122 events/min are likely soft percussion, as the picture is not hit-synced. Sound is optional on a show floor.

## What to take from it
- Two-level copy: letter-spaced eyebrow + light line with one italic word (0:27, 0:56); premium and readable.
- Wavy outline lens that sweeps across and reveals the next line inside it (0:35).
- UI that rises from the bottom edge as if pushed up into frame (0:24, 0:41).
- Person-at-screen photo that hands off to the same screen's real UI (1:05 → 1:13).
- A word typing on in large type as a quiet result reveal (1:25).

## Avoid
- Too slow and too quiet for an online launch film: no VO, one click in 100 s.
- Stock photos of people standing in for the product.
- Mask shapes in every shot (blobs, circles, squircles) dilute the system.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Previs Studio tour: an outline lens sweeps over the page and reveals option B inside it while A stays outside.
2. Setup chapter: the terminal rises from the bottom edge and the first command types on in large type.
3. Results chapter: eyebrow + one-line copy with a single italic word for each verified number (LUFS, scores).
