## Visual style
The product's own light UI: warm off-white page, white cards with soft shadows, small pastel icons, dark navy pills. Rounded, friendly sans. Nothing is drawn on top of the UI except scale-ups and highlights.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI, full-bleed, no device frame for most of the film; at 0:56 the camera pulls back to reveal the whole app shell, at 1:09 the shell tilts slightly in 3D.
- Arrow cursor clicks a card and the card fills with a pastel tint before the page changes (0:10, 0:23): the selection state is the click feedback.
- Camera push-ins on the card the VO is naming, then a pull back to home: the home screen is the hub the film returns to three times (0:06, 0:35, 0:56).
- Emphasis by scale: the pill being named grows while its neighbours stay (0:44, 0:48).
- An email row lifts out of the inbox list as a floating card (1:17), a neat way to point at one line in a busy screen.

## Motion and transitions
One 50 s continuous UI shot from 0:27 to 1:17: pages change in place, the camera pushes and pulls, nothing cuts. Hard cuts appear only when leaving the UI for stock footage. Moves are smooth ease-in-out with no overshoot. The home screen acts as the carry: every section starts and ends there.

## Pacing
VO at 117 words/min, one feature per sentence, short imperative lines ("pick a category and dive in"). A new UI state every 3–4 s; the screen holds while the line finishes. It breathes at the logo (0:00–0:06) and the end card.

## Sound
Light bed about 118 BPM, -15.5 LUFS. Warm, instructional, second-person narration. 152 sound events/min means soft clicks and swishes sit on most cursor actions and push-ins. Every sentence names exactly what is on screen.

## What to take from it
- 0:10 and 0:23: the cursor clicks a card and it fills with a soft colour tint before the push-in; clean click feedback with no ripple.
- 0:44–0:48: the named option scales up while its siblings stay: VO and picture point at the same thing.
- 0:56: pull back from a close-up to reveal the full app shell, then use that wide shot to tour the sidebar (1:09).
- 1:17: lift one row out of a dense list so it floats above the page; a clear "look here" without arrows.

## Avoid
- Stock footage of people with a phone and laptop (1:17–1:34) and the waving mascot (1:34): breaks the UI world and feels generic.
- Long reads of article text (0:27–0:31): a page of body copy is never legible on a phone.
- A gentle 117 words/min is still below the owner's "dense" bar; push faster for a launch film.
