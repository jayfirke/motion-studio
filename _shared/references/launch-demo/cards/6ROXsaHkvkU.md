## Visual style
UI-centric 2.5D. Two worlds alternate: near-black with a green radial glow, and white-to-mint gradient. One accent green for glow, pills and progress. Geometric sans, one headline word tinted green. Real photography (showroom, aerial) grounds the story twice.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real-looking high-fidelity UI (dashboard, profile, tables), shown as floating cards and devices without bezels.
- Push-through: the kiosk screen becomes the frame (0:29–0:34), so device to full-screen UI has no cut.
- Hero element isolation: a single avatar or stat card is lifted out of the screen and floated in space while the rest blurs (0:42–0:46).
- Stepper and table rows highlight in sequence; a small green triangle pointer and the round logo button act as the "cursor" (0:56, 1:10).
- Phones and screens tilted around 20–30° with a soft green rim light, stacked in depth (0:58–1:06).

## Motion and transitions
Only 11 cuts in 96 s: shots carry via pushes into a screen, a card pulled forward out of the layout, and gradient wipes (0:33, 1:25). The opening journey line carries the pills. Smooth ease-in-out, no bounce; pace comes from constant camera drift, not cutting.

## Pacing
Medium-dense: 130 words/min, one product action per sentence, new UI state about every 3–4 s inside long continuous shots. Breathes at the logo reveal (0:18) and the drone shot (1:16).

## Sound
Steady music bed at ~103 BPM, -15.7 LUFS. One narrator at 130 words/min (voice timbre not checked: the study reads numbers and captions, not the audio itself). Script runs pain → "introducing" → step-by-step customer journey → CTA. 99 sound events/min is a busy layer for a voiced film; given the picture, it most likely holds small hits on each pill and card and whooshes on the pushes.

## What to take from it
- 0:06–0:12: a glowing path that carries labelled pills across the frame; it explains a workflow before any UI appears.
- 0:29–0:34: push the camera into a device screen until its message fills the frame; device to UI with no cut.
- 0:42–0:46: lift one element (avatar, KPI card) out of the UI and float it in depth to make it the subject.
- 0:58–1:06: dark stage, tilted screens with a single-colour rim light; premium without extra decoration.
- Two-world palette (dark glow vs mint white) alternating by chapter for rhythm.

## Avoid
- The heart-shaped photo mask (0:02): cute, off-tone for us.
- Avatar ring around a slogan on the end (1:30): stock explainer cliché.
- Too many tiny SFX at 99 per minute would read busy in our mix; keep fewer sounds than beats.
- Green glow on UI chrome breaks our "no glow on chrome" rule; light the stage instead.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- Problems or setup chapter: one path line that travels hook → previs → build → critique → render, dropping a chip at each station, then the camera follows it into the first real screen.
- Previs Studio tour: push into the previs page until an option card fills the frame, then lift that card forward out of the layout for its VO line.
- Alternate dark-stage and light-page chapters so the 12 chapters read as distinct beats.
