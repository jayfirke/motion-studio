## Visual style
Hybrid of stock footage and soft glassmorphism. Problem act is tinted pink-red with blurred photo backgrounds; solution act flips to sky blue and white. Footage sits in rounded cards with soft shadows; labels are frosted glass pills with an icon chip. Clean geometric sans, one size per line, sentence case. The colour switch from red to blue marks problem → solution.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Very little product: one chat screen (0:51) and one program card (1:11), both redrawn and shown for 3–5 s.
- Cursor appears on the chat screen and on the end-card button (1:55); no visible click effect on the frames sampled.
- No typing, no navigation, no zoom-ins on UI. The product is described rather than demonstrated.

## Motion and transitions
Footage-to-card shrink is the main carry: a full-frame clip scales down into a rounded card and others slide in around it (0:05–0:07). Pills stagger in diagonally; the camera drifts across 3D logo cards; the logo scales down from oversized (0:43). Soft ease-out, nothing snaps.

## Pacing
Dense voice (134 words/min) over slower picture: shots hold about 6.6 s and most of the motion is drift. The problem act is the richest visually (bubbles, calendar, faces); the middle explains in abstractions (pills, rays) and loses energy.

## Sound
Mid-tempo corporate pop bed (~112 BPM), quiet master at -21.5 LUFS. Narration runs almost continuously; the script is a punchy problem list in short fragments, then benefits. SFX density (92 events/min) suggests soft whooshes and pops on pills, not verified by ear.

## What to take from it
- 0:05–0:07: full-frame footage shrinks into a rounded card that joins a stack; a clean way to go from "world" to "object" without a cut.
- 0:17: a calendar fills with named chat bubbles piling up; overload shown as UI, not as a sad face.
- 0:43–0:46: the colour of the whole film flips from red to blue at the reveal, and the oversized wordmark settles to its resting size.
- 1:40: outcome pills tick green in sequence over a blurred background.

## Avoid
- Stock footage of meetings and pensive men: generic and unrelated to any real product.
- Abstract light rays and orbiting icon circles (1:01–1:06) to explain how it works.
- Almost no product demo: exactly the "not looking like a demo" problem the owner flagged.
- Logo walls and a "30 years" stat bubble as proof.
