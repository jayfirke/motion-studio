## Visual style
A light world (white and ice-blue gradients, frosted cards, thin grey line art) with one green "solved" accent and red for faults. Footage is cinematic and warm, alternating with the graphic world. The UI is an airy window with traffic-light dots. An agency badge sits top-left throughout.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Rebuilt UI in a floating window** (macOS dots, no browser bar) with soft blue shadows.
- **The query types into the search field** beside a pink arrow cursor; the answer panel swings in with a 3D tilt and settles flat.
- **Provenance chips:** coloured chips under the answer show which sources fed it.
- **Screen tracking in live action:** a green stroke ties the real tablet to the product (0:24).

## What to take from it
- **0:00–0:04:** one subject goes live → render → line drawing.
- **0:14–0:21:** a clock fills from yellow to green on a still stage as fault pins resolve into ticks. Waiting becomes solved.
- **0:34–0:37:** source chips pop under the AI's answer, showing where it came from.
- **0:29–0:31:** a typed query, then an answer panel tilts in and lands flat.
- **0:39:** a one-word question card as the pivot into proof.

## Avoid
- A persistent corner watermark.
- Frosted benefit pills, and an aerial with floating labels.
- Glowing road light-lines.
- A long logo build (dead time).
