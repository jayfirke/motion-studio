## Visual style
- Dark Apple-keynote restraint: one near-black teal background with light-mode white cards floating on it.
- One accent, green (checks, nodes, scan line). Red only for redlines.
- One grotesk in two weights. Headlines at about 6% of frame height, left of the UI: one headline, one card.
- Cards fade out at their bottom edge into the dark, as if rising from the floor. Secondary cards get soft blur.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real screens (playbook, settings, Contracts table, Timeline), one panel at a time, scaled large.
- A small black arrow cursor clicks dropdowns, toggles and timeline rows (0:30, 0:41, 0:48–0:51). Every click changes the same card.
- **The Timeline is the hero component:** it returns three times (0:48, 1:09, 1:13) and grows each time.
- Status is shown by badges popping onto a card's corner, not by new screens. A scan line reads as "AI analysing" (0:58).

## Motion and transitions
- 14 cuts, but mostly soft: cards push in from the edges, scale up from blurred placeholders, or rise through the floor fade.
- Carries: contract becomes redline doc, the ring grows into the orbit, and one Revised MSA card holds while its badges change.
- Type animates per word or letter, never as a block fade. Fast ease-out, no overshoot, steady camera.

## Pacing
- Dense and even: a new state every 3–4 s, 127 words/min, 1.5 s shots in the email cascade.
- Breaths at the wordmark (0:19), the clock (0:55) and the end card, about 2 s each.

## Sound
- A bed at about 136 BPM that swells between lines and ducks under the voice. −15.8 LUFS, near target.
- Clean, confident narration with one idea per sentence. It ends on "legal at the speed of business".
- Sparse SFX (103 events per minute): likely ticks on clicks and soft swooshes on card moves.

## What to take from it
- **A grey-to-white per-word highlight** (0:00, 0:23). Type keeps pace with the voice, no fade.
- **One hero component that grows across the film** (Timeline 0:48 → 1:09 → 1:13). Progress as a visual thread.
- **Badges popping onto a persistent card** (0:57–1:06). Three beats on one object.
- **Panels rising out of a dark floor fade** (throughout). Depth without borders or glow.

## Avoid
- The clock dial as "time passes" (0:12, 0:55). Stock.
- The radial AI-hub diagram (0:34). Generic.
