## Visual style
Stock footage and big type. Purple-to-orange gradients and a deep navy ground, one orange accent (a dot, an x chip, a circle) that repeats. A bold heavy sans, centred or tracked over footage. The only product-like images are the BI-tool logos and the gauge.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
There is no product UI. The product is argued with words, numbers and logos: a gauge with one huge figure (0:31.9), a ring of partner tool logos around the mark (0:37.0), cost icons (0:39.5). If this film wanted to show a product, it would be a slideshow of claims.

## Motion and transitions
Fast and varied, and the most carry-driven of this batch:
- footage seen through the letters, then growing to full frame (12.5 s to 0:14);
- the full frame closing to a circle in neon rings, with an orange square as the next target (14.8 s);
- type that drifts and replaces itself in place ("SaaS" then "SaaS Mode" at 26.2 s) over footage;
- an orange dot that returns on "Analyze" (0:29.3) and "The Rest" (0:57.4).
Light streaks, particle rings and flare transitions sit next to these and are the weak part (0:06.4, 0:19.1).

## Pacing
Very dense: median shot 1.47 s, a new idea every 2–3 s, no breathing room except the blank gradient frames (0:21.7, 0:49.7). Short claims with a verb, one per beat.

## Sound
No voice. Loud, energetic electronic bed at about 152 BPM (-11.0 LUFS, loudest in this batch) and 167.6 events per min. The frames suggest a whoosh on each type move, a hit on the finger-press (0:06.4) and a riser into the logo (0:15–0:16). Inferred, not heard.

## What to take from it
- Footage or UI seen through the type, then the type steps aside and the picture grows to full frame (12.5 s to 0:14).
- One accent object that returns, an orange dot or chip, so the viewer knows what to watch (0:29.3, 0:57.4).
- A hub with partner logos orbiting the product mark: "works with any tool" in one frame (0:37.0).
- One large number with a tiny unit, as a gauge, for a headline result (0:31.9).
- A last line split across two beats, "Do what you do best" then "the rest" (0:49.7, 0:57.4).

## Avoid
- The finger press with a particle burst (0:06.4), light streaks (0:19.1) and flare transitions: particles, glow and light leaks.
- Centred titles on a purple-orange gradient (0:21.7, 0:49.7): a centred title on a gradient.
- Stock-footage cuts that stand in for the product.
- A master at -11 LUFS: ours is -14 LUFS with -1 dBTP.
