# The Product Launch Video That Ticked All the Boxes

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=du0qhu4Ecck · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:36
- **Type:** launch ad (short, narrated, footage + real UI) · **Product:** affable.ai, an influencer-marketing platform (discovery, campaigns, analytics)
- **Numbers:** 25.2 transitions per min · median shot 2.04 s (0.17–5.7 s) · 140 words/min · ~63 BPM (likely half-time of ~126) · 89.2 sound events/min · -17.6 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:04 **Hook (stat):** stock creator filming yoga with a ring light (0:00); the shot shrinks into a phone frame as an orange/teal diagonal split slides over it and a big "6x ROI" sets beside the phone, hearts popping (0:01–0:04).
- 0:04–0:08 **Problem (scale):** an orange card counts up to a 30-million influencer figure in front of a mosaic of thousands of tiny profile thumbnails, coloured squares twinkling across it (0:04–0:07).
- 0:08–0:11 **Context:** creator at a laptop as DM bubbles pop around her (0:08–0:10).
- 0:11–0:23 **Solution, four features in 12 s:** discovery screen with the cursor toggling a filter (0:11); social-network icons pop around the same screen (0:13–0:14); campaign chat (0:16) and a Create Campaign modal (0:17); a motion-blur whip to analytics on a magenta slab with a pie, rankings and hashtags (0:18–0:20); top branded posts (0:22).
- 0:23–0:27 **Benefits:** three round badges pop one by one on a teal/peach diagonal: Easy to use, AI Powered, Affordable (0:23–0:26).
- 0:27–0:30 **Goodbye / hello:** a messy spreadsheet fills the frame (0:27); the cursor grabs it and it slides away (0:29).
- 0:30–0:36 **CTA:** happy user at a laptop (0:30) fades back as two pill buttons form (0:32); logo, then the cursor clicks the Get-a-Demo pill (0:34–0:35).

## Visual style
Warm peach base with bold teal, orange and magenta slabs cut on diagonals. Rounded geometric sans, big and white on colour for stats, otherwise text lives in the UI. Real UI windows sit on the colour slabs with soft shadows. Stock lifestyle footage frames the start and end.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real web-app screens, frontal, sliding or whipping in on coloured slabs.
- Cursor acts in most UI shots: toggles a filter (0:11), drags the spreadsheet out (0:29), clicks the CTA pill (0:35).
- Platform icons pop around the window to signal it covers every network (0:13).
- No zooms into detail; screens are shown whole, so text is small.

## Motion and transitions
Fast and varied for a 36 s spot: footage-to-phone shrink (0:01), diagonal slab wipes, a counter animation on the stat card, a motion-blur whip (0:18), badge pops on an overshoot ease. Shots average 2 s; the stat card (4 s) and the feature run are the only holds.

## Pacing
Very dense: 140 wpm VO with every sentence carrying a new visual. Hook to CTA in 36 s with no rest, so features get barely 3 s each.

## Sound
Bright pop bed with a slow-feeling pulse (~63 BPM measured, probably felt at double time). About 89 events/min: pops on badges and icons, a whoosh on the whip, a click on the CTA. Upbeat, quick narration.

## What to take from it
- Live footage shrinking into a phone frame beside a big stat (0:01): a carry from world to product.
- Stat card counting up in front of a mosaic of thousands of tiny items (0:04–0:07): scale made visible.
- Cursor grabbing the old tool (a spreadsheet) and sliding it out of frame (0:29) as the "goodbye" beat.
- Final beat is a real click on the CTA pill (0:35).

## Avoid
- Twinkling coloured squares and heart pops: decoration with no job.
- Motion-blur whip (0:18); stock lifestyle footage.
- Whole screens at small size with no zoom: unreadable on a phone.
- Overshoot on badge pops is fine for UI, but three in a row feels templated.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Results chapter: a counter ticks up over a mosaic of every frame of the rendered film, the scale of the work in one shot.
2. Problems → setup: the cursor drags the old messy shot-list spreadsheet off screen and the previs page is underneath.
3. CTA: end on the cursor clicking the real call-to-action button, not on a static logo.
