## Visual style
White and pale blue with soft blue glows and small dotted marks. Flat line-art characters in pink-red, pastel icons in rounded squares, a multicolour asterisk logo. Real UI on white rounded cards with lots of whitespace. Small clean sans labels; type is never the hero.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real web UI in a rounded browser card or as bare cards; no device, no tilt.
- A filled blue arrow cursor drives the key actions: Connect to CRM opens a dropdown (1:00), Send Funds (1:20), hoodie swatches (0:47), the URL pill (1:37).
- The browser shrinks to just its search bar, then reopens as the next page (0:30): a container morph instead of a cut.
- Pages pan and zoom to the relevant area; the catalog blurs to make room for logos, then sharpens.

## Motion and transitions
One continuous canvas: 8 detected cuts in 99 s, one 39 s stretch with none (0:51–1:30). Everything arrives by camera push, slide, rise or morph. Soft, even ease-in-out.

## Pacing
Steady: 116 words/min, a new element every 3–5 s, the camera always gliding. Continuous rather than punchy; no burst, the only rest is the logo reveal.

## Sound
Bright, upbeat bed under one narrator with a plain brochure script. 129 events/min with almost no cuts suggests pops on icons and pins plus clicks on cursor presses (not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- One continuous canvas: 39 s without a cut (0:51–1:30), every change a camera move or morph.
- Browser collapses into its search bar and reopens as the next page (0:30).
- Cursor clicks Connect to CRM and a dropdown opens with the choices (1:00): an integration shown as an action.
- Logos rush past the camera around a blurred grid (0:35–0:39): depth instead of a static wall.
- Bookend: the stressed opener character returns calm at the CTA (1:33).

## Avoid
- Wordmark built by fading letters (0:14): opacity-only entrance.
- Orbit diagrams of labelled icons (0:19, 0:23) and dotted decoration.
- Flat corporate characters with flag badges (1:12): dated.
- Static logo wall (1:29).
- Even, gliding pace with no hits: smooth but low energy.
