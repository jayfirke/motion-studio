## Visual style
- Dark sci-fi: near-black navy with soft teal and rust bokeh.
- Neon line icons with multicolour nodes, plus glossy 3D primitives.
- The product's own dark theme (navy panels, teal accents, gradient module cards) matches the film's world.
- Almost no on-screen type. The logo is chunky, extruded and rim-lit.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI in borderless dark windows on the same dark stage, so the screens feel native to the film.
- **Big custom cursor:** a teal triangle about 5 % of frame height. The item it lands on gets a teal bar (0:30).
- **Perspective glides:** forms and tables lean back about 20° and the camera pans diagonally across them (0:46, 0:59, 1:03), reading a long form in one move.
- **Feedback states:** a green Success toast (1:03) and a confirmation modal rising from the bottom (1:19) prove the action finished.
- No typing. The values are preset.

## Motion and transitions
- Two hero fly-throughs (the ring tunnel and the logo lens) bracket the film; everything between glides.
- Real carry: line icons draw on, then become the next shape (waves curve into an orbit, 0:38–0:42).
- The windows slide and tilt on smooth ease-in-out with no bounce. The bokeh never breaks, so the cuts feel seamless.

## Pacing
- Slow and atmospheric: 88 wpm, and the VO often waits for the picture.
- Screens hold about 4 s with a constant drift.
- No workflow is ever followed from start to finish, so it feels cinematic, not like a demo.

## Sound
- A ~108 BPM ambient electronic track with pads. 113 events per minute, sparse for this batch.
- -22 LUFS: about 8 dB short of our target.
- Benefit-led, abstract script bookended by "what if" questions. It ends on "one platform infinite potential". The frames can't confirm the voice's gender.
- The fly-throughs probably carry whoosh or riser hits.

## What to take from it
- **0:09 ring-tunnel push:** a single star technique, a fast dolly through rings into the hero object.
- **0:38–0:42 lines that become the next shape:** carry instead of a cut.
- **0:46 / 0:59 tilted-form glide:** a long settings form read in one diagonal pass.
- **1:03 Success toast:** show the result state, not only the button.
- The UI's theme matches the film's background, so it feels like one world.

## Avoid
- Sci-fi metaphors (light bulb, hexagram, glowing chip) that say nothing about the product.
- 60 s of drift with no cause-and-effect clicks.
- Bokeh glow everywhere and a neon extruded logo (close to our glow and light-leak bans).
- A -22 LUFS master.
