## Visual style
Black, white and one electric cyan. Heavy geometric all-caps sans for slams, medium weight for headlines; the cyan word carries the meaning. Black/white splits and cyan discs are the only shapes. Stock clips are cool and desaturated to match.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Phone mockups shown frontal and still, screen swapped per feature, a cyan disc behind as a halo; headline on the left, phone on the right, always the same layout.
- UI composited into live action twice: a hand-held phone (0:57) and an office monitor (1:11), which links people to product.
- Desktop gets a real cursor: it travels to the map, a route tooltip pops and a detail button appears (1:16–1:20). This is the only interaction in the film.
- No typing, no click feedback on mobile; the screens just change.

## Motion and transitions
Snappy cuts on the beat with masks: circle wipes from footage into graphics (0:59), black/white panels sliding apart to reveal the next word (0:20, 1:34), the cyan disc scaling up to fill the frame before AUTOMATION (0:25). The strike-through draws across words in real time (1:39). Type pops in word by word with a hard ease-out; nothing floats.

## Pacing
Very fast in the hook (shots down to 0.07 s), then a steady 2–4 s rhythm for each app feature, with the longest hold (15 s) on the phone feature run where only the screen changes. Copy is short: two to five words per card.

## Sound
Music-only event edit: driving electronic track (~112 BPM), heavy percussion (auto-captions mislabel the hits as applause). 214 events/min: picture cut to the drums; no UI foley evident. Built for a booth screen where sound is optional.

## What to take from it
- Strike-through that draws across the words being removed (1:39): a kinetic-type move that carries meaning.
- Icon chain on a dotted line that grows from three to six nodes to map a messy problem (0:11–0:20).
- Real cursor on the desktop dashboard opening a route popup (1:16–1:20).
- UI composited into a hand-held phone and a monitor to tie product to people (0:57, 1:11).
- One-accent discipline: a single cyan word per headline.

## Avoid
- Seven-cut stutter on CHAOS (5.5–6.4 s) is a glitch-flash; one hit is enough.
- Static phone mockups with swapped screens: reads as slides, not a demo.
- No voice: viewers get slogans, not understanding (the owner's main complaint with our draft).
- Stock lifestyle footage.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Problems chapter: draw a dotted chain of the old pain points (brief, assets, sound, critique) and strike it through as the hand-off chapter removes them.
2. Hand-off chapter: cursor drives the real Previs page and a tooltip opens on a timeline marker, as on the ops map.
3. Keep one accent colour and let only the key word in each line wear it.
