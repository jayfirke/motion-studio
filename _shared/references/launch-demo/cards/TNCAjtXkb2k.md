## Visual style
Saturated magenta-to-violet-to-blue blurred gradient behind white UI cards. Headlines in a thin geometric sans with one bold word, small and left- or right-aligned. Brand mark is a retro black disc with serif lettering, the only bold graphic. The AI-generated portraits supply all the colour inside the UI.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI panels, cropped to the part that matters (seed field, prompt, model list), floating flat or tilted 20–30° in 3D.
- Multiplayer cursors: each arrow carries a round avatar of the teammate using it (0:17, 0:25, 0:35), which sells collaboration in one image.
- Typing and pressing are shown: prompt text in fields, the cursor clicking a plus button and Create (0:35–0:41); dropdowns open (0:46).
- Tabs change by sliding the whole panel sideways in 3D (0:48–0:51).

## Motion and transitions
Mostly continuous drifts (one 26 s shot from 0:27 to 0:53), with panels tilting in and out of depth. Strong carries: clutter pile → scatter → logo (0:00–0:14), logo disc → iris to end card (0:57). Headlines type on letter by letter. Ease is smooth, with some motion-blur on fast tilts.

## Pacing
Brisk for 63 s: four feature chapters, each opened by a typed headline and closed in under 12 s. VO at 109 wpm is clear with small gaps between sections; new UI state every 2–3 s.

## Sound
Mid-tempo electronic bed (~92 BPM) with lots of small transients (162 events/min): likely clicks on cursor presses, ticks on typing and swooshes on tilts. Clear, neutral narration in short declarative lines.

## What to take from it
- Problem as accumulating clutter: tool windows pile up until warnings appear, then scatter into depth to clear the stage (0:00–0:13).
- Multiplayer cursors with avatar badges (0:17–0:38): a strong way to show who is acting.
- Cropping the UI down to the one field that matters, then tilting it in 3D while the cursor acts (0:35, 0:38).
- Tabs changed by sliding the panel sideways (0:48–0:51).
- Logo disc that grows as an iris into the end card (0:57).

## Avoid
- The saturated gradient background behind everything; close to the banned gradient look.
- Thin small headlines: hard to read at 360 px.
- Motion blur on fast tilts, and the clock-hand wipe through text (0:33), which feels gimmicky.
