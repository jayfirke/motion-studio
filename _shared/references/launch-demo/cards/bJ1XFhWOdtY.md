# Dynamic Promo Video Typography Explainer for Digital Marketing Software / Animated Video

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJ1XFhWOdtY · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:34
- **Type:** explainer (kinetic type + real UGC footage + UI demo) · **Product:** Odore, a platform to find a brand's fans, invite them to content "missions" and reward them
- **Numbers:** 18.5 transitions per min · median shot 0.8 s (max 20.3) · 139 words/min · ~108 BPM · 200 sound events/min · -15.5 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:13):** "Are you ignoring the untapped potential" in kinetic type, with key words in violet and ghost echoes behind (0:02). Then real creator clips: TikTok phone frames with comment cards and outlined quote text (0:04–0:10). About 20 hard cuts in 3 s (0:09.8–0:12.9) stack phone cards into a growing collage.
- **Argument (0:13–0:30):** bold subtitle lines over UGC ("If you invite people in..", with a source credit). A framed expert talking head has huge outline words scrolling behind him as a marquee (0:22–0:25). Then "Engaging" letter-spaced (0:29).
- **Reveal (0:33):** a full violet frame and the logo.
- **Demo (0:37–0:53):**
  - **Find:** an Instagram post with metric chips ("Mentioned your brand 5 times", 18.27 % engagement) flowing into a "Your Brand" pill (0:37), then an audience breakdown (0:41).
  - **Invite:** a cursor clicks "Invite" over a blurred creator (0:45).
  - **Missions:** the cursor clicks "Launch", a mission preview card appears and a paper plane flies off (0:49). Mission briefs then pair with creator photos (0:53).
- **Proof (0:57–1:05):** a wall of UGC phones under "Network of Dedicated Fans"; EMV and engagement cards land on it, then a dashboard.
- **Community (1:09–1:24):** mission → complete → reward rows, an avatar network, direct-chat bubbles on creator video, and fans' names as pills.
- **Close (1:28–1:34):** a violet circle wipe, then the logo and URL.

## Visual style
A lavender-white canvas with one violet accent, and real vertical creator video as the colour source. Type carries the film: a tracked geometric sans, outlined stroke quotes, ghost echoes, giant marquee lines. UI cards are white with soft shadows, layered in shallow depth.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Simplified UI cards over real social content**, so the product always works on real posts.
- **A black arrow cursor clicks named pills** ("Invite", "Launch"), and each click gets a payoff graphic (the paper plane, 0:49).
- **Metric chips** slide out of the content they describe and land on the brand pill (0:37).
- Dashboards are brief (2–4 s), with a few big numbers.

## Motion and transitions
- **A burst montage** of creator clips on the beat (0:09.8–0:12.9): phones pop, stack, then blur into a wall.
- **Elsewhere, carry dominates:** the UGC wall stays while metric cards land on it (0:57–1:01), and type sits over footage instead of cutting away.
- Per-letter type animation with fast eases and outline-to-fill switches.

## Pacing
Very dense: 139 words/min, a 0.8 s median shot and 200 sound events/min. Real creator snippets make the first 13 s feel like a feed. It breathes on the full-violet logo (0:33) and on long dashboard holds.

## Sound
- A ~108 BPM pop-electronic bed.
- Extremely busy SFX: whooshes on type, pops on cards.
- The opening mixes real creator audio before an energetic narrator takes over.
- -15.5 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **0:09.8–0:12.9:** a burst of on-beat hard cuts that builds a collage. Energy, not chaos.
- **0:37:** data chips slide from the post into the brand pill, so the product logic shows as motion.
- **0:49:** a click with a small consequence animating out of the button.
- **0:22–0:25:** giant marquee type behind a framed talking head, adding depth.
- **0:57–1:01:** proof numbers laid over the evidence wall.

## Avoid
- Ghost echoes and outlined sticker quotes. Dated, and they break the two-text-level rule.
- SFX at 200/min, which turns into noise.
- A full-colour circle-wipe ending.
- Other people's footage and brands.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Results:** a 2–3 s burst of hard cuts across our ten finished films stacking into a wall, then critique numbers (scores, LUFS) land on it as cards.
- **Hand-off:** clicking Approve sends a small spec card flying from the button to the engine panel.
- **Any agent:** agent names as large pills (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor...), without the echo effect.
