# The Best Explainer Video cum Product Demo Video Example for SaaS Company (JustCall Sales Dialer)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A-1tCn5h1PI · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:12
- **Type:** demo / explainer · **Product:** an auto-dialer for sales teams (power, dynamic and predictive modes, analytics, AI call insights, CRM sync, mobile app)
- **Numbers:** 6.7 transitions per min · median shot 4.93 s (min 0.07 s, max 39.2 s) · 126.5 words/min · ~123 BPM · 109.1 sound events/min · -15.1 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **0:00–0:07 Problem:** a stock sales rep in a headset rubbing his forehead (0:02). Spreadsheet windows and file icons then float around his head (0:05). The VO asks the viewer a direct question.
- **0:07–0:12 Reveal:** the app icon pops alone on a pale blue-to-butter gradient (0:08). It slides left as the wordmark wipes in beside it (0:11).
- **0:12–0:19 Step 1, import:** a drifting grid of CRM and helpdesk logos (0:14), then the campaign contact list (0:17).
- **0:19–0:35 Step 2, dialing modes:**
  - Power: a "Dialing…" card above an "Up Next" card, with a "Power Dialer" pill label (0:20). The on-call card follows, with a red hang-up and a waveform pulsing between two avatars (0:23).
  - Predictive: four rows dialing at once beside live counters (0:26). Rows flip to red "Reached Voicemail" and are skipped (0:29), then one connects (0:32).
- **0:35–0:47 Step 3, insight:**
  - KPI tiles (0:35), then a tilted leaderboard (0:38).
  - A diagonal wipe into the dark purple AI panel with transcript, sentiment and topics (0:39–0:44).
- **0:45–0:50 Proof:** the UI slides off to reveal the rep, now smiling, as "Increase Connect Rates by 16 %" counts up (0:47).
- **0:50–0:59 Step 4, sync and mobile:** a disposition card with star rating draws a connector line through the dialer logo to an orange CRM record (0:53–0:56). Then the mobile app (0:59).
- **1:00–1:12 Close:** stock shots of an agent with a growth arrow and a hands-in team (1:02). "Level Up Your Outreach" over a chart card (1:05). The icon morphs (1:08), then the end card with a free-start line (1:11).

## Visual style
- Clean SaaS: soft pale-blue and butter gradients, white cards with big radii and soft shadows, one brand blue.
- Status colours (green connected, red voicemail) carry the story. One dark purple section is reserved for the AI feature.
- A friendly geometric sans, with the key phrase recoloured.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Componentised UI:** single cards (a call row, a contact panel, a KPI tile) are pulled out of the app and layered on the gradient at large scale, so each reads at phone size.
- **State changes are the demo:** rows flip from Dialing to Up Next to Voicemail to On Call. The dialer works on its own, with no cursor.
- **Mode pills** anchored under each scene act as chapter tags.
- **A waveform between avatars** shows a live call (0:23).
- **A connector line** card → hub → record shows the sync (0:53).
- No cursor and no typing anywhere.

## Motion and transitions
- The first 39 s count as one detected shot: cards slide, stack and swap on one continuous gradient.
- One big boundary, a diagonal split-wipe into the dark AI section (0:39).
- The UI slides away to reveal live action behind it (0:45).
- Soft ease-out with a light stagger on the rows. The pills slide up from below.

## Pacing
- 127 wpm, conversational and punchy: an opening question, then numbered steps joined by infomercial-style "and there's more" turns.
- A new UI state every 3 s in the middle act. The ending sags under three stock shots in a row.

## Sound
- A ~123 BPM upbeat electronic bed, 109 events per minute.
- A casual, salesy narrator speaking to the viewer as a peer. The frames can't confirm the voice's gender.
- -15.1 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **0:20–0:32 state changes tell the demo:** for us, critique rows flipping from failing to passing, or a render queue advancing.
- **0:20 mode pills as chapter tags:** a small solid label naming each section.
- **0:53 a connector drawn card → hub → record:** shows a hand-off (Previs spec → engine).
- **0:23 a waveform between two avatars:** a compact sign for "talking". Could be Sarah's VO beside the Claude Code prompt.
- **0:45 UI slides off to reveal the person:** a carry from the product to the human outcome.

## Avoid
- Stock call-centre faces, a hands-in team shot, file icons orbiting a head, a growth arrow on a photo.
- Everything automatic. With no cursor, the user's own actions are lost.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Build and critique:** critique-score rows on the Previs Studio palette flip red 6 → amber 7 → green 8+ round by round, with a pill "Round 1/2/3" under them. The rows carry across rounds instead of cutting.
2. **Hand-off:** the approved-spec card draws a connector to a HyperFrames node and on to a render tile, each node landing on a real click.
