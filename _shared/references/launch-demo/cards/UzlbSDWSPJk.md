# Ads2grid: one-take flat 2D explainer in a single peach-and-orange palette

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UzlbSDWSPJk · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:22
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** cloud software to design, schedule and monitor content on remote digital-signage screens
- **Numbers:** 1.5 transitions per min (1 detected, at 1:16) · median shot 41 s (one 76 s take plus the end card) · 130 words/min · ~99 BPM · 168 sound events/min · -19.8 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:14 Problem: peach line-art airport and café with ad screens on the walls (0:01); an orange card with a rising arrow pops over it (0:05), then a grey card with a paper menu board (0:08) and a red falling arrow while the screens glitch to colour bars (0:11).
- 0:14–0:24 Name: logo with a small orange square and a cycling label beneath (0:15); a laptop with cloud, wifi and screens wired round it (0:18); an orange card with a stopwatch counting to 4 while the voice promises a set-up time under five minutes (0:22).
- 0:24–0:42 Product: the real dashboard (dark sidebar) with an arrow cursor (0:25); push-in on the sidebar as the cursor glides to Dashboard (0:29); the media editor, cursor clicking a tool (0:32); the library grid (0:35); the UI simplifies into an orange icon window (0:39).
- 0:42–1:03 Features as icons: source-app icons drop into the window (0:42); a blank TV (0:46) splits into four menu zones (0:49); integration icons orbit the logo (0:53); a phone publishing (0:56); meshing gears with a clock and a smile (0:59); a TV still playing with a wifi-off icon (1:03).
- 1:03–1:22 Benefits and CTA: three icon-plus-label beats ("Multiple Industries" 1:06, "Remotely Operated" 1:10, "Ensuring Screen Health" 1:13); hard cut to white, logo writes on (1:16); end card with free-trial line and URL (1:20).

## Visual style
Strict two-colour system: peach background, saturated orange for every active element, thin dark line art for scenery, grey and red only for the problem. Hand-drawn orange flecks as texture. Flat 2D throughout; real screens appear briefly, then become simplified orange icon windows. One small sans label at a time, low in frame.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI for about 12 s (0:25–0:37): dashboard, sidebar, editor, library, as white floating windows.
- Arrow cursor glides between menu items and clicks a tool (0:29, 0:32); the camera pushes in so menu labels read.
- After that, features become icons: the editor turns into an orange wireframe window, zones are shown on a drawn TV (0:49).
- No typing, callouts or 3D.

## Motion and transitions
One continuous take to 1:16. Every beat morphs from the last: the problem card becomes the logo square, the square slides into the laptop, the UI flattens into the icon window, the blank TV fills with zones. Icons pop and orbit; snappy easing with a small settle, like a well-made template.

## Pacing
130 words/min over a list script, a new visual about every 3 s, no rest until the white end card. With no cut and one palette, the 76 s take blurs together after the first minute.

## Sound
Light corporate bed near 99 BPM, -19.8 LUFS. One narrator reading a plain feature list. 168 events/min: a pop or tick under nearly every icon.

## What to take from it
- 0:05–0:15: the problem card morphs straight into the logo square, one object carrying problem to brand.
- 0:29: push in on the sidebar while the cursor glides to a menu item, so the label reads on a phone.
- 0:46–0:49: a blank screen that splits into four zones on the beat; the frame explains the feature.
- One-palette discipline: one background, one accent, one line colour for the whole film.

## Avoid
- Real UI for 12 s then generic icons: the demo stops being a demo.
- Rising and falling arrow cards, orbiting app icons, meshing gears.
- 76 s with no cut or rest; a pop on every icon drifts toward game sound.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- Setup: a blank 16:9 frame that splits into the Previs Studio panels (player, timeline, A/B/C options, notes) on four beats.
- Problems → turn: the red "re-render" card shrinks into the Previs Studio icon and carries into the solution.
- Previs tour: push in on the page's sidebar as the cursor glides to each section, then hold on the real panel, never swapping to an icon version.
