# Turning Complex B2B Tech Software Into Engaging Explainer Video

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S-lwrVXj3dc · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:30
- **Type:** agency social reel, 9:16 (finished animation on top, editing timeline below) · **Product:** the agency's motion service; the sample is a monochrome promo for an enterprise "specialised AI" brand
- **Numbers:** 2.0 transitions per min (no cut; one 30 s shot) · 6 words/min (music plus one shouted "Woo!") · ~152 BPM · 141.7 sound events/min · -25.9 LUFS (very quiet)

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00 **Hook layout:** from frame 0 the vertical frame stacks the agency logo, the 16:9 animation in a rounded window, a bold two-line client quote, "Can you make our boring B2B software look cool?", and the editing timeline filling the lower half.
- 0:00–0:05 **Key:** a small key shape flies in on a curve and stops beside a thin door slab (0:01–0:04); "A key designed to open every door" appears in small type (0:03).
- 0:05–0:11 **Doors:** the one door multiplies into a row of slabs (0:06–0:07); the row ripples into a fan and sweeps away until one vertical bar is left (0:08–0:11).
- 0:11–0:14 **Slot:** that bar becomes the gap in a sentence, "Rarely fits the | that matters", with ONE waiting below to drop in (0:12–0:13).
- 0:14–0:20 **Noise:** "Noise of generalist tools kills precision" with dots circling on rings around it (0:14–0:18); the rings break into arcs and vanish (0:19).
- 0:20–0:24 **Contrast:** "While the world chased Large Language Models" with radial speed lines streaming past (0:21–0:23).
- 0:24–0:27 **Turn:** "we perfected something better" assembles; a white highlight box sweeps over the last two words (0:24–0:26).
- 0:27–0:30 **Payoff:** "Specialized Minds customised for YOUR enterprise", the highlight box sweeps over the name (0:28–0:29); then it loops.

## Visual style
Strict monochrome: black ground, grey and white line shapes, one small white sans in two weights. No colour in the animation at all; the only colour on screen is the timeline's layer labels (blue, yellow, green, beige). Premium by subtraction, but the type inside the small window is tiny.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- No product UI. The product is told through objects that turn into each other (key, door, slot).
- The "UI" on show is the agency's own animation timeline: layer bars and keyframe markers scroll as the playhead runs, and the diagonal cascades of bars make the staggered timing visible.
- The single emphasis device is a white highlight box that sweeps over the key phrase (0:25, 0:29).

## Motion and transitions
One continuous take. The core grammar is the metaphor chain: key → door → row of doors → fan → single bar → gap in a sentence (0:01–0:13). Each shape becomes the next, so nothing simply appears. Smooth, weighted ease-in-out on the shapes; quick snap on the highlight box. The orbit dots and speed lines are the weaker, busier passages.

## Pacing
A new idea every 3–5 s, carried by continuous transformation rather than cuts. Text lines hold about 3 s. Built to loop on social, so the end lands straight back on the key.

## Sound
Music only, energetic (~152 BPM), with one shouted vocal sample. Very quiet mix (-25.9 LUFS). 142 sound events per minute suggests ticks and whooshes tied to the shape moves or a busy percussion track (not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- Metaphor chain where each shape becomes the next (0:01–0:13): carry, not replace, in its purest form.
- A shape turns into the gap a word drops into (0:12): the sentence completes itself on screen.
- One emphasis device only: a highlight box sweeping over the key words (0:25, 0:29).
- Show-the-process layout: finished frame on top, the timeline scrolling under it with a moving playhead (whole reel). Directly usable for our studio.
- Restraint: monochrome, one face, line shapes, no decoration.

## Avoid
- Tiny type inside the 16:9 window: well under 3 % of frame height, unreadable at 360 px.
- Orbiting dots and radial speed lines (0:16, 0:21): particle-adjacent clichés.
- No product at all: abstract metaphors alone never feel like a demo.
- Very quiet mix and a vocal "Woo!" sample.
