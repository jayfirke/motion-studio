## Visual style
Near-black navy with soft blue light in the corners and a glowing blue ring as the brand device. One thin geometric sans in light weight, white, with key connector words in blue. Dark product UI (forms, chart, phone wallet) appears only as dim partial screens under the headline. The agency badge sits in the top-left corner throughout.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Verb headline on top, matching real screen rising from the bottom edge underneath, about 2 s each (0:17–0:23).
- The cursor clicks the headline word itself (0:18): type and UI share the same click.
- Small drawn accents act out each verb: pencil for Create, two arrows for Trade, speed strokes for fastest.
- Screens are cropped and dim, never readable at phone size; the phone wallet (0:24) is the clearest view.

## Motion and transitions
One continuous canvas of type swaps: lines build word by word, the next line pushes the last up and blurs it, echo copies stack and collapse onto the key word. Only two detected cuts. Quick ease-out on words, slow drift on the UI.

## Pacing
Fast for a silent piece: a new line every 1.5–2 s, never more than five words. The verb trio runs at about 2 s per verb. Holds only at the reveal and the end card.

## Sound
Music only, mid-tempo (~108 BPM) and quiet (-22 LUFS). 163 sound events per minute is extremely dense, likely a tick, click or whoosh on almost every word (not checked by ear). Built to loop at a booth where it may play muted.

## What to take from it
- Verb trio: one verb headline per 2 s with the real screen rising under it (0:17–0:23). A compact way to show three actions.
- Cursor clicking a word in the headline (0:18): the type becomes the button.
- Question-stack hook: three short questions in 6 s before the name (0:00–0:07).
- Line push: each new line pushes the previous one up and out (0:14–0:16), so text carries instead of fading.
- A light punchline before the CTA (0:29–0:33): one human beat after the feature run.

## Avoid
- Agency badge in the corner of every frame: corner labels are banned.
- Glowing ring portal and pedestal with rising specks (0:09, 0:26): glow and particles.
- Echo duplicates on several words (0:08, 0:11): fine once, tiring when repeated.
- UI as dim, cropped peeks: never readable, so it never feels like a demo.
- No voice and a quiet mix.
