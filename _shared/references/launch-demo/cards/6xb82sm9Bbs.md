## Visual style
"Glass and light": a cobalt-to-indigo gradient over a faint grid, swapped for a pale lavender version on contrast beats. One hero component, the frosted gauge card, recurs throughout. A thin, light white sans, with a blue highlight box on key phrases like selected text. Small rounded chips with line icons. Depth comes from blur: out-of-focus copies of the gauge sit behind the sharp one.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **UI as icon:** the product is reduced to its most legible component, the gauge, rebuilt at hero size with a needle sweep and number count, and shown at several values.
- **One full screen:** the real dashboard appears once (0:49), as proof, after the gauge has taught the viewer what to read.
- **Inputs as chips:** data inputs orbit as chips, not screens.
- **No interaction:** no cursor, typing or clicks, so it reads as a brand explainer rather than a demo.

## What to take from it
- **0:04–0:07:** a word roller cycling synonyms in a highlight box, then landing. Compact breadth.
- **0:13–0:16:** a gauge needle sweep with a count-up as the reveal. One hero UI element, alive.

## Avoid
- **No VO and no cursor,** the opposite of the dense, narrated demo the owner wants.
- **The orb bloom repeated,** which flirts with the light-leak cliché.
- **Exits by pure fade or letter dissolve.**
- **The agency's watermark** in the top-left corner the whole time.
