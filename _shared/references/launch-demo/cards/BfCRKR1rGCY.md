## Visual style
A cool light-gray world with faint pink blooms. The product's own colours (red logo, green accents, dark sidebar) give the only saturation. White cards with long soft shadows sit on the ground plane. Small icon chips (icon tile plus label) orbit every hero object. On-screen type is limited to UI labels and one header line; the voice carries the copy.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product screens (themes, dashboard, CRM, email editor, mobile app), mostly flat with gentle 3D tilt on hero moments (0:10, 0:41).
- **Build from a bar:** the dashboard does not fade in. Its header strip appears first, and the body unrolls below it (0:19.5).
- **Pop-out:** one card (leads list) lifts off the dashboard with a bigger shadow to show focus (0:21).
- An arrow cursor moves and clicks rows (0:24, 0:33, 0:41). No click effects are visible, only the motion and the UI change.
- The phone enters from below the frame, and its feature chips pop in staggered around it (0:44–0:50).
- Multiplication shows breadth: one theme becomes a fan of twelve.

## Motion and transitions
Almost one continuous take: the camera drifts and pushes across one light canvas, objects entering and leaving instead of cuts. The two cuts are structural (theme fan to header bar, CRM to phone). Chips pop with a small overshoot; cards slide on long ease-outs. The carry is the shared gray ground plus the cursor moving between panels.

## Pacing
The VO is steady (126 wpm) and names one feature per clause. The picture changes state every 2–3 s through new chips, cards or a cursor action, but the background never changes, so the film feels smooth rather than punchy. The end card holds 7 s, which is too long for social.

## Sound
Upbeat corporate electronic (~123 BPM). The VO opens on an outcome claim and closes with social proof (rating plus client count) before the CTA. Dense SFX (about 94 events/min), likely pops on chip entrances and soft whooshes on card moves, under the voice.

## What to take from it
- A dashboard built from its header bar unrolling downward (0:19.5): a strong reveal for the Previs Studio page.
- One panel lifting off the screen with a deeper shadow to direct attention (0:21).
- A UI card over live-action, or over the owner's own screen recording, as a hook device (0:04).
- Breadth by multiplication: one item fans into many (0:16).
- A phone rising from below with feature chips staggered around it (0:44–0:50).

## Avoid
- Generic stock live-action of handshakes and keys (0:01): not our product.
- Orbiting icon chips around every object. Used everywhere, they become wallpaper.
- A 7 s static end card (1:01–1:08), which is dead time.
- A gray-on-gray world with tiny UI type, illegible at 360 px.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Previs Studio tour:** build the page from its top bar. The header slides in, the timeline unrolls, and then the option cards drop in. The cursor then clicks option B and that card lifts with a deeper shadow.
2. **Any agent:** one terminal window multiplies into a fan of agent windows (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor…) to show breadth in a single move.
3. **Results:** a single-row carousel of real rendered frames from our films, the centre one enlarged, with critique scores on it.
