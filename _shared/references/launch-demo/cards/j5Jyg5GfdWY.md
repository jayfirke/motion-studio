# Product Explainer Video for Enterprise Social Media Management Tool (SocialHP Exec Comms)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j5Jyg5GfdWY · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:55
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** a platform for PR and social teams to run and measure executives' social posting
- **Numbers:** 2.1 transitions per min (detector) · median shot 32.0 s (max 50.9 s) · 135.6 words/min · ~117.5 BPM · 143.4 sound events/min · -18.4 LUFS
- The detector found only 3 hard changes because the film is one continuous camera on a white board. By eye, the state changes every 4–5 s.

## Structure (beats with times)
- **0:00–0:10 Hook:** influencer profile cards pan past, then pull back to a wall of about 20 cards with coloured tag chips popping on.
- **0:10–0:30 Stakes:**
  - Money leaves fall into empty cards and a donut counts up (0:12).
  - A collage of typed-on headline words, photo cut-outs and stickers (0:17).
  - A bio field is typed into and Update is pressed (0:22). A yellow price-spike chart follows (0:26).
- **0:30–0:52 Stats:** a line-drawn magazine building with magnifiers over social icons (0:31), and a magnifying glass sliding across a big headline (0:36). Then a stock executive on his phone (0:41) and a 3X sales bar (0:50).
- **0:55 Reveal:** a small brand tag over a bold wordmark, centred on white.
- **1:00–1:10 Problem 2:** a stock team photo with role labels popping on (1:00). A chat thread follows, with the bubbles stacking and "still waiting" underlined twice (1:05).
- **1:10–1:44 Features:**
  - Inbox, then a composer where the cursor clicks Schedule (1:14).
  - A Top-100 leaderboard (1:19), then a 5X bar race between an executive avatar and a building icon (1:24).
  - The Reaction Channel dashboard pulls back while avatars and emoji orbit in (1:29–1:34). Then a LinkedIn grid (1:39).
- **1:44–1:55 CTA:** benefit icons (1:44). A big blue cursor sweeps onto an outlined "Book a Demo" button (1:48), which is filled on the end card (1:53).

## Visual style
- Flat 2D collage on pure white: blue UI line art, stock photos in rounded frames, a few line illustrations.
- One saturated blue, plus yellow for money.
- A heavy navy geometric sans set large (the headline spans about 40 % of the width) over thin grey UI text.
- Depth comes only from soft card shadows.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- The UI is redrawn as simplified wireframes (grey text bars, real photos in the media slots), not screenshots.
- **Typing:** a caret and a hashtag appear in the bio field, then Update is pressed (0:22).
- **Cursor:** a solid blue arrow about 4 % of frame height. A button fills when the arrow reaches it (1:14, 1:48).
- **Chat as story:** the bubbles alternate sides, with the key words underlined (1:05).
- **Data as motion:** bars grow, the 5X bar fills with animated stripes, and a badge pops (1:24).
- **Pull-back reveal:** the dashboard shrinks as its ecosystem orbits in (1:29 → 1:34).

## Motion and transitions
- One continuous camera pans and zooms between compositions laid out on one big board.
- Elements build in place: cards stagger, chips pop, words type on.
- Mask wipes reveal and retire the stock photos (0:44). The cursor carries across the late beats.
- Snappy ease-out, with small overshoot on the chips.

## Pacing
- Dense: 136 wpm of ad copy with names, stats and objection handling. Nearly every sentence gets its own composition.
- The one real breath is the reveal (about 3 s, 0:55). The CTA gets about 10 s.

## Sound
- A ~117 BPM upbeat corporate bed, with 143 events per minute (likely pops on the builds).
- Direct-response script: names, a stat, "just imagine", an objection, then the solution. The frames can't confirm the voice's gender.
- -18.4 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **0:22 type, then press:** a field is typed, a button pressed, and the consequence follows in about 4 s.
- **1:05 chat thread as the problem:** the bubbles stack with key phrases underlined. Fast and human.
- **1:24 bar race with a pop-on multiplier badge:** a stat told through motion.
- **1:29 → 1:34 dashboard pull-back with its ecosystem orbiting in.**
- **1:48 cursor sweeps onto the CTA button and it fills:** the CTA becomes an action.

## Avoid
- Stock people and dated flat-corporate illustration.
- A centred wordmark on white (0:55).
- Redrawn wireframe UI instead of the real product.
- Emoji orbiting the dashboard (too close to confetti).

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Problems:** a fast chat thread between the owner and an agent, with the pain words underlined, ending on a typing indicator that becomes the Claude Code prompt.
2. **Results:** a before/after bar race on critique scores, with a multiplier badge on the impact hit.
3. **CTA:** an outlined button the cursor presses so it fills on the click sound.
