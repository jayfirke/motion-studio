## Visual style
Flat 2D with real photo cut-outs in circles. Cream and near-black navy alternate by section, with one red accent. A light serif wordmark and a small grotesk for the UI. Airy, centred layouts. Depth is limited to card shadows, side-card blur and a vignette.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- The UI is redrawn into simplified white cards, with no device or browser frame.
- One visible click: the cursor picks a survey option (0:45).
- **Row lift callout** (0:50–0:58): one row pops forward and scales past the card so tiny UI becomes readable.
- Chart lines build one colour at a time on the same card (1:00–1:09).
- Blurred-sides carousel (1:14).

## Motion and transitions
- **Branded matte:** the person glyph scales to full frame and swaps the background (0:23, 1:09, 1:15, 1:43, 1:47). It replaces every crossfade.
- **Shape morphs:** pill → bar → survey card (0:42–0:47), and one card grows into the comparison chart (the 27.9 s unbroken stretch, 0:47–1:15).
- Soft, out-heavy easing. No overshoot on type.

## Pacing
A steady, dense VO (136 wpm). Most of the 26 detected cuts are frames of the glyph wipe, so there are only 6–7 real scene changes, with something new every 3–5 s inside each. The only rests are the glyph wipes and about 3 s on the end card.

## Sound
The stills can't confirm the voice or SFX. A dense onset rate (146/min) at ~152 BPM (likely 76 half-time) suggests a light percussive bed with ticks on the pops. The voice runs almost wall to wall. The mix is quiet at -18.8 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **Row lift callout** (0:50, 0:55): makes small real UI readable without a hard zoom.
- **One container that keeps growing** (0:47–1:15): almost 28 s with no cut.
- **Branded matte wipe** (0:23, 1:47) for chapter boundaries.
- **Label pill morphs into the UI it names** (0:42–0:47).
- **Blurred-sides carousel** (1:14) for one option among several.

## Avoid
- UI type at about 1 % of frame height: unreadable at 360 px.
- Redrawn, simplified UI.
- Stock avatar-network, world-map and orbit diagrams (0:00–0:21, 1:28–1:43).
- A logo wall as proof.
- Only one real click in nearly two minutes.
