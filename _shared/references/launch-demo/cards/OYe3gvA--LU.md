## Visual style
Very minimal: pale lavender-white stage with a faint glow, one purple accent, red for recording and pink for stop. Real-photo avatars in small rounded cards. The product is a light wireframe so pills and cursor read on top. Geometric sans, small type, lots of empty space; often one or two elements on screen.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Wireframe UI as backdrop, real controls (record pill, Start/Stop, link pill) drawn crisply on top: only what matters is detailed.
- A black arrow cursor is the protagonist: dotted-path flight (0:26), link click (0:28), Start (0:39), Stop (0:45), Try Now (1:04).
- Button state change on click: purple Start becomes pink Stop (0:39–0:42).
- Status pills narrate: recording, system failure, no installations.
- A tag morphs and travels between people (warning → tag → play pill → support, 0:10–0:15).

## Motion and transitions
Zero cuts. Each element morphs into the next: record dot → recording pill, logo mark → link pill, one card → stack. Dotted paths show where things travel. Soft ease-in-out, quick but never snappy. Carries: cursor, avatars, pills.

## Pacing
Dense voice (135 words/min), a new element every 2–3 s, but little on screen at once, so it stays readable. No rest until the wordmark at 1:01.

## Sound
Light bed near 123 BPM, -15.4 LUFS. Single narrator (timbre not checked; the study reads captions and numbers). Script: question hook, "meet X", one sentence per step, "it's that simple", extras, question CTA. 154 sound events per minute with no cuts: every pop, morph, click and cursor flight has a small sound.

## What to take from it
- 0:26–0:28: the cursor flies a dotted path to its target before clicking; the cursor's travel is the shot.
- 0:39–0:45: click Start, button becomes Stop, click Stop: a real control with a state change on each press.
- 0:10–0:15: a status tag that morphs and travels from customer to support; carry, not cut.
- Dense voice, sparse picture: one or two elements, one accent, 135 words/min.

## Avoid
- Garbled-symbol speech bubbles (0:01): cheap shorthand for confusion.
- Orbiting browser and integration icons (0:20, 0:58): stock, close to a spin.
- Wireframe UI where real screenshots exist.
