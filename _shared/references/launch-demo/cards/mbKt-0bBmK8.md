# Enterprise Product Demo Video for Drilling Operations SaaS (IDBS walkthrough)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mbKt-0bBmK8 · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 2:51
- **Type:** demo · **Product:** an internal drilling-to-billing workflow app (drilling logs, approvals, sales orders, self-billing) for an oil-and-gas driller
- **Numbers:** 6.3 transitions per min · median shot 6.04 s (min 3.3 s, max 31.6 s) · 122.9 words/min · ~129 BPM · 164.9 sound events/min · -16.9 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **0:00–0:05 Title:** product name set large, left-aligned, dark grey on a pale lilac/teal blurred-blob background.
- **0:05–0:32 Process map:** a wavy line draws left to right through six round icon nodes (log, approvals, sales order, service sheet, self-billing, invoice). Each caption arrives with its VO line, and the active node fills teal (0:32). This 31.6 s shot is the film's longest.
- **0:37–1:51 App tour:** drilling log, workflow log, service orders, operations tabs (BHA, casing, mud, BOP), and tracking with a filter form. Apps open from a launcher grid of tiles (1:36).
- **1:51–2:30 Billing half:** the process map returns as a chapter marker with the next node lit (2:05, 2:26), with SAP-style screens in between.
- **2:41–2:51 End:** the last SAP screen, then a small blue 3D cube logo on an almost empty frame.
- There is no problem beat, proof or CTA. It plays as a training walkthrough.

## Visual style
- A soft gradient stage: a white base with lilac blobs at the top and teal at the bottom.
- Thin-line teal glyphs in glowing white circles.
- A neutral grotesk. The map captions are about 1.5 % of frame height, unreadable on a phone.
- One accent, teal, used for active nodes and highlighted tabs.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real screenshots in frameless floating windows with long, soft shadows.
- **Empty window first:** the cuts sheet shows a blank white window sliding in, then the content fills it (0:37, 1:10, 1:41, 2:08). The container arrives before the data.
- **3D tilt:** windows lean back in perspective and the camera glides across the tables (0:39, 1:01, 1:22).
- **Magnified row callout:** one table strip lifts off at about 2× scale while the window behind blurs and greys (0:54, 1:08). This is the main emphasis device.
- **Cursor:** a large custom teal arrow moves to a tab (BHA 1:20, MUD 1:22, BOP 1:24), and the tab turns teal when the arrow reaches it. There's no press, squash or ripple.
- **Launcher tile lift:** the chosen tile turns white and rises out of a dark blue-grey grid (1:36).
- No typing. The filter form is already filled in (1:58).

## Motion and transitions
- Slow, smooth pushes and drifts with ease-in-out. Windows rise from below and settle.
- Soft dissolves between screens, and the map fades in and out as an interstitial.
- Only the gradient background carries across boundaries; no container morphs.

## Pacing
- Slow. The opening map holds 31.6 s, and most screens drift for about 6 s.
- The VO runs at 123 wpm, steady but heavy with jargon. It describes steps the viewer never sees performed.

## Sound
- A steady ~129 BPM corporate electronic pulse, probably under the whole film; 165 detected events per minute.
- One formal narrator reads a feature list ("the X app allows Y to…"). The frames can't confirm the voice's gender.
- No confirmed click or whoosh hits.
- -16.9 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- **0:37 empty window, then fill:** a clean "app opens" without a bare cut. It could be how Previs Studio enters.
- **0:54 magnified row callout:** lift one row, keep it sharp, and blur and desaturate the rest. Good for one note or one score row.
- **0:05 / 2:05 / 2:26 process map with an active node:** returning to the map works as a chapter progress bar.
- **1:36 tile lift in a launcher grid:** a clear "you are here" before the app opens.

## Avoid
- A 31 s diagram, and screens that drift with nothing clicked. This is exactly the "slow, not a demo" problem.
- A hover-only cursor with no visible cause for the change.
- Tiny captions, a title on a gradient, and dissolves between every shot (crossfades are banned).

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Chapter rail:** a 12-node path that flashes up for about 0.6 s between chapters. The next node fills with the accent, then grows into that chapter's container instead of fading.
2. **Previs Studio tour:** an empty panel slides in and fills. The cursor presses an A/B/C option, and the chosen card lifts and magnifies while the rest blurs.
