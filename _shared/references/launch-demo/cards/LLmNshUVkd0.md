## Visual style
Flat vector characters, magenta-pink and royal blue on white and blush pink, purple for transition masks. Rounded icon tiles, a friendly rounded sans. Lots of white space, no depth or shadow beyond flat cards.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI inside a browser frame with three dots, always centred and front-on.
- Typing is shown: a company name types into a field with a text caret (0:31). No mouse cursor anywhere in the sampled frames.
- State changes happen inside one frame: blocks drop into a calendar, lock badges pop onto them, slots fill a grid (0:52–1:04).
- A tab bar shot (0:48) works as a map before each tab gets its own screen.
- Output leaves the UI: invites fly out as pills to people on phones (1:08–1:13).

## Motion and transitions
Purple iris wipes (a circle that closes to a dot and reopens) are the signature link, used at least four times. Inside scenes, elements pop and scale in with a light bounce; the app tiles carry from the problem shot into the logo (0:14–0:19). Brisk and bouncy, very much 2018-era explainer.

## Pacing
Brisk 144 words/min with a new UI state every 2–4 s through the tour. Each tab gets one sentence and one or two states, then moves on. Rests only at the logo (0:23) and the end card.

## Sound
Upbeat bed near 118 BPM, mastered very quiet (-23.2 LUFS). Narration is fast and plain; the script is built as a numbered recipe, "in just three easy steps", then a tab-by-tab tour and a free-offer CTA. 119 sound events per minute fits pops on each element and swishes on the irises.

## What to take from it
- 0:26–0:40: setup as three numbered steps, each one a single typed or uploaded action; quick proof that onboarding is easy.
- 0:48 then 0:43–1:21: show the tab bar as a map, then give each tab one sentence and one state change.
- 0:52–0:56: calendar blocks drop into cells, then lock badges pop on: two quick states on the same screen.
- 1:08–1:13: an action in the UI flies out as a message to the people it affects.

## Avoid
- Repeated purple iris wipes; dated and a cliché.
- Stressed character with floating "?" and "!" icons (0:10).
- Bouncy easing on UI and flat redrawn screens; house rules want real UI and weighted easing.
- Quiet -23 LUFS master; feature recap with yoga poses (1:33) that shows no product.
