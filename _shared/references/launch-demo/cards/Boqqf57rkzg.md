# Marketing Video for SaaS Product - Viable - Reaction Video Style

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Boqqf57rkzg · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:03
- **Type:** ad (social-proof reaction montage over product UI) · **Product:** Viable, an AI that analyses customer and employee feedback and writes the reports
- **Numbers:** 2.9 transitions per min (2 detected cuts; one continuous canvas) · median shot 10.8 s (0.07–51.9 s) · 168 words/min (real user reactions as the voice track) · ~123 BPM · 136.8 sound events/min · -22.6 LUFS (quiet)

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:07 **Hook:** a big purple exclamation word (0:01), then an all-caps reaction line with the key words in a gradient (0:03), then a small setup line saying these are reactions to the analysis (0:06). The audio is real people gasping and praising.
- 0:07–0:27 **The product:** a real Viable report window floats in, tilted slightly with a pink outline offset behind it (0:09); it scrolls down through a stacked area chart, a theme card with numbers and the AI-written summary text (0:11–0:16); captions of what testers said appear under each new screen (0:19–0:24).
- 0:27–0:43 **The people:** the window drifts off bottom-left (0:27); circular webcam portraits of testers pop in one at a time, each with its caption beneath (0:30–0:40), four different faces.
- 0:43–0:58 **Back to the product:** more report screens (sentiment chart, survey breakdown, executive workspace) each paired with another quote caption (0:43–0:56).
- 0:58–1:03 **CTA:** a target icon draws (0:58) and becomes the logo; a short line, a Try It Free pill and the URL; an arrow cursor clicks the pill (1:01).

## Visual style
Pastel lavender-to-pink gradient ground with slow wavy pastel lines drifting across. One purple accent with a magenta gradient on emphasis words. Captions in heavy all-caps sans (about 5 % of frame height, two lines max), emoji allowed. Webcam faces in circles with a thin pink ring. UI windows are real screenshots on white with a soft shadow and a pink outline offset by a few pixels.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product UI in a floating, lightly tilted window, no device frame.
- No cursor inside the app: the window scrolls by itself, revealing chart → summary → numbers, and slides sideways to change report (0:09–0:16).
- The proof comes from people reacting, not from interactions. The UI is the thing they react to.
- The only click is on the CTA pill at the very end (1:01).

## Motion and transitions
One continuous canvas: windows slide in and out along diagonals, faces pop in at centre, captions type or decode in letter by letter (a scramble effect at 0:50). The wavy background lines keep moving across every beat, which holds it together. Smooth ease-out; webcam circles scale up with a small overshoot.

## Pacing
Very dense audio (168 words/min of overlapping reactions), but the picture is calm: a window scroll lasts 5–8 s and each caption holds about 2.5 s. The rhythm comes from caption swaps on each reaction rather than from cuts. No rests until the logo.

## Sound
No narrator. The voice track is a montage of real testers (several women and one man) on video calls: gasps, laughter, short praise. Under it a bright pop-electronic bed at about 123 BPM, kept quiet. SFX are small pops and swishes on captions and faces.

## What to take from it
- Captions that mirror the voice word for word in heavy caps with one gradient keyword (0:03, 0:19).
- The real report window scrolling itself to show depth of output without a cursor (0:09–0:16).
- Testimonial faces in circles that pop in between UI beats (0:30–0:40).
- CTA pill with a cursor click as the final action (1:01).

## Avoid
- Caption letter-scramble (0:50) reads as a glitch.
- Pastel gradient background with drifting wavy lines is close to the gradient-title cliché.
- Repeating the same praise line twice (opening and ending) breaks the no-repeated-tagline rule.
