## Visual style
Near-white lavender ground, one violet accent, a yellow logo mark saved for the end, WhatsApp green only inside the UI. Wide all-caps sans for labels, bold italic sans for headlines that type in letter by letter with two words in violet. The signature graphic is the ribbon: one fat rounded path with soft shadows that the whole film travels along. Depth comes from cards at different blur levels.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn but faithful UI: phone chat cards, the channel panel, flow-builder nodes and stat tiles, all cut out with no device frame.
- **Drag and drop shown literally:** the cursor grabs the WhatsApp block, a dashed ghost marks where it came from, and the block drops into place (0:07–0:09). The words DRAG, DROP, CREATE! land in time with the action.
- Flow nodes append one by one with connectors drawing between them (0:10–0:13).
- Counters tick up on the stat tiles (0:26–0:27).
- The cursor clicks the CTA pill as the final action (0:30).

## Motion and transitions
No cuts at all: one camera follows the ribbon, and every scene is the next stretch of track. Cards ride the ribbon in and out; a banner strip wipes across the stats; a purple flood takes over for the logo. Quick ease-out landings, with type and UI animating in overlap rather than in sequence. The ribbon is the carry across every boundary.

## Pacing
Dense for its length: a new state about every 1.3 s, with no rests until the end card. Without a voice, each text block is 3–6 words and readable in about 1 s. The whole feature story (what, how, use cases, proof, CTA) fits in 32 s.

## Sound
Music only, mastered very loud (-10.4 LUFS); pumping electronic, probably about 178 BPM felt in half-time. Very dense SFX (about 188 events/min): ticks under typed letters, pops on cards and nodes, whooshes along the ribbon. As in ZapBG, the SFX stand in for a voice.

## What to take from it
- One continuous ribbon path as the carry device: a single graphic the camera rides through every chapter (whole film).
- Literal drag and drop, with a dashed origin ghost and a word stack in sync (0:07–0:09).
- Flow nodes connecting one by one as the how-it-works shot (0:10–0:13).
- Stat tiles in depth with counters ticking (0:25–0:27).
- Cursor clicking the CTA pill as the last action (0:30).

## Avoid
- Mastering at -10 LUFS: far louder than our -14 target.
- Ticks on every typed letter: synth-like and fatiguing.
- Giant outlined ghost type behind the hook word (0:00), a dated template look.
- The exclamation-mark word stack, which feels like a template.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Through-line:** a single path (the timeline track from Previs Studio) that the camera rides from setup through hand-off to build. Each chapter is the next stretch, so the film never needs a hard cut.
2. **Hand-off:** the cursor drags the approved spec card from Previs Studio and drops it onto the engine node; the connector draws and the engine starts.
3. **Results:** critique-score tiles at different depths, counting up from round 1 to final (for example 6 → 8.5), with a dashboard banner wiping across.
