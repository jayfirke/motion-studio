## Visual style
Warm greige gradients, champagne gold accent, elegant high-contrast wordmark and a light sans for lines. Footage is framed in faceted masks (hexagon, octagon) that echo a cut diamond. Photoreal 3D ring and stones. Spacious, quiet, luxury.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Fairly faithful light UI in a floating window at a slight angle, with short pill captions docked at its corners.
- Arrow cursor clicks an option and the hero object responds in 3D: metal changes from gold to white gold, price updates (0:31–0:34).
- Exploded UI (0:47–0:50): the panel breaks apart into its controls, which hover around the product they change. The clearest shot in the film.
- Customer avatar plus price chip shows who the result is for.

## Motion and transitions
Only 4 hard cuts in 78 s. Carries everywhere: the diamond becomes the ring, the ring rides into the configurator, the panel explodes into controls, facet masks grow and slide to reveal footage. Slow, silky ease-in-out with gentle 3D turns; nothing bounces.

## Pacing
98 words/min, but in staccato fragments of two to four words with music between ("No guessing.", "Your brand."). Holds of 3–5 s per idea. Calm and premium rather than dense; the demo section (0:28–0:50) is the busiest.

## Sound
Soft, elegant bed near 112 BPM, -17.6 LUFS, 89 sound events per minute (light whooshes on 3D turns and pills). Narration is short declaratives and a closing promise, "Unlock a same-day yes". Text on screen repeats the VO word for word.

## What to take from it
- 0:47–0:50: exploded UI, controls detached and floating around the object they change, which updates as they are set.
- 0:31–0:34: cursor click, then the hero object re-renders and a price chip lands; cause then visible effect.
- 0:02–0:15: on-screen lines where the key word tints to the accent as it is spoken.
- 0:57–1:03: a mask shape taken from the product (a cut-stone facet) used for every footage window; a motif, not a border.

## Avoid
- The 98 words/min fragment style is too slow for the owner's "dense" brief.
- Agency watermark badge in the top-left corner throughout: a corner label.
- Logo built from shards twice (0:18, 1:10): a repeated technique.
- Type-only cards (0:37–0:41) with no product.
