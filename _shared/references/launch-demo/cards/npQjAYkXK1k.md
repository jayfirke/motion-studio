## Visual style
Monochrome teal: deep teal-to-navy gradients for titles, bright aqua for the demo, white frosted-glass panels with soft shadows. One regular-weight sans; small calm titles. The UI is restyled into glass so it matches the stage.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn UI as glass panels, legible at phone size, mostly tilted 10–25° with the camera riding them.
- The full loop in order: type → thinking dots → answer with chart → add to story → success tick → summary options → publish.
- Character-by-character typing with a caret (0:26, 0:32, 1:48 on).
- Teal triangle cursor ticks boxes, opens dropdowns in place, presses buttons (0:49–1:18).
- Camera dives onto the exact control in use (0:43, 1:12), then eases back.

## Motion and transitions
Cuts land every 2.5–4 s on a regular, music-like grid. Panels slide and re-tilt between beats; some transitions dip through a dark teal frame that becomes the next title's background. The closing montage uses real 3D depth and parallax. Smooth slow-in slow-out.

## Pacing
No voice: typed questions and UI labels carry the meaning, each state holding 3–4 s to read. Calm and premium, but not dense: the workflow is clear, the "why" is never said.

## Sound
Music only, mid-tempo bed near 99 BPM at -17.3 LUFS. Just 36 sound events per minute: UI actions are mostly silent, the opposite of the click-and-type foley the owner wants.

## What to take from it
- 0:20–1:24: a complete workflow told in order, every step driven by a visible press or typed text.
- 0:26: three pulsing dots with "Analyzing…" as the thinking state between question and answer.
- 0:43, 1:12: camera dives onto the exact control as the cursor uses it.
- 0:55: a success card with a big green tick and the next button pressed at once, so the flow never stalls.
- 1:48–2:11: a 3D field of chat cards each typing its own question.

## Avoid
- No voiceover and sparse SFX: fails the owner's bar for narration and real UI sound.
- Generic title cards ("Now", "2x faster", infinity curve) and the logo ring round a word (1:36).
- Two minutes in one hue feels flat; keep one accent but vary value and depth.
