# Explainer Video Animation for SaaS Product Company - Happy x What a Story

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nWay8rdGmAg · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:27
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** Happy, a people-insights platform with work-style profiles and coaching tips delivered in email, Slack and Teams
- **Numbers:** 4.1 transitions per min · median shot 9.8 s (longest 37.0 s) · 121.7 words/min · ~99 BPM · 135.5 sound events/min · -15.0 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:11):** the script logo on an orange gradient (0:02) opens into the product's real website hero card (0:05). The camera slowly pulls back (0:09).
- **Question (0:12–0:14):** a giant orange kinetic "HOW". Its "O" is a circle photo of a puzzled man, and an orange ring sweeps around it as it lands.
- **Work style (0:14–0:22):** the real profile page with a green "Relater" badge (0:16). The camera pushes in until the summary fills the frame (0:20).
- **Coaching (0:22–0:30):** floating tip cards and portrait bubbles on white (0:24). One tip comes forward, with a hand-drawn arrow to a colleague's face (0:27).
- **Research (0:30–0:37):** a magic-wand photo card (0:31), then the logo in an atom-style orbit of portraits (0:35).
- **Daily work (0:37–0:50):** stock footage of a woman at a laptop (0:38), with the real People Directory composited on the screen (0:42). The footage blurs behind a sharp "Coach" tip card (0:45), then cuts to team footage with sparkles (0:49).
- **Integrations (0:50–1:02):** Slack, Teams, Gmail and Calendar icons float in (0:53), then gather around a real email with a Happy coaching message (0:56). A Slack DM from Happy follows (1:00).
- **Team (1:02–1:20):** a gradient headline beside portraits with emoji pops (1:04). In the People Directory one row lifts out past the table edge (1:07). Profile photos ("share your story") slide over the UI (1:11–1:15).
- **Close (1:18–1:27):** team photo cards (1:18, 1:22), then the logo (1:25). No CTA line.

## Visual style
A warm orange brand gradient alternates with white. Real UI in white, orange and green. A script logo plus a bold rounded sans for kinetic type ("HOW" is about 40 % of frame height). Circle-masked portraits, rounded photo cards and hand-drawn doodles. Stock live action fills the middle third.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI as floating cards, or composited onto a laptop in stock footage (0:42).
- **Push-in on the UI** until one block reads (0:16 → 0:20).
- **Row lift** (1:07).
- **Product inside the user's own tools** (0:56–1:00): a real email and a real Slack DM.
- Footage defocuses behind a sharp UI card (0:45).
- Almost no visible cursor or clicks.

## Motion and transitions
Only 5 cuts. Push-ins, pull-backs and cards sliding over one another do the work. The integration icons converge into the window they lead to (0:53 → 0:56). Footage turns into UI by screen replacement, then into a card by a focus pull. Soft easing, small emoji pops.

## Pacing
A warm 122 wpm, with a new idea every 3–5 s. The UI glimpses are 2–4 s each. The last 9 s are photo holds without VO, a breath before the logo.

## Sound
A light ~99 BPM bed with moderate onsets (135/min). An upbeat, second-person explainer VO. -15 LUFS is close to our target. The SFX timbre isn't confirmed.

## What to take from it
- **Show the product inside the tools people already use** (0:56–1:00).
- **Push into real UI until one line reads** (0:16 → 0:20).
- **Row lift callout** (1:07), also used in the GlobeSmart film.
- **Kinetic question word with an image in the letter** (0:13).
- **Icons converge into the window they describe** (0:53 → 0:56).

## Avoid
- Stock footage of smiling office workers (0:38, 0:49, 1:18–1:22).
- Wand and sparkle doodles: close to banned sparkle territory.
- UI shown but never operated.
- No CTA at the end.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Any agent:** the Codex, Cursor and Copilot marks fly in and settle around one real terminal running the same workspace command.
2. **Previs Studio tour:** a steady push-in on the owner's real time-anchored note until it reads at phone size, then a cursor click resolves it.
3. **Chapter title:** a kinetic "HOW" with our UI inside the counter of the O. Use it once only.
