## Visual style
- Flat 2D, Bauhaus-minimal: semicircles, squares and circles.
- Warm cream and near-black navy grounds alternate by chapter, with one red accent.
- Real photos in circle and blob masks.
- A serif wordmark and a small grotesk UI face.
- No gradients or glow.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Simplified, redrawn product cards.
- One arrow cursor clicks a Likert option (0:45).
- Zoom-callouts lift one row out of the card at a larger scale (0:50).
- Data builds layer by layer: each person adds a coloured line.
- The carousel blurs side cards to focus on one.

## Motion and transitions

## What to take from it
- **Brand glyph as the transition (0:23, 1:43):** one shape zooms through to change chapter, with no crossfade.
- **Row callout pop-out (0:50):** the setting being discussed lifts out at a larger scale.
- **Layered data build (1:00–1:09):** each new person adds a line, so the graph tells the story.
- **Chapter colour flips:** cream for people, dark for product.

## Avoid
- A sound on nearly every pop. It breaks "fewer sounds than beats".
- Redrawn UI with only one cursor click, so it never fully reads as a demo.
- Typewriter text and blob-masked stock photos, which feel generic.
