## Visual style
Dark mode: near-black stage, faint green vignette, product green as the one accent, teal for charts, red only for problems. The first 30 s are pure line art, no UI, so the dense dashboard lands as a payoff. Geometric sans, white, small; the logo is the only large type.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product screens in Site24x7's dark theme, not cartoons.
- Tilt to travel, straighten to read: the screen lies like a table top while the camera dollies (0:37, 0:52, 1:16), then flattens when a detail matters (0:42, 1:21).
- Click shown as a filled green dot with a pale ring (0:57); no arrow cursor in the sampled frames.
- Pull-outs: a single metric strip or small window lifts out of the dashboard (0:57, 1:06).
- What the UI cannot show (integrations, AI, automation) becomes icon diagrams in the same palette.

## Motion and transitions
One cut in two minutes. Line art morphs idea to idea: loop → falling line → tangle → straight line → ring. The dashboard tilts up from below and the camera keeps travelling across it. Smooth slow-in slow-out, no bounce. Carries: the dark stage, the green accent and the screen as a surface the camera rides.

## Pacing
Slow voice (106 words/min) under a camera that never stops. A new idea every 5 s in the opening, a new UI area every 4–5 s in the demo. It breathes without going dead.

## Sound
Bed near 108 BPM, -15.3 LUFS, close to our target. Single narrator (timbre not checked; the study reads captions and numbers). Script: jargon problem, a "but it doesn't have to be" turn, capability list, question CTA. 182 sound events per minute with one cut: tick-level micro SFX on every dot, wire and UI arrival rather than big whooshes.

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:30: line art that morphs one idea into the next, then real UI as the payoff; the problem section moves with zero product.
- 0:37–0:42 and 1:16–1:21: tilt to travel, straighten to read the one detail the voice names.
- 0:57: one metric strip pulled out of the dashboard, and a dot click landing on a tab.
- The dense micro-SFX layer over a one-cut film: sound supplies the rhythm the edit does not.

## Avoid
- Radial fan of integration logos (0:47) and globe with icons on stalks (1:31): stock devices.
- Long tilted shots where nothing is readable at phone size (1:01, 1:26).
- A slow, jargon-heavy voice; ours must be denser and plainer.
