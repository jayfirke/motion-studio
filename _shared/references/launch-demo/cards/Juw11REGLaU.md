## Visual style
Deep navy to black with drifting blue nebula clouds and fine star specks. One brand blue; each sub-product borrows its own colour only for its 2 s. Geometric sans in two weights, white or blue, centred, rarely more than four words on screen. The glowing orb (the O of the logo) is the recurring motif.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
Not at all. No screens, no cursor, no device. Products are represented only by logo, name and a one-line descriptor.

## Motion and transitions
One canvas with word swaps; the few detected cuts are ring apertures. Words enter with strong horizontal motion blur, letters shuffle into words, colour shapes wipe in behind each sub-brand. The orb carries: it crosses the opening words, becomes the O of the logo, and returns before the end lockup. Fast and punchy, timed to the beat.

## Pacing
Fast for a no-voice piece: a new word or card every 1–2 s, the product family at one per 2 s. Without a voice, one sentence spreads over 5–7 s. The event invite (0:39–0:44) is the only hold.

## Sound
Music only: a loud, fast electronic track (very hot at -11.4 LUFS, well above the -14 target). About 61 sound events per minute, likely hits on word slams and wipes. Made for a trade-show booth loop where sound may be off.

## What to take from it
- A family shown as a rhythm: five items, 2 s each, same layout, different wipe shape (0:24–0:35). Fast, clear, no repetition fatigue.
- Logo motif seeded early: the orb crosses the hook (0:05) before it becomes the O of the wordmark (0:10) and returns for the close (0:44).
- A phrase built word by word, each new word nudging the line (0:01–0:05): kinetic type that reads at phone size.
- Lockup that stacks partner under brand with a plus (0:12, 0:48).

## Avoid
- Letter shuffles and scrambles (0:16, 0:35): glitch-adjacent.
- Heavy motion blur on type entries (0:03, 0:20): unreadable mid-move.
- Starfield and nebula backdrop with glowing orbs: dated "space tech" look.
- -11.4 LUFS: far too loud.
- No product at all: the opposite of the demo the owner wants; use only its rhythm ideas.
