## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** coin stacks grow into a bar chart with a rising arrow, then blue paper sheets pile up and topple (0:03).
- **Problem (0:05–0:11):** a flat character at a laptop. A rack focus blurs him and sharpens a document card (0:08). A fan of documents with red warnings, and a cursor clicking a red "Try Again" (0:10).
- **Reveal (0:12–0:15):** a ring draws around the AX mark, and the wordmark builds letter by letter.
- **Core demo (0:17–0:23):** pages get scan-corner brackets and field outlines. The field chips peel off and fly into a form panel (0:19), and the bank-statement UI fills row by row (0:21). Then a grid of documents ticks green in sequence as an electric line zips across (0:23).
- **Benefits (0:25–0:30):** round badges (10X, −50 %) slide along an orbit path.
- **Differentiators (0:30–0:47):** a type card, a 3D globe with language names and "100+ Languages", a laptop ringed by integration logos with category labels, then SaaS vs self-hosted cards.
- **CTA (0:48–0:53):** the mark in a ring, the wordmark, a tagline typing on, URL and email pills.

## Visual style
Flat 2D with soft gradients on a deep navy vignette, plus one bright light-blue problem scene. A red accent links the logo with the error triangles and "Try Again". Burned-in subtitles in a dark box at bottom centre throughout. Glossy round badges; a little 3D (the globe) and one rack-focus depth trick.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **The UI:** redrawn and simplified as a dark panel with labelled rows.
- **Data in motion:** the demo is the data moving, not a user clicking. Each field becomes a chip that flies into its exact row (0:19–0:21), showing "extraction" literally in about 4 s.
- **The cursor:** appears only in the problem beat. The pain gets a click; the product runs by itself.
- **Batch success:** documents tick green one by one (0:23).

## Motion and transitions
- **Continuous canvas:** one detected cut (0:05). Coins become paper, paper becomes the office, the ring draws into the logo, the globe becomes the language cloud.
- **Rack focus (0:08):** a cinematic carry inside a flat film.
- **Orbit path (0:25–0:30):** a white dot leads each badge in.
- **Energy:** quick pops and swishes on 152 BPM music.

## Pacing
Tight: 53 s with a new idea about every 2 s. The VO (115 wpm) uses short fragments that each get their own picture, and there are no rests.

## Sound
- **Music:** upbeat electronic-pop at about 152 BPM.
- **Voice:** a brisk explainer read, including "no more errors, no more delays".
- **SFX:** dense (178/min): whooshes for flying chips, pops for badges, a zap for the electric line.

## What to take from it
- **0:17–0:21:** data chips peeling off the source and flying into exact UI rows. The core action as physical motion.
- **0:08:** a rack focus from person to document, a change of subject without a cut.
- **0:10:** the problem shown as a cursor clicking a failing "Try Again".
- **0:23:** a grid ticking green in sequence as a fast "it scales" beat.
- **0:36–0:41:** the product inside a ring of integration logos, with labels appearing around it.

## Avoid
- **Coin stacks, rising arrows and lightbulbs.**
- **A flat cartoon office character.**
- **Burned-in subtitles** fighting the on-screen type.
- **The electric zap line and glossy badges,** which feel stock.
