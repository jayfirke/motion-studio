## Visual style
Flat 2D vector on mint and peach split by big diagonal colour planes; orange and purple accents. Hard offset drop shadows, thin outlines, floating dots and dashes as texture. Real photos in white polaroid frames, people in round avatar crops. One rounded sans, used sparingly.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI cards, not real screens; no device frames apart from a flat phone icon.
- A small arrow cursor clicks options in the brief (0:43, 0:47) and the checkout pill (1:06).
- The core idea is a data path: the logo pill at the centre, inputs on one side, outputs on the other.
- Selection shown by desaturation: everything goes grey except the chosen photos (1:06).

## Motion and transitions
One continuous canvas from 0:21 to 1:19 with no detected cut; elements pop, slide and scatter while the background planes shift. Two circle wipes (0:08, 1:19) and a viewfinder push-in (0:19–0:21) are the only real transitions. Springy pops with small overshoot; staggered photo scatters.

## Pacing
Brisk: 136 words/min, a new idea every 2–4 s, dense gallery beats. Breathes only at the logo pill (0:32) and the end wordmark.

## Sound
Upbeat, bouncy bed under one friendly narrator. 90 events/min fits pops on elements and clicks on cursor presses (not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- Selection by desaturation: the gallery greys except the chosen frames, then the cursor confirms (1:06).
- Real material in the first frame: the scrolling column of creator cards (0:00–0:08).
- Product as the hub of a chain: input → logo pill → output, joined in one line (0:54).
- Brief built field by field with a cursor (0:43–0:47).
- Push through a viewfinder to frame "the old way" (0:19–0:21).

## Avoid
- Circle iris wipes (0:08, 1:19) and logo burst lines (1:24): dated, close to particle bursts.
- Puzzle pieces, hourglass, coin and stamp badges (0:17–1:19): explainer clichés.
- Redrawn UI where a real product exists.
- Springy overshoot on everything, including type.
