## Visual style
White ground, navy and royal blue with green and coral accents. Real UI in clean white cards, phones and a laptop drawn as thin line devices. Icons are flat, rounded, one colour each. Type is almost absent: the wordmark is a heavy geometric sans, and UI text does the rest. A lot of empty white space.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI inside simple line-drawn device frames (phone at 0:16, laptop at 0:21) or as floating windows.
- Calendar shows drag and drop: content thumbnails fly into day cells while an arrow cursor rests on the grid (0:25, 1:22).
- Toggles flip on one by one to show connected networks (0:21).
- Engagement numbers count up beside icons (1:08).
- Every feature gets one screen for 3–5 s, then a metaphor icon, then the next screen.

## Motion and transitions
Shape transitions do the joins: a thick blue ring scales from huge to small and wraps the next content (0:30); a heart shape works as an iris mask with a phone inside, then pulses outward in rings over an ECG line (1:28–1:31); navy vertical bars close and open like doors at the start and end. Elements pop and slide with a light overshoot. Between those, the canvas flows continuously.

## Pacing
Brisk, witty narration at 142 words/min with one visual per sentence. Most images hold 3–5 s; the bursts of very short shots are only the mask wipes. Breathes slightly on the metaphor icons, which are quick gags tied to puns in the script.

## Sound
Narrator with a light, joke-friendly corporate read; the script uses puns and quick rhetorical lines. Upbeat pop-electronic bed around 129 BPM, mixed very low. Many small SFX (149 events/min): pops, swishes and ticks on every icon and toggle.

## What to take from it
- Calendar drag-and-drop with real thumbnails dropping into days (0:25, 1:22).
- Network toggles flipping on in sequence as the voice names them (0:21).
- A thick ring that scales down to frame the next content, used as a carry (0:30).
- Engagement counters ticking up next to their icons (1:08).

## Avoid
- Pun visuals: crossed-out desks, bin, mountains, Colosseum (0:35, 0:44, 1:41) feel like clip art.
- Heart iris with ECG and pulsing rings (1:31): dated.
- Thin line device frames make real UI look small at phone width.
