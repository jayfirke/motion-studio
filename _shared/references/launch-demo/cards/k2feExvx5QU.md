# SaaS Explainer Video for Enterprise's Product: Refract / Software Demo

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k2feExvx5QU · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 2:20
- **Type:** demo (music only, no voice) · **Product:** Refract by Fosfor, an MLOps platform (build, experiment, deploy, monitor ML models; Snowpark workbench; AI assistant)
- **Numbers:** 7.3 transitions per min · median shot 8.4 s (min 0.07, max 30.6) · ~7 words/min (captions only "[Music]") · ~103 BPM · 83 sound events/min · −15.1 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:22):** a stat on dark violet ("85% of ML projects fail"), set left with weight contrast, then the wordmark, a promise line, and "Flexible" with diagonal light shards.
- **Build and experiment (0:24–0:50):** a word label on one third ("Build", "Experiment") and a tilted product window on the other. A Jupyter cell types `import os`, and run cards show utilisation curves and model metrics.
- **Deploy (0:50–1:05):** a bridge line, then a deployment form in steep perspective. The purple cursor picks a dropdown option, and the purple Deploy button sits below. A deployed-models table follows.
- **Monitor and collaborate (1:05–1:24):** drift and monitor dashboards swing in. A comment thread where the cursor clicks into the reply field (1:20).
- **Integration and AI (1:25–2:03):** a Snowpark template picker, a notebook typing imports, a Streamlit chart, then a bar that flips in edge-on and opens into a chat answering with Python.
- **Payoff and CTA (2:05–2:20):** "months to minutes" with an hourglass. The film's UI cards scatter as floating tiles around the line (motion-blurred at 2:11), then the end card.

## Visual style
UI-centric 2.5D on a near-black-to-violet gradient with faint diagonal light planes. One accent, saturated purple, used for sidebar, buttons, charts and cursor. A light geometric sans with weight contrast inside a line ("**build**, **manage** and **scale**"). Text sits on one third and the window on the other. No device frames: white rounded panels with soft shadows, always in perspective.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **The UI:** redrawn but faithful (Jupyter, tables, forms, charts), simplified to a few readable rows.
- **The cursor:** a large custom triangular arrow in brand purple with a drop shadow. It lands on a field or button and the UI reacts (option highlight, input focus).
- **Typing:** syntax-coloured code types into notebook cells (0:26, 1:36).
- **Panels:** they swing in on a hinge, and content fills in after the panel lands. At 0:59 the cuts sheet shows four near-identical frames of a blank panel rotating, and at 1:03 and 1:53 a panel is seen edge-on mid-flip.
- **Camera:** close and low over metric cards (0:38), so numbers fill the frame at an angle.

## Motion and transitions
- **Hinge flip:** the main connector. Panels exit by rotating edge-on, and the next one rotates in on the same axis, so motion carries across changes.
- **Type:** word cards drift in over the gradient and hold about 4–6 s.
- **End scatter (2:11):** reuses the film's own panels as a constellation.
- **Easing:** smooth, slow ease-in-out; corporate, not snappy.

## Pacing
Slow. The opening 30 s is type alone, screens hold 5–10 s, and there is one idea per 6–10 s. The film breathes on empty gradient frames between sections (0:49, 1:24, 2:03).

## Sound
- **Music:** a light corporate electronic bed at about 103 BPM, flat energy, no drop.
- **SFX:** the 83 events/min are mostly music onsets. There is no audible foley on clicks or typing.
- **Voice:** none.

## What to take from it
- **0:59–1:03:** a hinge flip as the transition, one window rotating out edge-on and the next in, which keeps the camera "inside the product".
- **0:38:** a low, close camera over metric cards in perspective.
- **0:26 and 1:36:** a word label plus a tilted window on opposite thirds; two text levels, readable on a phone.
- **1:20:** a brand-coloured cursor clicking into an input (cause, then effect).
- **2:11:** the film's own UI cards scattering around the closing claim.

## Avoid
- **No voice and no foley:** the "slow, not engaging" feel the owner rejected.
- **30 s before the first real UI:** a stat on a gradient is close to the banned "centered title on a gradient".
- **Tilt and hinge flips on nearly every shot,** against our one-star-technique rule.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Hand-off:** use the hinge flip once. The previs page swings edge-on and the HyperFrames build window swings in on the same edge.
- **Results:** scatter real frames from the critique contact sheet around one claim line, then collapse them into the CTA.
- **Talk to Claude Code:** a brand-accent cursor with real click foley, on a steady camera rather than tilted.
