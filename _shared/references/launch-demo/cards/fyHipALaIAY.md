## Visual style
A dark slate base with a teal-to-indigo corner glow and one green accent for key words. A clean white sans, typed on character by character, lower-left over footage. The product's dark UI matches the palette. Stock and real satellite imagery give texture; thin-line icons sit in rounded squares.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Real screens:** in a floating window with a light tilt and soft shadow, no device frame.
- **The cursor:** a white arrow drives every demo beat (Upload, a field, a ship), and the UI answers each time.
- **Result reveal:** detection boxes pop onto objects in sequence, the clearest "AI did it" moment.
- **Before/after:** a slider on the UI shows change detection without explaining it.
- **Context:** the UI is composited over the use case's footage.

## Motion and transitions
- **Card push:** footage arrives as rounded cards sliding in (0:07).
- **Window rise:** the product window rises over each sector (0:58, 1:01, 1:03). The window is the carry, and the world changes behind it.
- **Easing:** quick, confident ease-outs; text types on rather than fades.

## Pacing
Tight: 77 s with a new idea every 3–4 s. Demo holds are 3–6 s with one click and one visible response. The VO runs 109 wpm in short benefit clauses, with no rests until the logo.

## Sound
- **Music:** driving cinematic-electronic at about 144 BPM.
- **SFX:** the densest in the batch (201/min): ticks on typed characters, whooshes on card slides, blips as boxes appear.
- **Voice:** a neutral, assured narrator; a problem question, then "introducing".

## What to take from it
- **0:24–0:29:** a click on Upload, then detection boxes popping onto ships one by one. Real cause and effect, short.
- **0:59–1:05:** one product window held while the world behind it changes, each with a before/after slider. Breadth fast.
- **0:50–0:53:** a track line drawing across the map, then a click on the target opening a detail card.
- **0:11–0:15:** a question typing on with one accent phrase, synced to the VO.
- **Overall:** dense, small SFX tied to type-on and UI events, which keep the film alive.

## Avoid
- **Stock actors** at data walls and in meeting rooms.
- **A thin-line "AI" orbit logo and growth charts with no numbers.**
- **Synthetic type-on blips,** close to our banned synth bloops. Use real key taps.
