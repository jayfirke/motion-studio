## Visual style
Near-black canvas, grey type, one cool cyan-white glow reserved for particles and streaks. Small UI-scale sans; the only large type is the chat open. Frosted cards float in z-depth. Warm daylight live action is the contrast to the cold UI world.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI, deconstructed: a bubble, status list or report is lifted out alone on black, then the full app window appears around it (0:48 → 0:58, 1:19 → 1:29).
- Agent work shown as stacked status chips with spinners that tick to checks (0:17, 0:53, 1:24).
- No cursor or clicks; prompts sit in an input with a character counter, so asking is the action.
- Output arrives as a document built by particles, not a fade (1:38).

## Motion and transitions
The middle minute is one continuous camera through black space: elements drift in from depth, defocus and hand over without cuts. Light streak → pill and particle front → document are the signature carries. The open is the opposite: hard cuts on bubbles every 0.5–1 s. Slow, weighty ease-out, no bounce.

## Pacing
Dense narration (153 wpm) spoken by the founder in first person, framed as "let's say I want…", so the demo feels like a walkthrough. Each prompt holds about 5 s, enough to read. Founder shots are the rests (1:06, 1:49).

## Sound
Calm pulsing electronic bed (~118 BPM) under the voice (-16.4 LUFS). About 76 events/min: likely soft ticks on chips and checks, swells on streak and particle builds. Male founder voice, relaxed and sincere.

## What to take from it
- Cold open on a huge, cropped chat thread with fast cuts: the problem in three lines (0:00–0:05).
- Agent progress as stacked status chips resolving to checks (0:53, 1:24): a direct model for showing Claude Code at work.
- Isolate one UI element on black, then pull back to the full app (1:19 → 1:29).
- A light streak landing as a "Processing" pill (0:38).
- First-person task framing: state a real job, then watch the product do it.

## Avoid
- 51 s without a cut and tiny type: most UI text is unreadable on a phone.
- Particles and glow sit near the banned list; one restrained use at most.
- Founder footage is not something our film has; don't fake it.
