## Visual style
Flat 2D on a near-white canvas. One electric violet as the hero colour, with coral and butter-yellow blocks behind the UI as depth. Thin black line art, small grid patches and "+" crosshair marks frame every composition. Type is a small geometric sans, left-aligned, two short lines, never a centered headline. The UI is wireframe-clean, with rounded white cards and soft shadows.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
The UI is redrawn and simplified:
- a browser window with traffic-light dots;
- a phone mock with a visible arrow cursor (0:50);
- a kanban-style plans board (0:54);
- a chat screen (1:10).

Instead of clicks, the flow is shown by thin arrows linking floating cards (1:06), so the viewer reads a pipeline more than an interaction. The orb doubles as the AI's face and as a connector.

## Motion and transitions
Circles do the cutting: a growing white circle reveals the phone (0:39), a violet disc sits behind the browser (0:53), and an iris closes on the tagline and shrinks to a dot (1:26.6–1:27.0) before the end frame. Only 14 cuts in 97 s, so almost everything is a morph or a mask. The orb carries the film from 0:26 to 1:18 (avatar, ring, building block, chat bubble, network hub).

## Pacing
Two speeds. The stock opener cuts every 2.2–2.8 s. The animated half is one long continuous sequence (the longest shot is 33.7 s) where something new lands every 3–4 s. The voice runs at a steady 155 words/min with almost no gaps. The only breath is the iris into the end card.

## Sound
An upbeat electronic bed (~123 BPM, energy rising after the orb appears) under a bright, friendly female voice playing the AI character in first person. 124 onsets/min suggests small UI ticks and pops on each card landing, but that's inferred from the numbers, not heard. Mix at −17 LUFS.

## What to take from it
- 0:26–1:18: one brand shape as the carry. A single orb is the narrator, a ring, a hub and a chat avatar, so the film never resets.
- 0:53–1:27: a 34 s continuous feature run with no hard cut. Each feature grows out of the last.
- 1:06: a pipeline drawn as arrow-linked UI cards with "+" marks and grid patches, explaining a hand-off without words.
- 0:39 and 1:26: circle reveals and the iris close, used once each, as entry and exit.
- Throughout: the product speaks in first person, so the feature list becomes a promise.

## Avoid
- Generic stock office footage as the hook (0:02–0:15). It says nothing about the product.
- Concentric pulsing rings (0:34) drift toward glow.
- Some UI labels are tiny and unreadable at phone size (0:54 column headers).
