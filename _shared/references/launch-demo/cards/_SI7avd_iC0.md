## Visual style
Dark navy ground, neon mint-green as the single accent, and red only for problems. Flat 2D clip-art metaphors mix with light UI panels in green-glow frames. Type is sparse, with one key word in green, and some words type on letter by letter. Footage appears only in the first 7 s.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI on floating glowing panels with no device frame; one panel is tilted (0:44).
- **A plain black arrow cursor.** The clicked element detaches as a raised white callout (0:44), and the hovered table row extrudes forward (1:09).
- Real UI fills about 15 s of 100. The rest shows the product as metaphor (an AI core with chips).

## Motion and transitions
- **One continuous camera move with only 3 cuts** (0:07.5, 0:42.6, 1:05.3), each landing on a statement beat.
- A single green line draws through the scenes and leads the camera from the reveal through calendars, chats and agents (0:23–0:36).
- The logo travels around an orbit and becomes the hub of the benefit cycle (1:21–1:25).
- Snappy ease-outs and lateral pans.

## Pacing
Dense: 138 words/min on a 129 BPM bed, with a new graphic idea every ~4 s inside the continuous move. It breathes once, on "And the best part?" (1:05).

## Sound

## What to take from it
- **0:44 and 1:09:** the clicked or hovered element lifts out of the panel toward camera. It shows the target without a zoom cut.
- **0:23–0:36:** one drawn line as a thread that guides the camera between beats.
- **0:00–0:07:** UI and data overlays tracked onto live footage before going graphic.
- **1:05:** a question card as a breath before the strongest feature.
- **0:40:** a headline word types on as the matching screen slides in, all on one beat.

## Avoid
- Neon glow around UI panels (glow on UI chrome).
- Clip-art metaphors (basket of coins, trophy, stars) and an orbit benefit cycle.
- Thin real-UI time, and SFX density that edges toward game sound.
