# DON'T MISS This Product Launch Video Example

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aqfc2RvMve8 · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:47
- **Type:** launch (kinetic type plus UI demo, no voiceover) · **Product:** ZapBG, a one-click background remover for images
- **Numbers:** 31.8 transitions per min · median shot 0.96 s (0.08–10.4 s) · 0 words/min (no VO) · ~118 BPM · 207 sound events/min (very dense SFX) · -18.2 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:08 **Problem:** a white line and a monospace line on flat blue (0:01). A blue header band sits over black-and-white footage of a frustrated man gritting his teeth (0:03). Words then land one per frame (0:05), with a dog photo inside clunky editing software beside a line about it being slow (0:07).
- 0:08–0:11 **Name:** the frame flips to white, the word Introducing slides in letter by letter, and the logo pops (0:09–0:11).
- 0:11–0:18 **Proof burst:** product photos flick past about once a second with horizontal whip blur between them: bulldog on orange, sneaker in a hand, smartwatch, man with a baby carrier on green screen, dog on a beach (cuts 11.5–17.7 s). The dog's background drops to a checkerboard between columns of outlined ZAP text (0:17).
- 0:18–0:23 **Audience:** dark navy word cards (0:19), then a split wipe of navy and light blue reveals FOR Designers and FOR Non-Designers (cut sheet).
- 0:23–0:40 **Demo:** the landing page tilts in 3D (0:23) and flattens (0:25). The app window pushes in from a blue frame (26.1 s); the cursor clicks a tool tab (0:29), paints red erase strokes and green keep strokes on the original while the cutout on the right updates live (0:30–0:36), then swaps in a new background texture (0:38).
- 0:40–0:47 **CTA:** share icons pop in a 3×2 grid (0:40). A save word types out on light blue (0:42), and the logo arrives with a subscribe pill and a line typed letter by letter (0:44–0:46).

## Visual style
Flat electric blue, white and navy, light-cyan alternate. One bold geometric sans plus a monospace accent line. Kinetic type is the spine: one word per frame, sometimes typed letter by letter, the background flipping colour on each word. Real product photos sit as square tiles on the blue. No 3D apart from one tilted landing page.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real app UI in a flat browser window, before and after side by side: original on the left, result on the right.
- **Live cause and effect:** each brush stroke on the left (red erase, green keep) changes the cutout on the right in the same moment (0:30–0:36): the clearest demo in the batch.
- The arrow cursor is large and always visible. Clicks are shown by the cursor landing and the tab highlight changing (0:29).
- Push-in from a coloured frame into the app window (26.1 s); no device frame.

## Motion and transitions
- **Whip carousel:** horizontal motion-blur pushes between photo tiles, roughly one every 1.1 s, with 2–3 frame blur smears (11.5–17.7 s).
- **Colour-flip type:** each new word brings a new background colour (0:05–0:09, 0:19).
- **Split wipe:** a two-colour panel slides across to swap the audience line (about 21 s).
- **Typed CTA:** letters appear one at a time (0:09, 0:42, 0:44).

## Pacing
Rapid fire (median shot under 1 s), then a steady 10 s demo, then rapid fire again. With no voice, viewers read one or two words at a time, which works only because each card holds a single word.

## Sound
Upbeat electronic (~118 BPM), no VO. SFX are extremely dense (about 207 events/min): a tick per typed letter and word, a whoosh on every whip, small hits on photo changes. The sound does the job of a voice.

## What to take from it
- **Live before/after with brush strokes:** the cursor acts on the left, the result changes on the right in the same frame (0:30–0:36). That is a pure demo grammar.
- A whip carousel of real outputs, about 1 s each, to prove range fast (0:11–0:18).
- One word per frame with a colour flip, for a punchy problem setup (0:05–0:09).
- A push-in from a colour frame into the app window as the demo entrance (26.1 s).
- SFX density as rhythm: every typed letter and whip has a sound (whole film).

## Avoid
- The typo in the share line (0:40). Always proofread on-screen copy.
- Black-and-white stock reaction footage of a frustrated man (0:03): cheap.
- Synth-style tick SFX on every letter: for our film use real key taps, sparingly.
- No VO: the owner wants dense narration.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Build and critique:** split screen with the critique scorecard on the left and the rendered frame on the right. Each fix the cursor makes on the left (a note, a score) updates the frame on the right in the same beat.
2. **Results:** a whip carousel of our real film frames (006–010), about 1 s each with motion-blur pushes, to prove range.
3. **Problems:** one word per frame with colour flips on the beat (slow, flat, silent), voiced by Sarah in sync.
