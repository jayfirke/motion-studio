# Booth Video Example for Agentic AI & Data Fabric (AOSEN)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uw3rtrNc15c · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 3:20
- **Type:** explainer (silent trade-show booth loop) · **Product:** AOSEN, an agentic-AI data fabric platform pitched to US defence health (DHA/DoD)
- **Numbers:** 0.3 transitions per min (0 detected cuts: one continuous camera move) · median shot 200.1 s · 0 words/min (no voice, no captions) · ~112 BPM · 179.3 sound events/min · −17.1 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:08 Hook: a thesis line in mixed light and bold weights on a navy radial glow.
- 0:12 Wide isometric map of the whole pipeline: white tiles on dark glass plates, with cyan light pylons.
- 0:21–1:03 Stations 1–4 (collection, ingest, AI data engineering, ML cluster). The camera parks by each one while a headline plus a 6–12 line bullet list fades in on the empty side.
- 1:11–1:45 Core ("AOSEN IQ" plate, embedded LLM), then storage. At 1:28 the frame is almost empty grid as the camera travels between stations.
- 1:53–2:43 Pull-backs to the full map for platform, user-output and benefits beats. Bullet sets swap in place.
- 2:51–3:08 Close: a centred cyan all-caps mission line over the blurred map, with use-case subheads swapping below it.
- 3:16 Logo: strokes draw in and a yellow arc closes the mark.

## Visual style
- A 3D isometric "system map" made of flat-shaded white rounded tiles with soft blue edge light, on translucent navy plates.
- The background is navy with a faint perspective grid. The accent is cyan light beams; yellow appears only in the logo.
- A geometric sans. Labels are printed flat on the isometric floor, so they belong to the world rather than floating over it.
- Bullets are tiny, about 1.5% of frame height. Depth comes from bokeh on distant tiles.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- No real UI. The product is an architecture diagram built as a 3D place.
- No cursor, clicks or typing. Activity is shown as light pulses running up the pylons and tiles lifting off their plates (0:21–0:30).
- The callout rule: park the camera beside a cluster, fill the empty half of the frame with a headline and bullets, then move on.

## Motion and transitions
- One unbroken camera path across a single world. Travel across empty grid is the transition (1:28).
- Pull-backs to the full map (0:12, 1:53, 2:26) work as chapter resets that show where you are in the pipeline.
- Text swaps while the camera keeps drifting, so the frame is never static. The easing is slow and floaty.

## Pacing
- Booth pace: each station holds about 8–16 s, and bullet sets swap with no narration.
- No rests, but no peaks either. The energy is flat from start to end.

## Sound
- No voice. Music at about 112 BPM. The onset count (about 3 per second) suggests a busy percussive or arpeggiated tech bed.
- −17.1 LUFS, under our −14 target. No UI foley, because nothing is clicked.

## What to take from it
- **One world, one camera path** (0:12 → 3:08). Every module lives on a single map and the camera travels between them, which is "carry, don't replace" at film scale.
- **Chapter resets by pulling back to the map** (0:12, 1:53, 2:26). The wide shot reorients the viewer before the next push-in.
- **Labels printed on the floor plane** (0:21, 0:54). Integrated and premium, with no floating cards.
- **Light pulses as data flow** (0:21–0:30). Cause and effect between modules without any UI.

## Avoid
- Bullet lists in 1.5%-height type (0:37, 1:03, 1:19, 1:36). Unreadable on a phone, and they break the two-text-level rule.
- A centred title on a gradient (0:04, 2:51).
- Text that only fades in, and flat energy with no build. Three silent minutes suit a booth, not a launch film.
