- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mXVnR5mcpqA · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:14
- **Type:** explainer / demo · **Product:** time and attendance app for distributed crews: face-recognition clock-in, GPS geofencing, shifts, timesheets and reports
- **Numbers:** 8.1 transitions per min · median shot 4.7 s · 123 words/min · ~123 BPM · 141 sound events/min · -15.1 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:06 Hook, persona: stock footage of a manager at his desk (0:01); floating UI chips (Time Tracking, Location, Job Scheduling) with small inset photos pop around him (0:05). VO introduces him by name.
- 0:07–0:20 Problem: blue stage, one icon tile with question marks multiplies into three tiles labelled Who / Time / Location (0:08–0:11); two pain illustrations, "Buddy punching" and "False clock-ins", each with a red alert badge (0:14); the manager footage returns in a rounded frame, rubbing his eyes, with timesheet icons and a "Timesheets, Reports" line around him (0:17–0:20).
- 0:22–0:28 Solution: white stage, logo tile appears, wordmark resolves with worker photos and app icons orbiting it (0:23–0:26).
- 0:29–0:40 Feature 1, clock-in: the blue logo tile scales up until it becomes the blue background (0:29); cards slide in: kiosk photo and a man scanning his face on a phone with a green check (0:32); map with a geofence, the avatar pin outside the zone gets a red alert, then travels the route inside (0:35–0:39).
- 0:41–1:02 Feature 2, manager view: web dashboard rises (0:42), phone admin dashboard (0:45), timecard panel with "Planned vs Actual hours" caption (0:48), shift cards and donut chart (0:51), kinetic words "All … easily" over the cards (0:54), timesheet table (0:57), reports list with one row highlighted (1:00).
- 1:02–1:14 Proof and CTA: "Trusted by 500+ customers" with stars and stacked testimonial cards (1:03); smiling manager with tablet, "Save Time" and "Stop Revenue Leaks" badges (1:06–1:09); logo end card (1:12).

## Visual style
Corporate blue gradient stage, soft rounded shapes, white cards with big radii; red only for problems, green only for success. Stock footage in rounded frames mixed with flat icon tiles and real-looking app screens. Short centered sans captions.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Hi-fi UI screens (web dashboard, phone app, reports), front-on in thin browser or phone frames, often paired desktop + phone.
- No cursor: state changes happen by panels sliding in and rows highlighting (1:00).
- Real-world proof via photos: face scan on a phone with a green tick (0:32).
- Map storytelling: geofence circle, pin with avatar, route line drawn to the site (0:35–0:39).
- Kinetic captions sit beside the screens and name the benefit while the UI shows it ("Planned vs Actual hours", 0:48).

## Motion and transitions
Only 12 cuts; most changes are in-frame. Key carry: the logo tile grows into the full background for the first feature (0:29). Cards and phones slide up from below and stack with a slight stagger; pins and badges pop with a small overshoot. Smooth, mid-speed corporate easing.

## Pacing
Steady: a new visual state every 3 s, VO at 123 words/min, footage holds 4–6 s. Problem act about 14 s, features act about 33 s. Breathes on the logo (0:23) and testimonials (1:03).

## Sound
Music bed near 123 BPM, -15.1 LUFS. One narrator throughout (timbre not checked; the study reads captions and numbers). Script uses a named persona ("meet John"), lists his pains, then shows each feature through his day. 141 sound events per minute suggests a dense layer of small hits and pops under the voice, probably on each card, pin and badge.

## What to take from it
- 0:29: the logo tile scales up into the full background, so the brand mark literally becomes the stage for the demo.
- 0:08–0:11: one tile splits into three labelled questions; a quick way to state the problem in three words.
- 0:35–0:39: map + pin + route as a demo of a rule, a pattern for showing logic that has no UI.
- 0:48: caption beside the screen naming the exact benefit the UI shows.

## Avoid
- Stock footage of a stressed man rubbing his eyes (0:17): generic.
- Orbiting photo tiles around the logo (0:26) and the stars + "Trusted by" block (1:03): dated explainer stock.
- No cursor or clicks, so the demo feels like a slideshow of screens; the owner wants visible cause and effect.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- Hook: the Motion Studio mark grows into the full stage and the first real screen rises out of it.
- Problems chapter: one tile splitting into three tiles, each naming a pain of our old draft (slow, no demo, no voice), then the camera pushes through the middle one.
- Results chapter: a caption beside the critique report naming the number the VO reads (for example "8+ on every score").
