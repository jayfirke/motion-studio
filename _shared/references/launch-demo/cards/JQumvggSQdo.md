## Visual style
- Three layers: live-action office footage, the real app at full frame, and teal-and-lime brand interstitials with portrait discs.
- The app itself is unstyled (Salesforce chrome, dense map). The brand lives only in the badge and the chapter cards.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Real screen recording**, full frame, with the actual OS arrow cursor. Nothing redrawn.
- **Into the screen:** live action pushes into the monitor until the UI is the frame (0:00 → 0:14).
- **Persona badge:** a corner portrait says whose job you're watching. Swapping the badge marks a new story (0:48).
- Plain cursor clicks with no ripple. Dialogs open the way the real app opens them.
- Map state changes (pins recolouring, territories filling, a route drawing, the radius) do the "wow" work.
- Search → results → detail card → action button (1:00–1:06) is a complete micro-story.

## Motion and transitions
- Long uncut stretches of real UI (median shot 12.8 s). The frame mostly stays put while the app changes underneath.
- The one designed transition is the push through the logo's "o" into the persona disc (0:23). It mirrors at 0:57.
- A largely static camera on the UI, with gentle zooms at most.

## What to take from it
- **Push from a real desktop into the screen until the UI is the frame** (0:00 → 0:14). A literal "enter the product" opener.
- **A corner badge naming who is driving** (0:29–0:56). Swap it to mark a new chapter.
- **A push through a letter of the wordmark into the next chapter** (0:23). A carry that brands the cut.
- **Search → results → detail → one action** (1:00–1:06). The full demo arc in about 6 s.

## Avoid
- 35 s of raw, un-zoomed enterprise UI (0:23–0:58). Tiny Salesforce text fails at 360 px and is what makes demos feel slow.
- Stock live action of a person at a desk, and flat icons popping beside a monitor (0:05, 0:11).

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Any agent:** a corner badge shows which agent drives (Claude Code, then Codex, then Cursor), swapping on each beat while the same workspace keeps running.
- **Setup:** open on the owner's real desktop and push into the terminal until it is the frame, then type the first command.
- **Results:** search → list → detail → one click to open a past film's VERIFY.md scores, framed tighter than Geopointe so it reads at 360 px.
