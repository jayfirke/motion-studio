# How We Design FinTech SaaS Explainer Videos

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bcqSNW1XYoE · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:24
- **Type:** agency short (vertical 9:16 behind-the-scenes: the film plays in a small 16:9 window above its After Effects timeline) · **Product:** Axi, a trading platform adding crypto buying
- **Numbers:** 7.5 transitions per min · median shot 8.49 s (5.8–9.8 s) · 157 words/min · ~118 BPM · 106.9 sound events/min · -18.9 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
Layout throughout: agency logo top, the film in a rounded 16:9 frame, a two-line caption (the key words in red), and below it the scrolling layer stack of the AE comp with its keyframes.
- 0:00–0:03 **Hook (brand):** the red Axi wordmark glows on black (0:00); a red wireframe globe turns while trader avatar pins pop onto it (0:01–0:03).
- 0:03–0:05 **Trust:** a red shield fills the frame as a trust line types on, the last words turning red (0:03–0:04).
- 0:05–0:10 **Platform:** the real dark trading dashboard with a red header slides up (0:05); crypto coins tumble in around its edges and the cursor moves over the table (0:06–0:09).
- 0:10–0:16 **Buy flow in three steps:** a phone slides in on the right while the instruction line types on the left (0:10); the coin chart screen (0:11); a keypad where the amount 0.10 is typed and the buy button pressed (0:13); a red loading ring that resolves to a check and a success message (0:14–0:15).
- 0:16–0:18 **Simplicity:** two short phrases type on in sequence, the second in red (0:16–0:17).
- 0:18–0:24 **Pricing:** three dark circles sit on a glowing red arc; each one lights up with an icon and a label in turn (0:20, 0:22, 0:23), cut off mid-sentence at the end.

## Visual style
Black with one saturated red: red glow, red keywords, red header bar in the product. Clean geometric sans, white with one or two red words per line. Real UI on dark plates; the phone mock is white for contrast. Glows and a globe make it feel "fintech" but also stock.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real dashboard and real mobile screens, the phone in a clean device frame beside the copy.
- The purchase is shown as the full action sequence: pick asset → type amount on a keypad → press buy → spinner → check (0:10–0:15). Each step is about 1.5 s.
- Cursor over the desktop table (0:08); tap on the phone keypad (0:13).

## Motion and transitions
The real lesson is the timeline under the film: dozens of layers whose keyframes form staircases, each element starting a few frames after the one above (visible at 0:00–0:10). That stagger is why entrances feel layered instead of all at once. Text types on letter by letter; coins and pins pop with overshoot; the dashboard slides up and settles.

## Pacing
Very tight: 157 wpm over 24 s, a new state every 1–1.5 s. Only two hard cuts; everything else is built inside the frame.

## Sound
Light electronic bed (~118 BPM) under fast, clear narration; 107 events/min of small ticks and pops on typing and icon reveals. Quiet master for a short (-18.9 LUFS).

## What to take from it
- A complete flow on a phone in 5 s: select, type amount, press, spinner, check (0:10–0:15).
- Staggered keyframe staircases: every element offset a few frames from the last (timeline, 0:00–0:10). The principle behind "overlap".
- Three placeholders lit one by one, icon then label (0:18–0:24): a tidy way to list three benefits.
- Lines that type on with the last words switching to the accent colour (0:03–0:04).

## Avoid
- Wireframe globe, red glows, tumbling coins: fintech clichés and glow on chrome.
- Showing the AE timeline is an agency flex, not a product film device.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Build-and-critique chapter: a split view like this one, the film playing above and the HyperFrames timeline (GSAP tweens as staggered bars) scrolling below — our own "how it's made" proof.
2. Hand-off chapter: three placeholders light up in turn for spec, voice and sound as each is generated.
3. Previs tour: show the approve flow as a 5-step micro-sequence (pick option, add note, press Approve, spinner, check) in about 5 s.
