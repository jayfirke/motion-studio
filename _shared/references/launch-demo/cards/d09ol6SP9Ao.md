## Visual style
Dark "tech noir": a navy-black field, soft teal and emerald glows, frosted-glass discs and capsules, thin orbit lines with comet tails. Minimal type: small white labels beside icons, no headline cards. The product's own dark theme matches the film, so UI and graphics share one palette. Depth comes from perspective planes, glow falloff and vignettes.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **The UI:** real dashboards as large flat slabs in strong perspective, with the camera close and low so a chart fills the frame and the far edge falls into darkness.
- **No interaction:** no cursor, clicks or typing. The demo is a guided flyover where the camera picks what to read.
- **Focus device:** a glass magnifier stands in for "pinpoint" and "discover".
- **Counters:** numbers count up on the health dashboard.

## Motion and transitions
- **One camera:** two detected cuts, so the film is effectively one glide. Graphics dissolve through glow, or the camera passes one slab onto the next.
- **Carry:** the orbit drawn by the comet becomes the stage for the next beat.
- **Easing:** slow drift; icons fade-scale in with glow.
