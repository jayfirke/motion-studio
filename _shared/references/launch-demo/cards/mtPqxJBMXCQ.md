## What to take from it
- Dim-and-modal focus so one dialog is lit and the rest sits back (0:49, 1:20, 1:22).
- The same three-step loop shown twice with different content, so the viewer learns it once: review, modify, approve (0:54, 1:19).
- Empty chart that fills in as the result (1:32 to 1:36).
- A question typed in chat with a written answer as the last feature, framed as "as simple as asking" (1:41).
- A scene change that carries the previous card out with it (0:23.6), where the label shrinks while the ground floods.

## Avoid
- The first 20 s of floating platform logos and stock faces in circles: generic, and our film has its own problems chapter.
- The "Madison, your new AI-powered..." title centred on a near-empty page (0:21): a centred title.
- The faces and emoji reaction collage (1:46): confetti-like and decorative.
- Redrawn imitations of Google, Gmail and a social profile; our rule is real captures.
