## Visual style
Dark blue nebula for word cards, flat teal for UI cards, one purple accent. White geometric sans, always a light line over a bold line. UI redrawn as tilted frosted-glass cards with soft shadows.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn UI, simplified to one card per idea (notebook, pipeline, chart, accordion, chat), never a whole app window except Refract Wave at 1:10.
- Cards float at a 10–20° tilt and drift; no device frame.
- One cursor moment: a solid teal arrow glides to a suggestion pill (1:21), then the screen cuts to the answer card (1:26). The click itself is implied, not animated.
- Typing is shown in input fields with a caret and three bouncing dots on the send button (1:48–1:55), repeated across many cards at once.
- Process steps reveal as connector lines drawing from source icons into a stacked card (0:25).

## Motion and transitions
A diagonal purple slab wipes from the nebula into the notebook (cut at 13.95 s); word cards fade up through dark gaps between spots (cuts at 39.5, 54.7, 74.7, 95.8 s). Inside a spot the camera pushes in on one card, then a quick slide hands over to the next. The close multiplies the chat card into dozens at different depths. Soft ease-in-out; nothing snaps.

## Pacing
Steady, not dense: about one new card every 4 s, UI holds up to 14 s while drifting. Every product spot opens and closes on a dark word card, so there is a lot of dark breathing space; without a voice, the text cards carry all meaning and slow the film down.

## Sound
Loud tech-house/electronic bed at about 129 BPM drives everything; the 140 events/min count is mostly drum transients, not UI foley. No voice. Any UI sound sits under the music.

## What to take from it
- Source icons wiring into a stacked pipeline card (0:25): a clean way to show "your inputs become a build".
- Cursor lands on a suggestion pill, then the answer card returns text plus a chart (1:21–1:26).
- Chat cards typing in parallel, then multiplying into a deep field (1:48–2:00): scale in one image.
- Strict two-level type: light setup line over a bold payoff (0:14, 0:58).

## Avoid
- No voiceover and only one cursor moment: exactly the "not looking like a demo" problem.
- Fading word cards on a nebula between every spot: slow, and a near-cliché gradient title.
- Snowflake specks and glow in the backgrounds feel decorative.
