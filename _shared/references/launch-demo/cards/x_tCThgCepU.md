- 0:53–1:00 End card: wordmark, the URL typing in, phone number, a get-started line.

## Visual style
Black with a soft green radial glow behind every hero object. A caps sans in white, the accent word in brand green, always top-centre above the object. Rounded UI panels with a thick green rim float tilted about 10–20° in 3D. AI-made fashion stills add texture.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn dark UI that reads as real: landing page, brief, portfolio grid, chat, node editor, milestones.
- Two cursors: a hand for buttons (0:26, 0:44), an arrow for picking (0:31, 0:34). Each press triggers the next screen.
- Typing is shown: the chat message is already composed and Send is clicked; the end-card URL types on.
- Screens enter tilted from a frame edge (0:41) or rise from below, then settle; moving the camera in z replaces scrolling.

## Motion and transitions
- 0:04.6–0:27.5 (22.9 s) has no detected transition: ring → laptop → portraits → dial → orb → blob → tablet is one morph chain.
- The only hard moves are the zoom-blur push (0:27.5) and the two-shot caption (0:48, 0:51).
- Smooth ease-out of about 0.5–0.8 s, no bounce; hook letters about 0.1 s apart.

## Pacing
About 100 wpm, but it feels faster because three voices trade lines (narrator, agent prompt, client). A new state every 2–3 s; a short music-only breath at 0:29–0:31.

## Sound
- Moody electronic bed at about 118 BPM, building into the proof montage.
- A male narrator with swagger, cast as a dialogue with the agent and the client rather than one explainer voice.
- 97 events/min: a click on each press, whooshes on tilted entrances, a bigger whoosh on the radial push.

## What to take from it
- 0:04.6–0:27.5: a 23 s morph chain where each object becomes the next container. This is the carry rule exactly.
- 0:15–0:24: a voice orb plus a second speaker shows talking to an agent without any chat transcript.
- 0:26 and 0:44: hand-cursor presses on a tilted UI, each followed by the next screen. The cause is always visible.
- 0:41: panels slide in tilted and settle flat, depth without device frames.

## Avoid
- The thick neon-green rim glow on every panel and the radial glows behind them (glow on UI chrome is banned).
- The radial zoom-blur warp (0:27.5): close to a glitch or light-leak cliché.
- Thin caps over full-screen video (0:48), hard to read on a phone.
