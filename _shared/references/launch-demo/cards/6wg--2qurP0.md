## Visual style
Mint-white stage, teal accent, dark grey sans for lines. Same photoreal 3D rings, faceted footage masks and floating configurator as the Caratwise film, re-skinned to this retailer's colours and a handwritten logo. Text-led: VO lines live on screen most of the time.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Product UI on frame 0 (a laptop rising in), so the hook and the demo start together.
- Cursor click on a swatch, the 3D ring changes metal and a price chip lands (0:10–0:12): cause then visible effect.
- Exploded UI where the control being named lights in the accent as the VO names it (0:28–0:30): voice and UI in sync.
- Product grids with prices to show range (0:21–0:25).

## Motion and transitions
Three hard cuts in 53 s, all inside one footage beat. Words write on in karaoke style, grey to dark with key words in teal, and push the UI aside; the stone morphs into the ring; facet masks slide in to frame footage. Smooth ease-out; the script logo draws on as the closing move.

## Pacing
Much denser than the Caratwise cut: 145 words/min against 98, 53 s against 78, same visuals. Lines run back to back with almost no music gaps. A new state every 2 s; the longest hold is the delivery footage.

## Sound
Light upbeat bed near 118 BPM, -18.3 LUFS, 129 sound events per minute (soft ticks on word builds and chip landings). Voice is quick and conversational; second-person script that ends on "Say yes right now."

## Avoid
- Agency watermark badge in the top-left corner throughout.
- Full sentences on type-only cards (0:17–0:19, 0:32) with no product in view.
- Handwritten logo draw-on and faceted masks are this brand's language, not ours.
- "Before" photo as a lone beat (0:05).
