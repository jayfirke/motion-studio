## Visual style
Full 3D in a single deep violet space. The product's own artworks are the set, multiplied into walls, cubes, grids and tunnels. Type is a heavy, wide all-caps sans in white, always placed on a 3D plane or wrapped on a ribbon. Depth comes from perspective, fog and motion blur. Purple glow and light beams add drama.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
The "UI" is the product card itself (artwork, item number, price button). It becomes the building block of every shot:
- a wall (0:00);
- the hero object (0:06);
- a cube face (0:11);
- a grid tile (0:21);
- a tunnel lining (0:25).

There's no cursor or interaction. It's a launch tease, not a demo.

## Motion and transitions
One continuous camera for 24 s: it slides, cranes, orbits and rises, and only cuts once into the tunnel (0:24.3). Type rides the camera's planes instead of sitting on top of the frame. Fast moves ease into short holds where a line can be read. The cards are the carry: they are always in frame, just rearranged.

## Pacing
Music-driven at about 144 BPM. A new line of type roughly every 1.3 s, and nothing held longer than about 2 s except the end mark. The density comes from type and camera, not from a voice.

## Sound
An energetic electronic track (~144 BPM, 175 onsets/min) carries everything at −13.1 LUFS. There's no voiceover. The captions mark applause or crowd at the start, so a hype stinger may open it (inferred, not heard).

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:05: kinetic type set on the same 3D plane as the product, so words and product share one space instead of type pasted on top.
- 0:16–0:21: the camera orbits one object while benefit lines wrap around it as ribbons. Four benefits in 5 s, one shot.
- 0:21–0:24: a scale shot. An isometric grid of hundreds of the product's tiles with one diagonal line of type.
- 0:24.3: a single push-through cut into a tunnel built from the product, with no other cut in the film.
- The whole film: the product's own objects are the set, rebuilt into different shapes as the carry.

## Avoid
- No narration. The owner wants dense voice.
- Glow and light beams (0:08) and purple haze.
- Echo and ghost copies of type (0:00.6, 0:09.7).
- Spinning cards (0:06–0:10), since spins are banned.
- Filler lines like "and more".
- The NFT-cube look will date quickly.
