## Structure (beats with times)
- **Problem (0:00–0:09):** a pink pill reading "Losing your brand identity?" over a circular portrait of a stressed "Health Insurer" (0:01). The portrait shrinks while five problem pills pop around it (Quoting Delays, Lack of Transparency, and others).
- **Reveal (0:10–0:17):** the logo, then a pyramid of pink outlined puzzle pieces for the microservices architecture, with one piece lifting out.
- **Back office (0:19–0:37):**
  - the Quotations table, with the cursor on a row (0:19);
  - the view tilts into 3D as the cursor clicks the "Quotation" tab (0:22);
  - Policies, with the cursor on an Active chip (0:25);
  - a policy detail page tilts as the cursor clicks "Financials", then flattens (0:28–0:34);
  - the Provider network table (0:37).
- **Member app (0:40–0:50):** claim cards with "Approved" ticks (0:40), claim details, a symptom questionnaire and its result with a doctor avatar (0:49).
- **Resolution (0:52–1:05):** the same circular portrait returns smiling with a "Complete Transparency" tag (0:52). Benefit icons orbit it, then a compliance triangle with a check (1:01–1:04).
- **CTA (1:07–1:11):** logo, tagline, and a pink "Contact us today" button that the cursor reaches.

## Visual style
An off-white canvas with faint pink and blue blurs, navy UI headers and a coral-pink accent from the logo. Desktop tables are white cards with soft shadows; status chips are the only saturated UI colour. Pink triangular shards (the logo shape) slide in at frame corners. Type is small, mostly in pills.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Redrawn UI at desktop fidelity** (tables, tab bars, detail pages) on floating cards with no browser chrome.
- **A black arrow cursor clicks real targets:** a row, a tab, a chip, the CTA.
- **Tilt-on-click:** as the cursor goes to the tab bar, the card rotates into steep perspective so the tabs read big, then flattens on the new tab (0:22, 0:31). This is the signature move.
- Mobile screens are tall white cards with ticks animating in. Each screen holds about 3 s.

## Motion and transitions
- **Zero cuts:** one continuous camera in a single 2.5D space. The portrait scales down to make room for pills, UI cards slide and tilt, and the portrait circle returns to close the arc.
- Pink shards wipe corners between screens.
- Smooth, moderately fast eased tilts land flat before the next click.

## Pacing
Brisk at 117 words/min, with a new UI state every ~3 s. The script is a capability list with little air. The one breath is the portrait return (0:52).

## Sound
- A ~129 BPM upbeat corporate electronic bed.
- Dense SFX (140/min): clicks on presses, pops on pills, whooshes on tilts.
- -15.2 LUFS.
- A fast, confident corporate read.

## What to take from it
- **0:22 and 0:31:** tilt-on-click. The window rotates into perspective as the cursor travels to the tabs, then lands flat on the new screen.
- **0:01 → 0:52:** the same circular frame bookends the film, stressed then smiling, carrying the arc across the demo.
- **0:00–0:07:** the subject's circle shrinks to make room as problem pills pop around it.
- **0:40:** claim cards land with an "Approved" tick, ending each mini-flow on a result.
- **0:00–1:11:** 71 s with no cuts, proof that a demo can live on camera moves alone.

## Avoid
- Stock-pose portraits (stressed, then smiling).
- An orbit-icon benefit ring and a compliance triangle.
- Decorative pink corner shards that do no job.
- SFX on every pill, and a list script with no breath.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Previs Studio tour:** as the cursor reaches the A/B/C option tabs, tilt the page into perspective; flatten on the chosen option as the timeline updates.
- **Hook and Results bookend:** frame a still of the old "basic" showreel at the hook, and return to that exact frame at Results with the new film playing in it.
- **Problems:** shrink the old film's frame as three problem pills pop around it ("thin sound", "no voice", "slow").
