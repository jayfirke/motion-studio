## Structure (beats with times)

## Visual style
White stage with lavender and cyan corner blooms; a glossy blue-violet 3D mark as hero; isometric light-blue glass blocks for "environment"; the UI in a clean white window. One bold geometric sans for the wordmark; the chat uses the product's own type. One blue accent.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- One UI moment (0:09–0:12), done well: the window tilts in, then the camera dives so the typed prompt fills the frame.
- Character-by-character typing in the real chat field, ending on a concrete task, not a generic query.
- Cursor in the field before typing and on the CTA at the end: the film opens and closes on a cause.
- Concepts (security, integrations, teams) shown with the mark and surrounding objects, not UI.

## Motion and transitions
No cuts: the mark is the carry object through every scene: spins, rises onto the stack, centres the logo ring, centres the team ring, returns to the wordmark. Big continuous camera moves: pull back into isometric, dive into the UI, whip round the ring, avatars flying past the lens. Fast in, soft out; the 0:15 whip is the energy peak.

## Pacing
Very dense for its length: 131 words/min, a new scene about every 3 s, and 190 sound events per minute (densest in the batch), a hit roughly every 0.3 s. The wordmark holds about 3 s at the end.

## Sound
Upbeat electronic bed near 123 BPM, -20.5 LUFS. One narrator, four sentences: name, promise, two capabilities, CTA. Busy SFX: spins, a whoosh on the ring whip, typing ticks, pops per avatar.

## What to take from it
- 0:09.7–0:11.5: tilt the app window in, dive onto the chat field, type a concrete prompt character by character. The fastest way to say "you talk to it".
- One hero object passes through every scene, so nothing needs a cut or a crossfade.
- 0:15: a fast whip of the integration ring with motion blur as the peak of a short ad.
- 0:21: the last frame is a cursor clicking the CTA pill.

## Avoid
- Isometric glass server stacks and floating data blocks: stock "secure AI" imagery.
- Stock portrait avatars with job titles.
- A spinning logo for half the runtime: the mark gets more time than the product. 190 events/min can tip into busy.
