## What to take from it
- Shape-morph carry: card pinches, icon draws, card joins a mosaic framing the next UI (0:42–0:45).
- Real UI on tilted planes inside a moving shape field (0:16, 0:36).

## Avoid
- Letter-scramble decode and chrome warp (0:02, 0:23): glitch.
- Neon wire cubes with glow (0:05); flash-cut stutters (0:28, 0:43).
- No VO and no cursor: it never feels like a demo, which is exactly the owner's complaint.
