- **Type:** launch / demo · **Product:** AI technical-assessment tool that builds custom tests, interviews candidates by voice and code, and writes the report
- **Numbers:** 7.5 transitions per min (10 cuts) · median shot 4.2 s · 116 words/min · ~144 BPM · 102 sound events/min · -18.2 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:08 Hook: dark navy field of floating video-call tiles with a cheeky bold headline and an emoji heart, "You ❤ Playing Where's Waldo" (0:01); camera pulls back, a magnifier ring hunts across tiles (0:04) and fills the frame (0:07).
- 0:10–0:20 Product: glassy pill logo with a chip icon (0:10); a glowing globe horizon of candidate portraits, the chip beams down on one (0:13–0:16); a "Hiring Speed" gauge needle snaps to max (0:19).
- 0:21–0:30 Recruiter side: app dashboard, cursor heads for "Invite a candidate" (0:22); modal opens, skills chosen, the email types in, a dropdown picks Beginner/Intermediate/Expert (0:25–0:29), cursor on "Send invitation".
- 0:30–0:43 Candidate side: skill-set screen with Start (0:31); spoken answer with a live waveform and "Recording…", cursor on "Submit & continue" (0:35); coding exercise with editor and countdown (0:38); cursor clicks Submit, status turns Accepted, a green dot pops (0:41).
- 0:43–0:50 Result: a "Report" tag above the page (0:44); it scrolls through per-skill AI ratings (0:47) to "Did the candidate cheat?" and "Trust score 70 %" (0:50).
- 0:52–1:05 Payoff: stretched kinetic "AAAAAAZY!" with a surprised cutout woman (0:53); a recruiter node beams onto the globe, which fills with highlighted candidates (0:56–0:59); stock businessman in a glowing circle with crossed icons for long cycles and tedious interviews (1:02–1:05).
- 1:08–1:12 End card: logo, then the cursor clicks "Sign up for free" (1:11).

## Visual style
Dark navy-to-black stage with blue radial glows and a curved horizon; bright white app windows pop off it. One indigo accent for buttons. Bold rounded sans headlines. Stock photos in circles and a cutout for the joke.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real (or faithfully rebuilt) screens in plain browser windows, flat to camera, main labels readable.
- Both users' sides in order: recruiter invites, candidate answers by voice and code, recruiter reads the report; one step per cut, every 3–4 s.
- Arrow cursor drives field focus, typing, dropdown choice, Start, Submit; the Submit click at 0:41 flips status to "Accepted" at once.
- Long report scrolls inside a fixed window and stops on the most surprising field (0:44–0:50).
- A floating section tag ("Report") names the step without VO.

## Motion and transitions
Clean cuts on the voice between UI steps; brand scenes use pull-backs and beam reveals. Windows slide up with soft ease-out. The globe horizon recurs at both ends (0:13, 0:56) as the brand space; the final CTA click mirrors the demo clicks.

## Pacing
Moderate voice (116 words/min) but tight picture: median shot 4.2 s, shortest 1.3 s, demo steps every 3 s. The 20 s metaphor intro is slower; the "AZY" gag is a 2 s rest.

## Sound
Upbeat electronic track near 144 BPM at -18.2 LUFS. Casual, energetic male narrator with jokey asides. About 102 sound events per minute: UI clicks and typing under demo steps, whooshes on beams and pull-backs.

## What to take from it
- 0:21–0:50: the whole product flow in order, from both sides, one step per cut, in under 30 s; this is the "looks like a demo" the owner wants.
- 0:25–0:29: real form interactions (typing an email, opening a dropdown and picking) instead of finished states.
- 0:41: Submit click, status flips, confirmation dot: cause then effect in under a second.
- 0:44–0:50: scroll a long report and stop on the field that matters.

## Avoid
- Cutout stock people and the stretched "AAAAAAZY!" gag: cheap and dated.
- Glowing globe and scan beams; near the glow ban.
- A 20 s metaphor intro before any UI.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- Hand-off and build chapters: both sides of our flow, one step per cut: the owner leaves a note in Previs Studio, the agent's terminal picks it up, the critique report comes back.
- Results chapter: scroll VERIFY.md inside a window and stop on the final critique scores, all 8+.
- CTA: end on the cursor clicking the real call to action, echoing the demo clicks.
