- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GJjVTYFVeQc · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 0:48
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** Feedier, a customer-feedback tool that turns surveys into a rewarded, gamified flow with a satisfaction dashboard
- **Numbers:** 3.8 transitions per min (2 detected) · median shot 11.1 s (max 35.6 s) · 0 words/min (no caption transcript: music and SFX only, or uncaptioned VO) · ~123 BPM · 108.8 sound events/min · -19.3 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:02 Hook: five gold stars pop in a row above a flat city skyline while three characters cheer.
- 0:02–0:07 Concept: a card with a sad and a happy face sits inside camera-viewfinder corner brackets on navy. It flips to a hand icon (0:05), then a teal square spins and scales up to fill the frame (0:07).
- 0:07–0:11 Problem: in a teal office a man rubs his face at a laptop while a wall chart builds tile by tile (emails, calls, meetings, survey forms).
- 0:11–0:13 The laptop shows a No Feedback Yet! state with grey stars, and a hand points at it. The screen then becomes the next scene (0:12.4).
- 0:13–0:22 Use case: two characters hand an item over; a shopper at a desktop clicks Add to cart (0:17). Three split panels slide in, desktop, tablet in hands and phone in hand (0:19–0:21), to say "every device".
- 0:23–0:26 Bridge: the panels collapse to a teal card with a basketball hoop, and a round rating badge drops through the net (0:25).
- 0:27–0:32 The product: a white widget with a four-step stepper (Ratings → Questions → Rewards → Engagements). Each step fills the next icon: 4 of 5 stars plus a green emoji pill (0:27), a 10 % discount reward (0:29), social icons with a follow pill (0:31).
- 0:33–0:36 Payoff: a character gestures while ribbon streamers fall.
- 0:37–0:40 Dashboard: a Feedback #87185 panel whose satisfaction gauge sweeps from 23 % orange to 90 % green as every rating bar fills green.
- 0:41–0:48 CTA: a teal Start Collecting Feedback button and URL, then the logo drops in above them for a 5 s hold.

## Visual style
Classic flat 2D vector in teal, navy and white with warm orange and yellow; no gradients or shadows. Thin-limbed characters. The UI uses the same flat language (white card, teal accents, small thin sans). Each act has its own solid background colour.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Fully redrawn UI, simplified to one widget and one dashboard.
- The interaction is shown by step state, not by a cursor: the stepper icon turns solid navy and the content below swaps, one step per 2 s.
- The one visible click is on the shop's Add to cart button (0:17), with a small tooltip pop.
- The gauge and bars animate from bad to good in one move (0:37–0:40): a before/after inside one panel.

## Motion and transitions
- 35.6 s with no detected transition (0:12.4–0:48). Every scene arrives through a shape that carries: the laptop screen grows into the next scene, panels collapse into a card, a badge drops through a hoop, a stepper reflows.
- The opening uses a spinning square to scale up and change scenes (0:07).
- Quick ease-in-out with small overshoots on icon pops.

## Pacing
Steady: a new state every 2 s, holds of 2–4 s, no voice to pace against. The end card gets the longest hold (7 s).

## Sound
- A bright, upbeat bed at about 123 BPM.
- No voice in the captions.
- Dense SFX (109 events/min): pops on every star, tick or flip, swishes on the scene morphs. The sound carries the story in place of narration.

## What to take from it
- 0:27–0:32: a four-step stepper where each step swaps the widget content below. It is a compact way to show a flow in 6 s.
- 0:37–0:40: one panel animates from bad (23 %, orange) to good (90 %, green). The before/after happens in place.
- 0:11–0:13: the laptop screen becomes the next scene, a push through a screen as the carry.
- 0:41–0:48: the CTA button and URL first, then the logo drops on top. The button reads before the brand.

## Avoid
- Confetti and ribbon streamers (0:33), and the spinning-square transition (0:07): both banned.
- Generic flat-character office scenes: dated, with no real product.
- No narration: the owner found our draft "not engaging" partly because there was no voice.

2. Results: a critique scorecard sweeps from the first-round scores (orange) to 8+ on every row (green) inside one panel.
