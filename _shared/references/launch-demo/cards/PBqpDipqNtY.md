## Visual style
Two-tone system: warm white with a soft orange corner glow, and charcoal with an orange underglow. One sans family, regular weight for connector words and bold orange for the key word in every line. Thin orange line-art for venues (isometric stage, stadium). UI is drawn as minimal outlined phone wireframes, then a real-looking ticket screen with poster art.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Problem UI as outlined wireframes (loading bar, checkout total) so the pain is shown on a phone, not described (0:08–0:11).
- Product as a single centred phone with a ticket screen; words split around it (0:33).
- A check pill pops next to the phone as the proof moment (0:37).
- No cursor, no taps, no navigation; it is a type-led promo with phone stills.

## Motion and transitions
Kinetic type carries the film: one word lands, the rest of the line builds beside it on the beat (0:02–0:06). Varied wipes: an expanding white circle (0:18), orange vertical stripes (0:23), a split dark/white panel slide (0:41). Light and dark alternate scene by scene, which gives contrast without cuts. Snappy ease-out on words, small overshoot on the pill pops.

## Pacing
Brisk and rhythmic: a new word or element every beat, no voice to wait for. Each scene holds about 2–3 s once complete. The only rests are the logo hits (0:25, 0:55).

## Sound
No narration; a music-led track (~118 BPM) at a loud -15.0 LUFS. 173 sound events per minute suggests the music is busy and every word landing has a tick or pop. Not checked by ear.

## What to take from it
- 0:08–0:11: each pain point shown as a tiny outlined UI moment (stuck loader, surprise fee at checkout) instead of a stock face.
- 0:02–0:06: line-art draws on behind a sentence that builds word by word, with the key word in the accent colour.
- 0:30–0:33: the product phone rises into the gap as the words split apart around it.
- 0:23 and 0:41: a different wipe per section turn, each tied to the brand colour, used only at the big turns.

## Avoid
- No voice: the owner wants dense narration.
- Phone stills with no interaction; nothing is pressed.
- Overloaded wipe vocabulary (circle, stripes, split) reads like a template pack if overused.
- Emoji chat bubbles as social proof of frustration.
