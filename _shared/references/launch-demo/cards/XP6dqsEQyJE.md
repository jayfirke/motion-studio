## Visual style
A live talking head with motion-graphics overlays. The palette is near-black with a faint topographic texture, deep-purple chapter cards and one gold accent on key words (0:11). Type is a white grotesque. Chapter cards put a gold number badge over light-and-bold two-line caps. A "whatastory.agency" watermark sits bottom-left throughout.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
Barely shown. UI appears only as small framed samples:
- a yellow marketing-automation dashboard in a thin box (0:56);
- a phone-shaped testimonial card (3:56);
- a portfolio frame where a call pill (timer, waveform, one-line complaint) sits above huge black type on a warm gradient (8:26).

There's no cursor, click or typing: the talk is about demos without demonstrating one.

## Motion and transitions
Labels and icons pop in with tight staggers. Section changes use colour washes and white flashes (0:03.6, 0:15.5–0:15.9 red to orange to yellow, 0:25.4), each a burst of 4–8 detected cuts within half a second. Text lands beside the speaker as the word is spoken. Only the presenter carries across beats.

## Pacing
- Talk-dense: 169 words/min with almost no pauses.
- An overlay change about every 2 s, between talking-head holds of up to 21 s.
- Busiest in the first minute (39 transitions) and the last two (33, 30).
- Each type ends with a spoken "in summary" recap, a recurring anchor.

## Sound
A light music bed under a warm, conversational male voice, unscripted with some fillers. At −18.3 LUFS it is quieter than our −14 target. The 106 onsets/min are mostly speech syllables. From the frames and the numbers, the SFX look limited to pops on the overlays.

## What to take from it
- 0:03.3–0:03.5, the audience burst: six role labels scatter around the subject in under half a second and say who the film is for without a sentence.
- 2:04, the ticking checklist. Buyers watch demos to tick off features, so show the ticks.
- 0:56, a two-level formula caption under a framed product sample: white statement, terms in the accent colour.
- 4:41, a left/right split with the subject as the divider, a fast before/after.
- Principles worth keeping:
  - a demo is a pain-point-led walkthrough, where each feature answers a named problem;
  - dull demos lose viewers;
  - onboarding must beat a raw screen recording;
  - a real person beats stock footage.

## Avoid
- Colour washes and white flashes (0:15.5, 0:25.4), which read as banned light leaks.
- Centered titles on a purple gradient (0:34, 6:33).
- The corner watermark, stock laptop B-roll (5:48), five-bullet text cards (7:18) and a −18 LUFS mix.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Checklist carry.** Chapter 2 names three pains. A small checklist on the right edge then ticks one each time the Previs Studio tour (5) or build and critique (7) answers it.
2. **Audience burst in the hook.** Stagger 4–5 labels (Founder, Creator, Studio, Any agent) around the terminal in 0.3 s, a real key tap under each, then cut to the first typed command.
3. **Results split.** In chapter 8, put "brief only" against "approved previs" left and right, with the playhead as the divider.
