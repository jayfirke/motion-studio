## Visual style
Light grey and white with big royal-blue blocks; green check for good, red "!" for problems. The problem act is greyscale; colour arrives with the product. Real UI on white rounded cards; persona photos in rounded cards with a dark label pill. Semibold sans titles at top centre.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real screens (dashboard, sales list, voucher table) as flat cards, sometimes inside a laptop over a stock photo.
- Arrow cursor on the sales list (0:16); a dropdown open (0:28); no visible press or ripple.
- A table tilts back in 3D to bring its Submit button into view (0:31).
- Features arrive as chips beside a persona; key labels type on letter by letter (0:22, 1:01).

## Motion and transitions
Mostly one canvas, 6 detected cuts. Panels slide in to split the frame; the grouped panels shrink into a blue frame and recede, carrying the problem act into the reveal (0:09). In each persona chapter the photo card is the anchor and chips swap around it. Quick ease-out; blue blocks wipe in behind cards.

## Pacing
Moderate: 114 words/min, a chip or label every 3 s, persona chapters about 12 s. Three problems in 9 s; the middle runs evenly with no rest until the logo.

## Sound
Bright bed (~144 BPM) under one narrator with a punchy ad-read ("No more... No more... Just..." near the end). 149 events/min and a loud mix suggest a pop on every chip (not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- Problems as panels added one by one, greyed with a red badge, then grouped and pushed back (0:00–0:10).
- Greyscale "before", colour "after".
- Anchor card with chips cycling around it (0:33–0:45): one hold carries four features.
- Table tilting back to bring its Submit button into view (0:28–0:31).
- Labels typing on (0:22, 1:01) as a light stand-in for the voice.

## Avoid
- Stock personas acting stressed or happy (0:01, 0:58) and the hexagon avatar cluster (1:01).
- Cloud badge with map pins (0:19).
- Cursor with no visible press; UI often small inside a laptop over a photo.
- Even middle with no burst; pops on everything risk sounding busy.
