## Visual style
Flat 2D vector on pure white, brand orange plus sky blue, a little green for "low risk" states. Characters are simple faceless-style corporate figures. Thin geometric sans for labels; small orange and blue accent dashes in the frame corners. UI is redrawn as clean vector, not screenshots, with the brand's table, pill and tab styles.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn UI, full-bleed or inside a flat laptop outline (1:33).
- Arrow cursor and an orange ring ripple on click (1:40); a checkbox tick selects a row (0:58).
- Heavy push-ins: the camera dives into one tab or button until it fills the frame, while the rest of the page dims grey (2:01).
- Callout popups float over the dimmed page (2:07, AI modal at 1:19).
- Status pills recolour as the decision changes (2:14).
- Form fields fill themselves (1:47); no visible typed keystrokes.

## Motion and transitions
Almost no cuts in 166 s. Everything morphs: globe → orange circle → shrinking dot → next scene (0:07–0:08); the team circle is a recurring container that icons orbit; the camera zooms through the UI rather than cutting between screens. Ease is smooth ease-in-out, a calm explainer tempo with no snap.

## Pacing
Steady, not dense. VO at 126 words/min with brief pauses between features. Each screen holds 5–8 s while one element changes; motion is gentle and constant rather than punchy.

## Sound
Light corporate-tech bed (detector ~152 BPM, possibly double time), mixed low at -20.9 LUFS. Continuous narration, benefit-led and in second person. The 170 events/min likely reflect busy music more than SFX; not verified by ear.

## What to take from it
- 0:07–0:08: the globe collapses into a single colour dot that becomes the next scene; one shape carries across the boundary instead of a cut.
- 2:01–2:07: camera dives onto one button while the rest of the page dims, then a popup answers it; clear "look here" grammar for a demo.
- 1:33–1:47: a multi-step form inside a laptop, ripple on the step tab, fields filling; shows a flow, not just a screen.
- 0:58 and 2:14: tiny state changes (checkbox ticks, pills recolour) carry the story with no extra graphics.

## Avoid
- Generic flat corporate characters and a handshake for a stat (1:54): dated.
- Logo walls and award badges as proof (2:28): reads like a sales deck.
- Too slow for the owner: 5–8 s holds and low energy, the same note he gave our draft.
- Redrawn vector UI instead of real captures.
