## What to take from it
- An in-place edit on a live page: select the headline, change it, and the page changes (0:23.1–0:25.8).
- Form on the left and the live result on the right, filling in step by step (0:28.6–0:31.3).
- A commit diff in red and green as the proof that the agent really changed a file (0:42.2–0:44.9).
- Branches as coloured pills on metro lines for "isolated tests before publishing" (0:47.6–0:50.3).
- Roles shown as avatars joined by a loop that holds the tool's mark in the middle (0:36.7).

## Avoid
- Dissolves between scenes (13.6 s, 18.6 s): crossfades.
- Butter-yellow ground with dots and sparkles scattered: decoration.
- Pop overshoot on logos and avatars: bouncy.
- 162 wpm with no rests.
