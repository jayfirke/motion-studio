# Viable: feedback analysis explainer, isometric UI

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2AWzLDFJXjo · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:09
- **Type:** explainer / demo · **Product:** AI platform that turns support tickets, reviews and surveys into weekly themed reports with urgency levels
- **Numbers:** 0.9 transitions per min (0 hard cuts) · median shot 68.5 s (one continuous shot) · 132 words/min · ~118 BPM · 135 sound events/min · -17.3 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:11 Hook and problem: over-the-shoulder office footage; the monitor shows a dense spreadsheet (0:01). Role avatars with labels and app icons (Gong, App Store, Zendesk) burst out around the screen (0:04), then all fly into the monitor and gather there as a cluster (0:07–0:10).
- 0:12–0:18 Solution: lavender stage, logo with target mark (0:13); two round icons labelled "Artificial Intelligence" and "Natural Language Processing" (0:16).
- 0:18–0:27 Demo opens: magenta stage, the app window rises from below (0:19), grows to full and the area chart draws (0:21–0:24); integration tiles cluster to show "connect your sources" (0:27).
- 0:29–0:50 Deep dive: the window tilts into an isometric plane; a hover tooltip pops on a data point (0:30); cursor points at a report row, the list opens to Complaints › "Inaccurate & delayed notifications" (0:33–0:36); the theme page shows 15.8 % and a "Severe" tag (0:38), the chart fills while a red AI badge sits beside a caption saying the AI is writing the report (0:41); a TL;DR card with "Urgency: Severe" (0:44); a "People discussing this theme" breakdown table (0:47–0:50).
- 0:52–1:01 Use cases: four icons appear one by one: Product, Customer Experience, Employee Engagement, Marketing (0:53–0:56); coloured ribbons stream from them into the logo badge (0:58).
- 1:01–1:09 Close: a cutout person stands inside a curved ring of feedback cards that orbit her (1:01–1:04); logo end card with "Start your free trial" pill (1:07).

## Visual style
UI-centric 2.5D: magenta stage for the demo, pale lavender with soft pink glow for story beats. White cards, magenta-to-orange chart gradient, red for severity. Rounded sans, sentence case, captions set on the UI's tilt.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Isometric tilt (about 30°) for the deep dive: the camera glides along the tilted page like a dolly over a table (0:30–0:50).
- Arrow cursor points and clicks a row; the next page opens in place.
- Hover tooltip with exact numbers on the chart (0:30): data feels real.
- Captions printed on the tilted plane next to the UI ("…is writing the report", 0:41), so text and UI share one space.
- Empty chart, then it fills (0:38–0:41): before and after inside one shot.

## Motion and transitions
Zero hard cuts in 69 s. Everything carries: icons fly into the monitor and become the problem (0:07); the window rises and tilts rather than cutting to a new angle; ribbons stream into the logo (0:58); cards orbit into the end card. Smooth ease-in-out camera, steady speed, no bounce.

## Pacing
VO at 132 words/min, a new UI state every 3 s; the continuous camera keeps energy without cuts. Rests only at the logo (0:13) and end card (1:07), about 2 s each.

## Sound
Bed near 118 BPM, -17.3 LUFS. One narrator throughout (timbre not checked; the study reads captions and numbers). Script: pain in two sentences, "enter" the product, then exactly what it does in the order the UI shows it, then CTA. 135 sound events per minute in a no-cut film means the SFX sit on actions (icon arrivals, clicks, chart fills), not on edits.

## What to take from it
- 0:00–0:10: real footage of a person at a screen, problem icons burst out of the monitor and then get sucked back in; the problem is shown, not told.
- 0:29–0:50: tilt the UI into isometric and glide the camera along it while the cursor drills down three levels; a deep dive with no cut.
- 0:38–0:41: show an empty chart, then the AI fills it, with a caption laid on the same plane.
- The whole film as one continuous camera: proof that "carry, don't replace" can run 69 s.

## Avoid
- "AI" and "NLP" icon circles (0:16): abstract labels with no product.
- Use-case icon row and ribbons into the logo (0:53–0:58): generic explainer filler.
- Cutout person in a ring of orbiting cards (1:01): dated, and orbiting cards edge toward a spin.
