- **Type:** explainer / demo (with customer testimonials) · **Product:** healthcare platform that automates prior-authorization submissions and benchmarks payer performance
- **Numbers:** 0.7 transitions per min (1 detected cut, at 0:49) · median shot 82.6 s · 147 words/min · ~123 BPM · 129 sound events/min · -16.6 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:31 Hook as social proof: real customer clips play inside small circles on a cream canvas, linked by thin node-and-line strokes; a new circle pops in every few seconds (one at 0:03, about ten by 0:24), each with one pain line about payers.
- 0:31–0:45 The lines collapse into one horizontal thread leading into a blurred dashboard; a sharp insight card lands in front (67 % higher approval rate, 0:38). One testimonial grows into a large rounded video window (0:45).
- 0:49–0:58 Problem in kinetic type: a navy circle wipes the frame (the only hard transition); a stacked, repeated serif "Scroll." (0:52) morphs letter by letter into the next word, apparently "Crickets." (0:58).
- 1:05 Solution: lotus mark sits on the thread line as the VO names the product.
- 1:12–1:26 Demo: a small case window grows from the line (1:12); an AI suggestion card, "Submit via Availity portal for 30 % faster resolution", with a hand cursor on a green Submit button (1:19); a "…submitted." pill rides the thread off left (1:26).
- 1:33–1:40 Analytics: dashboard with four stat tiles and a bar chart (1:33); it blurs back as a benchmark card pops forward with "1.5 days longer" in red (1:40).
- 1:47–2:07 Proof scene: two circular portraits on a phone call (clinic and insurer) with an iridescent sine ribbon between them; a short acted dialogue quotes the benchmark.
- 2:14–2:42 Scale and close: loose strokes form a US map with avatars (2:21), then a DNA helix (2:35); end card with wordmark and "Contact us to get access" (2:42).

## Visual style
Calm editorial look: warm off-white canvas, hairline strokes in rose, teal and violet, soft grey shadows, lots of air. A display serif for kinetic words on navy, clean sans for UI. Real video and photos in circles; white UI often blurred behind one sharp foreground card.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified windows floating without device frames; small and centred.
- The insight card is the hero: the dashboard sits blurred (depth of field) and one sentence card with a coloured key number pops in front (0:38, 1:40). It reads at phone size.
- Hand cursor on the green Submit button (1:19); the result rides away as a "submitted" pill (1:26), so the click has a visible consequence.

## Motion and transitions
One hard transition in 2:45. Everything else carries on a single thread line: the network collapses into it, windows grow from it, the submitted pill rides it, strokes rebuild into a map and helix. Slow ease-in-out floating; words morph letter by letter rather than replace.

## Pacing
Dense voice (147 words/min) over relaxed picture that changes every 5–7 s. The testimonial hook runs 30 s and the product first appears at about 1:05. The phone-call scene (20 s) is the biggest rest.

## Sound
Soft electronic bed near 123 BPM at -16.6 LUFS under real customer audio, then a calm, warm narrator, then an acted two-person call. About 129 sound events per minute on a nearly cut-free film, so sound follows actions (circle pops, card landings, the wave ribbon), not edits.

## What to take from it
- 0:38 and 1:40: blur the dashboard and pop one sentence card with a coloured key number in front; one insight per shot makes dense UI readable.
- 1:19–1:26: cursor on Submit, then the result leaves as a pill riding the guiding line: cause then effect, no cut.
- One thin guiding line through the film (0:31 → 1:05 → 1:26 → 2:14): cheap "carry, don't replace".
- 0:52–0:58: a stacked word that morphs letter by letter into the next pain word.

## Avoid
- A 30 s testimonial hook before any product; too slow for the owner's bar.
- Network nodes, map with avatars, DNA helix (2:14–2:35): abstract filler.
- The small centred UI at 1:12 is unreadable on a phone; push in.
