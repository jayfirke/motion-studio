# High-Converting Product Explainer Video for Surfe (ex-Leadjet)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gjpXUD8Vz6I · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:00
- **Type:** demo / explainer (narrated, cursor-driven UI) · **Product:** Leadjet (now Surfe), a browser extension that pushes LinkedIn profiles and messages into a CRM
- **Numbers:** 18.9 transitions per min (inflated: 12 of the 19 "cuts" are bubbles popping in the first 1.3 s) · median shot 0.13 s (0.07–25.7 s) · 142 words/min · ~144 BPM · 102.5 sound events/min · -18.8 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:05 **Hook:** office footage; notification bubbles (report, follow-up mail, qualified lead, meeting) pop in one after another (0:00–1.3 s), then coloured circles grow out of the footage and take over (0:04).
- 0:05–0:13 **Problem:** on violet, four prospect avatars with draining ring timers (0:06); the cursor copies details from a profile card (0:09) and pastes them into a CRM form field by field (0:11) — the manual chore, shown, not told.
- 0:13–0:16 **Intro:** a white squircle spins and settles with a dot inside (0:14), the product mark forming.
- 0:16–0:35 **Feature 1, capture:** browser window with a social profile (0:16); the camera pushes toward the extension icon as the cursor reaches it (0:19); big crop of the header with an Add-to-CRM button (0:21); the email field pops out on a tooltip (0:24); the button morphs into a link to the CRM record (0:26) and is clicked (0:29); Open Notes is clicked (0:31) and a notes panel opens (0:34).
- 0:35–0:44 **Feature 2, templates:** a message window with quick-reply chips (0:36); the cursor opens a template menu (0:39) and the full message fills in, then Send is pressed (0:41).
- 0:42–0:52 **Feature 3, pipeline:** an orange circle wipe carries the message out (0:42) and a calendar icon pops (0:44); a status pill drops a coloured list of stages and the cursor picks one (0:46–0:49).
- 0:51–1:00 **CTA:** a play arrow meets the squircle to form the logo (0:51); wordmark, Book-a-Demo pill and URL (0:54), then CRM partner logos along the bottom (0:56).

## Visual style
Violet-blue gradient ground, white rounded UI, candy-coloured status pills. Redrawn, simplified LinkedIn and CRM screens with real-looking content. Medium rounded sans; almost no headlines, the UI copy is the text.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Cursor drives every beat: hover, click, copy, paste, open, choose, send. Each click is followed within a few frames by a visible state change.
- The camera zooms hard onto the region the cursor is about to use (extension icon 0:19, profile header 0:21, Go-to-profile button 0:29, Open Notes 0:31), so tiny UI reads at full-frame size.
- Pop-out tooltips lift one field off the page (email at 0:24).
- Typing is shown as a template insert rather than key-by-key (0:41).
- Dropdowns open as tall coloured pill stacks (0:46–0:49).

## Motion and transitions
One continuous 26 s shot through the browser (0:16–0:42): pushes, crops and pans with no cuts. Shape carries between chapters: footage → coloured circles (0:04), squircle mark (0:14), orange circle wipe (0:42), arrow + squircle → logo (0:51). Fast ease-out with a short settle; snappy, never floaty.

## Pacing
Dense: 142 wpm VO and a new UI action every 2–3 s. The camera is always moving toward the next click, so there is little dead time; the only breath is the logo morph at 0:14.

## Sound
Upbeat tech-pop bed (~144 BPM), quietish master (-18.8 LUFS). About 102 events/min: clicks on cursor presses, pops on bubbles and pills. Fast, energetic narration in plain benefit lines.

## What to take from it
- The chore shown with the cursor: copy from a profile card, paste field by field into a form (0:09–0:11).
- Camera pushes onto the exact control before the cursor clicks it, then pulls back for the result (0:19–0:31).
- A button that changes label and role after it is pressed (0:26–0:29).
- Template menu that fills a whole message in one move, then Send (0:39–0:41).
- Notification bubbles popping one by one onto live footage as the hook (0:00–0:01).

## Avoid
- Redrawn imitation of a real third-party site; our rule is real captures.
- Violet gradient ground everywhere.
- The spinning squircle intro (0:14) borders on a spin.
