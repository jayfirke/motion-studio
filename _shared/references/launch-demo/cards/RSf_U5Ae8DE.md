# Product Launch Video for Crypto Ticketing Platform (TicketSoft)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RSf_U5Ae8DE · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:01
- **Type:** launch (kinetic type led) · **Product:** TicketSoft, a ticketing platform promising no hidden fees, validated tickets and rewards
- **Numbers:** 13.7 transitions per min · median shot 4.0 s (longest 14.6 s; four detections at 57.2–57.3 s are one logo-shrink move) · 129 words/min · ~118 BPM · 165.2 sound events/min · −24.3 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:06 Hook: a question on white with its last word in bold orange. A cut (2.0 s) goes to a dark stage where an orange line-art stadium draws as a line types on.
- 0:06–0:11 Problem 1 as UI: a phone booking screen, then the same phone stuck on a "server is loading" bar.
- 0:11–0:19 Problems 2–3: dark isometric rows of ticket cards, a counterfeit headline, and a 3D arrow climbing past euro coins.
- 0:19 Four complaint chat bubbles with avatars.
- 0:17–0:24 Turn: a white circle iris (17.4 s), then orange vertical bars (22.2 s) carrying "Well, not anymore!"
- 0:24–0:29 Solution: the logo, with Secure, Efficient and Reliable nodes popping around it.
- 0:29–0:36 Features: "No Hidden … Charges" splits so a phone with a ticket rises between the words. Then the booking line beside the phone.
- 0:37–0:42 Use: a flat silhouette of a woman holding a QR ticket at the door.
- 0:42–0:47 "There are Rewards too", then a ticket card pushes the Counterfeit, Fraud and Scalping tiles off-screen.
- 0:47–0:55 Aspiration: a head silhouette, then a line-art stage truss.
- 0:55–1:01 CTA line, the orange bars shrink into the logo mark (57.2 s), then logo, URL and an orange dot.

## Visual style
- Pure kinetic type: white scenes with soft orange corner glows alternate with charcoal scenes in thin orange line art.
- One accent (orange) and one sans in light and bold. The key word in every line is bold orange, at about 7–9% of frame height.
- Supporting art is minimal: phone wireframes, isometric tickets, line-art venues, silhouettes.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Very little UI: simplified phone mockups (booking, loading, ticket).
- **The problem shown as a UI state:** "Book Ticket" then a stalled loading bar (0:06 → 0:09).
- **Words make room for the product:** the headline splits around the phone (0:29).
- No cursor or taps. The product card pushes the problem tiles off-frame (0:45–0:47).

## Motion and transitions
- **The logo mark is the transition system:** its triangle-and-bars shape becomes the bar wipe (22.2 s), and giant bars shrink into the mark at the end (57.2–57.3 s).
- An iris wipe resets dark to light (17.4 s). Text types letter by letter, often still finishing as the camera moves.
- Quick ease-outs, with frequent light/dark stage flips for contrast.

## Pacing
- Fast and even: 13.7 transitions per min, 4 s median and 129 words/min. Each voice phrase gets its own type card.
- Breaths at the logo (0:24) and the silhouette (0:37–0:42), about 4–5 s each.

## Sound
- A bed at about 118 BPM with frequent hits (165 per minute). Quiet: −24.3 LUFS, about 10 dB under target.
- A rhetorical narrator: question, problem list, a turn, then benefits. The script echoes the on-screen type almost word for word.

## What to take from it
- **The brand mark as the wipe and the end-card build** (0:22, 0:57). Every transition is branded.
- **The problem shown as a stalled UI state** (0:06–0:09). Faster than any metaphor.
- **A headline that splits to frame the product** (0:29). Type and UI share the shot.
- **The product pushing the problem off-frame** (0:45–0:47). A physical carry.
- **The key word in accent colour in every line** (throughout). Reads at phone size.

## Avoid
- Script echoed on screen for 60 s with little UI. It reads as a lyric video, the "not looking like a demo" problem the owner named.
- Iris and bar wipes everywhere, the coin arrow (0:17) and the complaint bubbles (0:19). Template-y.
- A mix this quiet.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Hook / problems:** a render progress bar that stalls at 97% and then shows a "2 failed renders" error, hard-cut on the beat into the new flow.
- **Hand-off:** build the chapter wipe from our own mark (or the previs Approve chip), so every chapter boundary is branded.
- **CTA:** the wipe shape shrinks into the final wordmark, held for 1 s or more.
