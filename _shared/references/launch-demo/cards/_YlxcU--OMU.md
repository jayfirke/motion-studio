## Visual style
Dark navy fading to teal, one glowing teal accent, glassmorphism (frosted cards, prism, orbs), HUD rings and circuit lines. Thin light sans for titles. The agency's badge sits top left on every frame. Dashboards are tinted the same teal, so UI and decoration share one palette.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Stylised dark-teal dashboards on 3D-tilted planes, product name as a title above (1:08–1:28).
- One multi-screen collage for the three-audience platform (1:21).
- No cursor, clicks, typing or zoom into a detail; screens drift past.

## Motion and transitions
Long, slow camera drifts in one dark world; 3.4 cuts per min, one 46 s stretch without a cut. Carriers: a glowing dot that writes words (0:23, 2:27) and the logo stripes that sweep the frame and become the mark (0:43). HUD rings rotate, orbs float. Floaty ease-in-out.

## Pacing
Slow for its length: 117 words/min of narration, but the picture changes every 10–15 s and drifts in between. The script leans on patterns: two "what if" questions, three "because ... matters", three "from ... to ...". Only the product run (1:05–1:35) feels dense.

## Sound
Cinematic electronic bed (~129 BPM) under one measured narrator. 163 events/min against very few cuts suggests a constant layer of whooshes and ticks on every drift (not checked by ear).

## What to take from it
- Product run: each module's name above its own dashboard, about 7 s each (1:08–1:28).
- Triplet VO lines, each with one picture (1:54–2:27).
- Logo as a transition: stripes sweep the full frame and contract into the mark (0:43).
- A recurring carrier: the dot trail that writes "Different Needs" (0:23) returns to write the name (2:27).
- A wall of screens at depth that the camera drifts through (0:10) and returns to (0:49).

## Avoid
- Glow everywhere, HUD rings, orbs, glass prism (0:33–0:42, 1:41): banned glow and decoration.
- Agency badge in the corner of every frame.
- Shield and trophy icons for trust (1:54–2:07).
- UI tinted, tilted and never touched: no demo.
- 2:37 of slow drifts and long holds: the slow, unengaging feel the owner rejected in our draft.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film

3. Hook and CTA: a wall of the studio's real screens at different depths; the camera pushes through it at the start and pulls back out of it at the end.
