## Visual style
A purple to magenta to cyan gradient is the ground; white rounded cards and browser windows float on it with soft shadows; one cyan accent for the product page and the Q and A panel. Real-looking store UI (a made-up brand, "Sport Co."), real stock people in vertical video tiles, bold white tiles with large figures. The study title is a heavy italic block type with multicolour fills.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- The page enters by scrolling or sliding up, with the camera following the content (9.6–16.9 s).
- A card floats above the page and the page blurs behind it, so the viewer knows which layer is the feature (0:15.7, 0:33.5, 0:38).
- Review cards come with star ratings and small attribute circles, so a review reads as data (0:38–0:42.5).
- Statistics are tiles with one large number each, alternating with video tiles in a grid (1:16–1:32).
- No cursor, no clicks, no typing: this is a booth loop, not a walkthrough.

## Motion and transitions
Pans and slides over a fixed page, with cards popping onto it and then being replaced by the next card. The camera follows the content up the page, which is why cut detection fires in clusters. Easing is a smooth ease-out; there are no hard cuts between the main scenes, only the gradient ground as a constant.

## Pacing
Steady, silent and busy: one card or stat every 3–5 s for 1:48, with only the quote and title pause. A booth loop needs this; a launch film with a voice would not.

## Sound
No voice, no spoken words. A loud master at -10.9 LUFS and about 92 BPM, 171.6 events per min. With no narration, the sounds are probably the loudest layer on card landings and number changes (inferred, not heard).

## What to take from it
- A card that floats above the page while the page blurs behind it, to say "this is the feature layer" (0:15.7, 0:38).
- A single big number per tile, alternating with a video tile (1:16–1:32).
- A page that moves under the camera so the viewer reads the whole length of a real screen (9.6–16.9 s).
- Review cards with attribute circles that read as data (0:38–0:42.5).

## Avoid
- The purple, magenta and cyan gradient ground (all film): a gradient ground and a bland look.
- A loud master and sound on every landing.
- Multicolour fills on the study title (1:04.9).
- Stock people in vertical video tiles standing in for the product.
- A film with no voice and no reason to watch longer than 20 s.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. Previs Studio tour: a note card floating above the Previs page while the page blurs behind it, to isolate the one thing the viewer must read.
2. Results chapter: a single big number per tile for the critique scores (hook 9, motion 9, sound 8) next to a frame of the render.
3. Hand-off: let the Previs page scroll under the camera so the whole spec is read top to bottom in one move.
