## What to take from it
- A violet band behind the key phrase of each line as the reading cue, instead of a glow (0:00.7, 0:10.6, 0:24.7).
- A dotted timeline with nodes that draw in one by one, labelled with the real moments (0:10.6–0:12).
- A counter rising as the proof of a loyalty feature (0:26.1–0:28.9).
- One phone staying put while its screen swaps, with the text line tied to it.
- A text caret and selection as the line's own cursor (0:06.4–0:07.8).

## Avoid
- A silent film with no voice for 34 s: ours is narrated.
- Phones and stock creator videos as the main picture (0:16.2, 0:21.9).
- Sparkly lavender-and-lime colour change in the last 3 s (0:31.7).
- A very quiet master (-22.3 LUFS): ours is -14.
