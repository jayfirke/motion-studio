- **Type:** explainer (feature tour) · **Product:** Peko, a business finance platform (bills, software, payroll, travel, eSIM, accounting, WhatsApp for business)
- **Numbers:** 4.9 transitions per min · median shot 10.65 s (5.1–24.3 s: one continuous camera, few cuts) · 123.1 words/min · ~123 BPM · 80.4 sound events/min · -15.0 LUFS

- 0:08–0:20 **Bills:** tilted dashboard close-up with big spend figures and category tiles (0:11), the full dashboard flat (0:14), the bill tiles with a cursor click (0:17), then a payment-method card with a red pay button (0:20).

## Visual style
White-pink ground, one brand red, black geometric wordmark, soft pink shadows. Pills with line icons are the recurring unit; pale chevron rows and dot grids decorate corners. Depth comes from tilted UI panels and soft blur on far planes. Labels live inside the UI; the voice carries the message.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Persona cursor:** a black arrow with a round user photo pinned beside it (man for bills, woman for payroll and hotels). Every click shows who is acting (0:17, 0:20, 0:29, 0:38, 0:50).
- Real-looking redrawn UI with exact numbers and labels. Panels tilt about 15–25° in perspective, and the camera glides over them so one region at a time is sharp (0:11).
- A click is the cursor tip landing on a red button or tile, which reacts with a soft highlight. There are no ripples or squash.
- Feature cards build in one by one, and the chosen one rises above the rest (0:47).
- Typing goes into form fields, with a role tag under the cursor (0:50).

## Motion and transitions
Only five cuts in 73 s (13.3, 21.3, 29.0, 43.7, 68.0 s); the rest is one continuous camera panning, pushing and rack-focusing across a UI canvas. The 13.3 s boundary is a blur-out into the next panel. The opening threads lead into the logo, and the empty card that logos orbit is the carry into Pay Now. Smooth ease-in-out glides: calm, premium.

## Pacing
Dense voice (about 123 wpm, one feature per sentence) over a calm, gliding picture. A new feature arrives every 5–8 s, and the camera never stops. The only breaths are the logo hits at 0:08 and 1:08. The eSIM sky-blue scene (0:41) is the single colour break and resets attention at the midpoint.

## Sound
Bright corporate electronic (~123 BPM). The VO reads a feature list, each line led by a benefit verb ("schedule, pay and track every transaction"). About 80 SFX events/min, likely clicks under cursor taps and soft swooshes on glides.

## What to take from it
- The persona cursor: an arrow with a small avatar so every click has a visible actor (0:17–0:50). For us that could be a Claude mark next to the cursor when Claude drives, and the owner's initial when the owner clicks.
- One continuous camera over a single UI canvas, with rack focus instead of cuts (0:08–0:44).
- An empty card that logos orbit, then the card fills with the action (0:23–0:26).
- Service pills on threads that converge into the logo as the hook (0:01–0:08).
- A single colour-break scene at the midpoint to reset attention (0:41).

## Avoid
- Decorative chevron rows and corner dot grids: decoration that does no job.
- Stock-photo portraits in orbit rings (1:05).
- Tiny UI type, unreadable at 360 px; magnify the region that matters.
- A calm glide everywhere: the owner wants more energy, so mix in harder hits.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
1. **Talk to Claude Code / Build and critique:** a two-actor cursor system. The owner's cursor (initial badge) approves in Previs Studio, and Claude's cursor (Claude mark) types and runs commands, so the viewer always knows who acts.
2. **Hook:** the problems hang as pills on threads (no voice, thin sound, slow previs…), the threads pull into the motion-studio wordmark, and the problems resolve into the brand.
3. **Results:** a cost or time line falls while a quality score bar rises, using our real VERIFY.md numbers.
