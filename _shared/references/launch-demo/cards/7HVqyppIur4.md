## Visual style
Three layers: a filmed presenter on a moody stage, dark documentary footage, and clean UI on a deep blue gradient. Brand blue plus emergency red as the only accent. White rounded pills and ringed circular icon badges. Simple small sans, sentence case.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real product screenshots, full window, floating on blue with a soft shadow; no device frame.
- Arrow cursor visibly travels to each field and clicks (0:54–1:07); one choice is picked in the protocol question (0:58).
- Panels open over the form (incident list, 1:07).
- Key data is lifted out of the UI into simplified cards and wired with dotted lines to a downstream system icon (1:11), then the system fans out to its users (1:15). UI → diagram → users in one move.
- Little zoom: the window mostly stays at one scale, which keeps small text unreadable on a phone.

## Motion and transitions
Circle growth is the signature transition: a dot expands into a blue field to open the product act and again to close it (0:15, 1:35). Within the demo the window holds while the cursor moves; elements extracted from the UI slide out and connect. Footage-to-presenter is a hard cut. Calm ease-in-out, nothing aggressive, fitting the emergency subject.

## Pacing
Moderate: 113 words/min, about 8.5 s per shot. The demo stretch (0:50–1:15) is the densest part: a new UI state every 4 s. The presenter shots are the rests. The ending stacks four benefit lines quickly.

## Sound
Loud master for this channel (-15.6 LUFS), so the music and voice are forward. Narration alternates between the on-camera CEO and VO over graphics; tone is serious and reassuring. Music is a driving cinematic-tech bed (detector ~144 BPM). SFX not verified by ear; 95 events/min suggests clicks under the cursor and soft swells on the circle wipes.

## What to take from it
- 0:54–1:07: cursor actually drives the real UI field by field, a choice is picked and a panel opens: a true demo beat.
- 1:11–1:15: the record lifts out of the UI as cards and wires to the next system, which fans out to its users; shows hand-off as motion.
- 0:06: work items float around a person as cards with live timers; urgency shown with UI chrome instead of words.
- 0:15 and 1:35: one shape (the growing circle) opens and closes the product act, so the structure is visible.

## Avoid
- Small, full-window screenshots held at one scale: unreadable at 360 px.
- Generic benefit pills (Team, Responders, Safer Outcomes) on stock footage.
- A presenter on a stage only works when the person is the brand; a talking head in our film would slow it down.
