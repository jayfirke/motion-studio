# Inspiring Explainer Video Animation for SaaS Product (Happy)

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BOupst1PWXI · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:53
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** Happy, an AI people-coaching platform that gives teams personalised communication tips
- **Numbers:** 3.7 transitions per min · median shot 9.3 s (shortest 2.4 s, longest 52.3 s) · 104 words/min · ~172 BPM (likely 86 half time) · 123.5 sound events/min · −16.5 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- 0:00–0:12 Hook and problem: stock clips of meetings going wrong, cut at 3.3, 7.0 and 9.4 s. The voice tells leaders their job is people.
- 0:16–0:21 Solution: the orange app icon, then a "Welcome to Happy" card with a team photo.
- 0:21–0:30 Bridge: a stock team at sticky notes, with an orange hand-drawn stroke sketched over it.
- 0:31 Turn: a giant orange "HOW" with a thinking man's photo masked inside the O.
- 0:35–0:45 Coaching: a "Coach" search card among avatar bubbles. A tip card points at its teammate's avatar with a hand-drawn arrow.
- 0:45–0:50 Proof: a research claim over a stock photo in a rounded frame, then an orbit diagram (an AI orb with avatars on rings).
- 0:54–1:00 Assessment: the cursor clicks an answer, and a profile card with a "Relater" badge and meters follows.
- 1:04–1:13 Daily coach: the cursor picks a topic, a tip card opens, and a tip bubble pops over a video-call shot.
- 1:18–1:32 Integrations: Calendar, Teams, Slack and Gmail icons pop around a laptop shot, then a Slack message.
- 1:32–1:42 Directory: a people list, then a profile page scrolls.
- 1:42–1:53 Close: a remote video grid, an office-chair race, then the script logo on orange.

## Visual style
- Mixed media: stock live action plus clean redrawn UI cards, held together by one warm orange-to-peach radial field.
- White cards with large radii and soft long shadows. A cyan glowing orb stands for the AI coach.
- Circular avatar crops and hand-drawn arrows. A friendly sans; "HOW" fills about 40% of frame height.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI: only the 3–5 elements each line needs, scaled larger than the real app.
- Cards float on the orange field with a slight tilt, with no device frame except the Slack window (1:27).
- A small blue arrow cursor clicks choices (0:54, 1:04). The result card slides in beside the old one.
- Tips are tied to people: a card plus an arrow to an avatar (0:40), or a bubble over live action (1:13).
- Integration icons pop around a photo frame, one per spoken app name (1:18–1:23).

## Motion and transitions
- Two halves. Hard stock cuts for 30 s, then no detected cut for 30 s and 52 s while cards, photos and avatars morph on a fixed orange field.
- The persistent background plus the orb carries every beat, so the second half reads as one continuous demo.
- The "HOW" photo-in-letter (0:31) is the one kinetic-type star. Cards slide and scale in with soft ease-outs.

## Pacing
- Moderate: 104 words/min, with a new card every 4–5 s after 0:31.
- The screen changes on the voice's key nouns. It breathes at "HOW" and on the end logo.

## Sound
- An upbeat bed at about 172 BPM, probably an 86 BPM groove. −16.5 LUFS.
- A warm, lightly playful second-person narrator, for example "conflicts they get resolved not escalated".
- SFX are likely small pops on card and icon entrances.

## What to take from it
- **A persistent brand field that carries the whole demo** (0:31–1:53): one backdrop and one AI orb, so 80 s of UI reads as one shot.
- **The question word as giant type with a photo inside a letter** (0:31), a pivot from problem to solution in under 1 s.
- **Output cards that point at the person they're for** (0:40, 1:13), so the benefit is concrete.
- **Integration icons popping on the voice** (1:18–1:23): "works where you already are" in 5 s.
- **A cursor click that spawns the next card beside it** (0:54, 1:04). The cause is visible.

## Avoid
- Stock office arguments, a magic wand and the chair race (0:00–0:30, 0:45, 1:46). Dated and off-brand.
- A centred logo on a gradient (0:16, 1:51). Hand-drawn arrows next to UI read cute; use them sparingly.
