## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:03):** a question line builds on pale lavender, its last word landing in a purple highlight box ("Rewards"). The mark extends into the wordmark.
- **Before (0:04–0:09):** "Before" types on beside a phone with a real campaign card. Dotted red lines tangle between icon stations (warehouse, products, packaging, inventory, logistics).
- **Consequence (0:11–0:13):** a red "Delayed product delivery" icon branches into five consequences, each with a red ✕.
- **After (0:14–0:17):** "With Odore", the same phone, with the stations on one straight line and green checks travelling along it.
- **Outcome (0:18–0:23):** a ring of creator portraits expands to a wall of real creator videos (0:21). At 0:22 the phones scatter over a dotted world map.
- **How (0:23–0:35):** "warehouses in [35 regions]" as region labels pop on the map, then three photo cards: nearest warehouse (0:26), custom packaging (0:30), delivery to creators (0:33).
- **Summary and CTA (0:35–0:41):** an orbit diagram with three benefits, then a full purple frame with the yellow mark, the URL and a yellow "Get Started Today" pill that an arrow cursor glides to.

## Visual style
Light SaaS look: a pale lavender background with thick rounded purple "track" lines behind the content like a route map. Purple plus one yellow pop. A geometric sans in two weights. Key nouns sit in solid purple highlight boxes. Real photos in rounded cards; line icons in white circles.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **Anchor phone:** a real campaign screen in a phone mockup stays put through before and after, while the diagram beside it changes from tangled to straight.
- **Process as diagram:** warehousing has no UI, so stations are icon circles and progress is a green check moving along the line.
- **The cursor:** appears only at the CTA.
- **Proof:** a grid of real creator videos.

## Motion and transitions
- **Continuous canvas:** the purple track lines carry scene to scene, and the camera seems to slide along the route.
- **Before/after in one layout:** the red curved tangle and the straight checked line share a composition, so the change is the transition.
- **Highlight-box type-on:** a solid box grows first and the word types into it, on every claim.
- **Easing:** quick, light ease-outs matched to 152 BPM.

## Pacing
Brisk for text-only: a new claim every 2–3 s, the whole story in 41 s. Each line is one short sentence with one highlighted noun, and the text is the narrator. No rests until the CTA hold.

## Sound
- **Music:** bright electronic-pop at about 152 BPM.
- **SFX:** light ticks and pops on type-ons, checks and icons (120/min).
- **Voice:** none. The master is quiet.

## What to take from it
- **0:04–0:16:** before and after in the same layout: a tangled red route becomes one straight line with green checks travelling station to station.
- **0:00–0:03 and 0:23–0:25:** an accent box that grows, then the key word types into it; readable at phone size.
- **0:18–0:22:** a ring of portraits bursts into a wall of real user content, then scatters over a world map. Scale in two steps.
- **Overall:** a persistent route line behind every scene, a carry that also means "pipeline".

## Avoid
- **No voice and a −19 LUFS mix,** both against the owner's dense-narration brief.
- **Stock-photo cards** for warehouse, packaging and delivery.
- **A five-item red ✕ list,** close to PowerPoint.
- **A CTA cursor that glides but never presses.**
