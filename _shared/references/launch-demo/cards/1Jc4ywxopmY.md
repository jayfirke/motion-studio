- **Type:** demo · **Product:** Fronter, a visual feedback tool that lays a private comment layer over a website or mock-up for teams and clients

## Visual style
Real-photo bookends (a bright office, slightly soft) wrap a middle of pure product UI full-frame. Deep indigo is the one accent: layer border, buttons, active tabs. Flat circular icons and avatars. No added captions or titles: the product's own type and the voice carry everything.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Real UI in a plain browser frame, filling the screen.
- A large white pointing-hand cursor with a dark outline (about 4 % of frame height) for every action.
- Hand lands and the UI responds at once: tab underline moves, modal swaps, page reflows.
- Camera zooms tight onto the control being pressed (0:45).
- Drag-to-select draws a dashed box before the comment opens (0:27).
- The concept (a private layer) shown by one graphic act: a thick blue frame round the browser (0:19).

## Motion and transitions
Push from the desk photo into the laptop (0:11–0:13) and pull back out (0:47) carry the viewer in and out of the product. Inside, the camera re-frames and zooms within one continuous screen while modals open and close. Quick, functional motion, no flourish.

## Pacing
168 words/min, the fastest voice in this batch, and almost every sentence pairs with a click. Each UI state holds 2–3 s. The only breaths are the logo on the laptop (0:06) and the end card. It feels like a real person using the app at speed.

## Sound
Light pop bed near 123 BPM, -21.6 LUFS. One narrator, direct second person ("you can annotate…, mention…, toggle…"). 176 events/min: clicks under the hand plus pops for icons and cards.

## What to take from it
- 0:13–0:47: real UI with a big hand cursor, one click per spoken verb: the "looks like a demo" grammar the owner asked for.
- 0:45: the camera zooms onto the exact button as it is pressed, legible at 360 px.
- 0:27–0:29: dashed selection, comment card, mention, Add comment: a full interaction in 2 s.
- 0:11, 0:47: push into the laptop to enter the product, pull back out for the result.
- 0:19: the product idea as one graphic act (an accent frame round the browser).

## Avoid
- Notification-badge icon swarm with third-party logos (0:01–0:03).
- Stock office photo as the bookend set.
- Mix at -21.6 LUFS.
