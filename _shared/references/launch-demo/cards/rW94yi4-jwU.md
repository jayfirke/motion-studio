## Visual style
Two worlds: a pale grey-white studio for brand beats (glass 3D logo, purple type) and a saturated purple-to-teal gradient for the product beats. UI cards are pure white with large radii and soft shadows. Italic medium sans for the short captions, set on the same tilt as the cards. Label chips in solid violet over footage.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn, simplified UI as separate floating cards, each tilted about 20–30° in 3D, one control per card (toggle, slider, list, grid).
- Arrow cursor performs the core action on each card: drag the age slider, pick a state, click an interest tile (selected tile fills blue).
- Captions sit beside the card on the same perspective plane, three words each.
- Inventory shown as a tilted logo wall the camera sweeps along, not a list.
- Cards enter from below on the cut (0:07, 0:10, 0:12) so the cut lands on motion.

## Motion and transitions
Hard cuts on the beat between cards, each cut landing on a card already moving. Brand beats use wipes to white. The logo glass shapes bookend the film (assemble at 0:01, reassemble at 0:32), a neat loop. The footage montage changes exactly every 1.0 s (25.8, 26.8, 27.8, 28.8, 29.8 s), about two beats at 118 BPM. Snappy ease-out on cards, heavy motion blur on footage.

## Pacing
Very tight: no shot longer than 5.3 s, a new state every 1–2 s, one three-word caption per state. Only the logo holds (0:02–0:04, 0:33–0:35). Without voice it works because each card shows a single control.

## Sound
Music only, loud and clean at -13.9 LUFS (right on social target), upbeat around 118 BPM. 142 sound events/min: clicks on each cursor action, swishes on card entries, ticks on the 1 s montage changes.

## What to take from it
- 0:07–0:14: one control per tilted card, the cursor does exactly one action on each (drag, pick, click), a caption on the same plane. Three features in seven seconds, each readable.
- 0:15–0:17: a tilted wall of logos the camera sweeps along to show scale.
- 0:25–0:30: a montage of real outputs on a steady 1 s clock, each with a label chip; proof by volume.
- 0:01 and 0:32: the logo built from parts at the start and reassembled at the end, a loop.

## Avoid
- Letter-by-letter fade-in captions (0:05, 0:24): reads as a typing gimmick and is essentially an opacity fade.
- Glossy glass 3D logo shapes: off-brand for a code-first studio, and close to decorative.
- Music only: the owner wants Sarah's narration; keep the pace but add the voice.
