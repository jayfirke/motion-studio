- **Type:** demo (partner-integration product explainer) · **Product:** Lumin by Fosfor, a decision-intelligence and analytics assistant running natively on Snowflake

  - An incidents dashboard with a typed search and a chart that builds (0:47–0:54).

## Visual style
- One-hue world: teal and mint gradients with large faint angled shapes behind.
- Frosted-glass UI panels with white cards.
- Dark navy only for the opening and closing line-icon chapters.
- Thin white line icons inside circles, with lines that travel and connect.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Redrawn but faithful product screens on glass cards, tilted in 3D (rotate X and Y about 10–20°) and drifting slowly.
- A teal arrow cursor clicks chips, tables and the final URL.
- Loaders ("Analyzing…", shimmer bars) show the work happening.
- Extreme close-ups on one control group (the summary dropdowns at 1:03) alternate with wide panel views.

## Motion and transitions
- The line-icon chapters are one continuous path: the line draws, an icon blooms in a circle, the camera follows the line to the next one.
- UI panels slide in with directional motion blur (0:22), stack, then the camera pushes in.
- Blank-teal frames act as breaths (0:33, 1:01).

## Pacing
- 132 wpm, steady and busy.
- Each UI state holds about 3–4 s.
- The one long hold (19.5 s) is the hook, kept alive by the travelling line.

## Sound
- Upbeat ~129 BPM electronic bed under a confident narrator.
- Dense SFX (169 events/min), probably ticks and whooshes on panel moves.
- Mastered quiet at −22.4 LUFS, about 8 dB under our −14 target.

## What to take from it
- **Cursor plus loader plus answer (0:55–0:58):** click a chip, "Analyzing…", the chart arrives. Cause, work and result in 3 s.
- **Motion-blur whip-in of a panel (0:22):** a fast directional slide reads as energy without a cut.
- **Close-up on one control group (1:03):** cut from a wide panel to a huge crop of the dropdowns so the viewer reads one thing.
- **Travelling-line icon chain (0:00–0:19):** one line carries the camera through the problem beats.
- **Cursor clicks the URL on the end card (1:35):** the call to action is shown as an action.

## Avoid
- Light beams and glow on the closing icons.
- All-teal monochrome makes several panels hard to tell apart.
- A −22 LUFS master.
