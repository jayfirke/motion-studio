## Visual style
A pale lavender problem act with stock footage, then deep violet with soft purple glows for the product. One green accent (logo, toggles, button). UI on white rounded cards; section names in translucent glass pills with a line icon. Clean geometric sans, never more than a pill and a card on screen. A small logo bug sits top right on every product shot.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- Simplified real UI as floating white cards, no device frame, no tilt.
- **Avatar cursor:** a round face photo on an arrow pointer glides in and clicks a date (0:38, 0:42). The click has a person behind it.
- **Product inside other tools:** Gmail icon → Gmail window → side panel, then the same panel in a CRM.
- Typing suggested by a prompt field (1:10) and chat bubbles stacking one by one (1:05).
- **Pill-to-header carry:** each pillar's pill lands large at centre (0:54, 0:59, 1:08, 1:15), shrinks to a header, and the UI card rises from the bottom under it.

## Motion and transitions
Few hard cuts; most changes are builds on one violet ground. Cards rise from below with a smooth ease-out; the pill-to-header move carries every pillar; the logo grows from one stroke. Proof photos fan out as three cards (1:38). Calm, no overshoot.

## Pacing
Moderate: 113 words/min, 4.2 s median shots, about 6–8 s per pillar (pill 1 s, card 5–7 s). The product act is well paced but never accelerates; there is no burst.

## Sound
Light corporate electronic bed (~118 BPM) under one measured narrator reading a brochure script. 147 events/min against 13 cuts/min suggests ticks and pops on every pill, card and bubble (not checked by ear). Very quiet mix.

## What to take from it
- Pill-to-header carry (0:54–1:15): the chapter label lands big, shrinks into the header, the UI rises under it.
- Avatar cursor clicking a calendar date (0:38): a face on the pointer makes the action human.
- Icon unfolds into its app window with the product inside (0:36–0:38).
- Transcript bubbles stacking one by one (1:05): a readable way to show a conversation.
- Logo born from a single drawn arc (0:22–0:25).

## Avoid
- Logo bug in the corner of every product shot: corner labels are banned.
- Typewriter title centred on a lavender gradient (0:11).
- Mascot with orbiting pills (0:29) and the app-icon swarm (0:07): childish.
- Stock "stressed worker" acting (0:16–0:20).
- Even pace and a quiet mix: it never builds, close to what the owner called slow.
