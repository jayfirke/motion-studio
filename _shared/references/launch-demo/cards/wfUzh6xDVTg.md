# Explainer Video Animation - UpRaise For Employee Garrison | Best SaaS Video | Motion Graphics

- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wfUzh6xDVTg · **Channel:** What a Story - The Video Partner for SaaS & AI · **Length:** 1:34
- **Type:** explainer · **Product:** Upraise for Employee Garrison, a Jira app for core HR (employee records, leave, calendar, directory, Slack approvals)
- **Numbers:** 2.5 transitions per min (one continuous morphing canvas) · median shot 20.8 s (min 6.3, max 46.4) · 143 words/min · ~99 BPM · 108 sound events/min · −20.7 LUFS

## Structure (beats with times)
- **Hook (0:00–0:06):** four gold medals swing in on ribbons, one per department, then morph into round character vignettes.
- **Problem (0:08–0:16):** the vignettes stack onto a staircase (0:10), then topple into a pile (0:14).
- **Solution (0:17–0:27):** a circle wipe clears the frame and the logo writes on. A character sits next to a Jira window, and a pointing-hand cursor taps a date on a Leave Request calendar (0:26).
- **Feature map (0:29–0:40):** hexagon tiles pop onto a faint honeycomb (records, leave, calendar, directory), then snap around a Jira hex (0:37).
- **Profiles (0:41–0:48):** the hand cursor clicks a "Profile attributes" tab, an Add Attribute modal opens over a dimmed screen, and checkboxes tick (0:45).
- **Leave (0:46–1:03):** the cursor taps the leave hex, the camera dives into a world map (0:52), and avatar pins pop with red and green status dots linked to a manager (0:57).
- **Slack and directory (1:05–1:22):** a "Leave Request" pill with Approve and Reject buttons, the org-chart Teams view clicked (1:17), and a January calendar with avatars in the cells.
- **CTA (1:24–1:34):** a cycle ring of department arcs that morphs into the logo, then the URL and a "Get Started" button.

## Visual style
Flat 2D vector with friendly characters in circular vignettes. Pastel: an ice-cyan background, royal blue, and a warm accent per department. A rounded sans at small sizes inside shapes, with almost no headlines. Soft shadows; no real depth. Clean but illustration-first.

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- **The UI:** redrawn and simplified (Jira chrome, sidebar, a few rows) as flat white windows, with no tilt and no device.
- **The cursor:** a cartoon pointing hand. Each tap (0:26, 0:41, 0:46, 1:17) opens the next state: a tab switch, a modal over a dimmed background, checkbox ticks.
- **Map as navigation:** features live in hex tiles, and the cursor taps a hex to "open" it (0:46).
- **Diagrams:** some features are drawn as diagrams rather than UI (the world map, the cycle ring).

## Motion and transitions
- **Continuous morph:** only three detected changes. Medals become vignettes, stairs become a pile, hexes become the Jira cluster, the ring becomes the logo.
- **Carry:** the circle wipe (0:17, 1:01) resets the frame, and a hex tap plus a camera dive carries from overview to detail (0:46 to 0:52).
- **Easing:** icons and pins pop with soft overshoot; windows ease out. Cartoon-friendly, not premium.

## Pacing
The voice is the densest layer (143 wpm of long compound sentences), while the picture changes about every 4 s with gentle moves, following the VO noun by noun. There are no rests, and the quiet mix makes the speed feel flat.

## Sound
- **Music:** a light, bouncy acoustic-corporate bed at about 99 BPM.
- **Voice:** a fast, cheerful explainer read with a "that's where X comes in" turn.
- **SFX:** many small pops and taps (108/min) on icons, pins and cursor taps.
- **Loudness:** a very quiet master.

## What to take from it
- **0:29–0:37 and 0:46:** a feature map that doubles as navigation. The cursor taps a tile and the camera dives into that feature.
- **0:41–0:45:** a tab click, a modal over a dimmed screen, then checkboxes ticking: one cause per state.
- **0:52–0:57:** a zoom into a map where status pins pop with coloured dots, a quick "many people, many places".
- **1:24–1:28:** category arcs collapsing into the logo, so the content becomes the brand mark.

## Avoid
- **A cartoon hand cursor, flat characters and overshoot pops,** which read as a kids' explainer.
- **Redrawn UI with empty windows** (0:49 is a blank window).
- **Medal and staircase metaphors.**
- **A 143 wpm VO on a −20.7 LUFS mix.**

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **Setup:** a tile map of the chapters or tools. The real cursor clicks one tile and the camera pushes into that real screen (once, as the star carry).
- **Previs Studio tour:** a click, modal and tick chain on the real previs page (open an option, choose B, a note marker drops), with real click foley on each step.
- **CTA:** a ring of agent logos closing into the Motion Studio mark.
