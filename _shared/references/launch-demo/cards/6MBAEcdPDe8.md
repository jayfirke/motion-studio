## Visual style
- Deliberately every style at once: flat 2D, 3D clay, glass SaaS UI, halftone collage, live action, kinetic type.
- What ties it together is the cut rhythm and a recurring yellow from the studio brand (the bolt, the dot, the type).

## How the product UI is shown (the demo grammar)
- UI appears as 0.25–1 s glimpses: tilted dashboards, chat panels, a cursor on a report form.
- Good evidence that a tilted UI panel with one clear chart reads even at a quarter second.

## Motion and transitions
- Mostly hard cuts on the beat grid, with in-shot motion continuing across cuts (rings spinning, cards arcing) so it never feels static.
- A yellow circle wipe (0:07) and the dot-to-logo grow (0:56) are the brand carries.

## Pacing
- The structure is a tempo ladder: half-beat flurry, then two-beat cuts for 30 s, then a strobe, then one-beat cuts, then the logo.
- The 30 s of steady one-per-second cuts is the "rest" between the bursts.

## Sound
- Music only: a driving ~118 BPM track, crushed to −11.8 LUFS, very loud.
- 190 events/min: hits and swishes on every cut.

## What to take from it
- **Tempo ladder (0:03 → 0:41):** half-beat, two-beat, strobe, one-beat. Varying shot length 4× and more is exactly the house rule, done on the grid.
- **Cuts locked to the measured grid (0:10–0:40):** every second on the dot. This is how our "burst of hits" sections should be built.
- **Big service word over live action (0:49, 0:54):** one bold word ("SOUND") over footage, held for one beat.
- **Configurator UI with a cursor pick (0:51):** a quick self-demo even inside a reel.

## Avoid
- The −11.8 LUFS master (our limit is −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP).
- The strobe burst and the flame-head gag are glitch-adjacent.
- Mixing every style suits a reel, but a single film needs one look.

## Ideas for the motion-studio launch film
- **"Results" chapter:** a beat-locked montage of our finished films. Start with half-beat glimpses (0.25 s), settle into one per bar, end on one held frame.
- **Hook:** one 0.25 s flurry of the engines (HyperFrames, Remotion, Diffusion) before landing on Claude Code for 3 s.
- **"Build and critique":** one-word supers ("SOUND", "MOTION", "SCORE") each over a real shot of that step, one beat each.
