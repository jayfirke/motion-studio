# Launch and demo film library: index

A reference library of product launch and product demo motion-graphics films, studied 2026-10-06 for motion-studio. Read [PLAYBOOK.md](PLAYBOOK.md) for the rules distilled from it.

- **187 videos** from two YouTube playlists and three single links. **All 187 were studied** (frames, cut detection, loudness, tempo, transcript); 16 of them on a second pass on 2026-10-06, because the first download hit caption rate limits.
- 180 of the 187 come from one agency, What a Story, so the library shows one house style very well and the rest of the field only a little.
- Each row links the video and its card (`cards/<id>.md`). Raw numbers are in `data/<id>.json` (cut times, shot lengths, loudness, tempo, sound events, words per minute; transcripts removed). The contact sheets were frames of the films themselves, so they are not included: watch each film on YouTube from its link.
- **Rating** is how useful the film is as a reference for our launch film (5 = study it closely, 1 = skip). It is a judgement from the card, not a measurement.
- **Voice** is the measured words per minute of the caption track; "none" means music and on-screen text only.
- ★ marks the 15 films in [Watch these first](#watch-these-first).

Groups: [Launch](#launch) · [Demo](#demo) · [Explainer](#explainer) · [Brand/ad](#brandad) · [Agency talks](#agency-talks) · [Second pass](#second-pass) · [Watch these first](#watch-these-first)

## Watch these first

Fifteen films, in a sensible viewing order. Together they cover every rule in the playbook. About 17 minutes in all.

1. **[HeyGen Video Agent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k3ZItY0S57k)** (2:06, [card](cards/k3ZItY0S57k.md)). The closest product to ours: an AI agent that makes videos. It shows three finished videos by 0:13 and only then explains how. A "You" cursor and a labelled agent cursor share the screen (0:49, 1:16, 1:32), and a crew checklist resolves with spinners (1:00). 155 words/min.
2. **[Fronter](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Jc4ywxopmY)** (1:02, [card](cards/1Jc4ywxopmY.md)). The "looks like a demo" bar. A big hand cursor makes one click per spoken verb, and the camera zooms onto the button as it is pressed (0:45). 168 words/min, the fastest voice in the library.
3. **[Leadjet / Surfe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gjpXUD8Vz6I)** (1:00, [card](cards/gjpXUD8Vz6I.md)). The problem is acted out with the cursor: copy from a profile, paste field by field (0:09–0:11). Then the camera pushes onto each control just before the click and pulls back for the result (0:19–0:31).
4. **[GWI Spark](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wnzbApqFAsE)** (1:07, [card](cards/wnzbApqFAsE.md)). The template for "talk to Claude Code": a typed prompt with the camera following the caret, a streaming reply, chips filling in, a loader, then one giant number (0:01–0:35).
5. **[SurveySensum AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Afe8o2u1yY)** (1:21, [card](cards/_Afe8o2u1yY.md)). It opens on the prompt and the Ask click before any output (0:00–0:08), runs with no cut, and keeps one layout (label left, live chat right) for four features (0:39–0:49).
6. **[Avalara in Shopify Sidekick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GyYIBdrAASw)** (1:45, [card](cards/GyYIBdrAASw.md)). The problem as real friction: real browser tabs and a cursor hopping between them (0:13–0:20). Then five questions answered in 25 s, one per shot (0:39–1:04).
7. **[ResponseScribe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XePPHrTdPqA)** (0:38, [card](cards/XePPHrTdPqA.md)). The tightest script in the library: pain, definition, three steps, one control beat, CTA. The app tile grows out of a real laptop (0:09), and an avatar cursor edits a reply as it types (0:28–0:31).
8. **[Kitaabh dashboard spot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XGlri_JubsA)** (0:39, [card](cards/XGlri_JubsA.md)). Click, morph, every 1.6 s for 16 s inside one modal (0:10–0:26). The model for switching A, B and C in Previs Studio.
9. **[Viable](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2AWzLDFJXjo)** (1:09, [card](cards/2AWzLDFJXjo.md)). 69 s with no cut. Problem icons burst out of a real monitor and get pulled back in (0:00–0:10), then an isometric drill-down with the cursor (0:29–0:50).
10. **[Membrain Elevate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2yL0LhyxOGk)** (1:10, [card](cards/2yL0LhyxOGk.md)). The energy curve: 48 s of continuous camera, then 1.5–2.5 s action cuts (0:48–0:58). Headlines sit on the UI's own tilted plane (0:31). −15.0 LUFS.
11. **[Axias](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-tPZCQsQEqo)** (1:25, [card](cards/-tPZCQsQEqo.md)). One hero component, the Timeline, returns three times and grows (0:48, 1:09, 1:13). Status badges pop onto one persistent card (0:57–1:06). The model for our critique scorecard.
