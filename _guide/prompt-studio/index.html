Recorded foley: the ad palette

house default for ads

A real click for every press, one switch for toggles, key taps on visible typing, airy whooshes on moves, a riser into the reveal and one impact as the loudest moment. Every sample here was used in the test films. Freesound files are CC0 and come from _shared/tools/freesound.py get <id>. Mixkit files are render-only, so they have no preview on this page.

  • Fewer sounds than beats, in one shared room (a light common reverb).
  • Runs: alternate two samples, step the volume down and tighten the gaps; when hits blur, play one swoosh instead.
  • Whooshes: a slow swell (attack 300 ms or more), dark (centroid under 1.3 kHz), low-passed near 6 kHz, under the voice.
  • Pin each sound to its shot start plus an offset, after picture lock. Check the hits on an SFX-only stem, not the full mix.
  • Never in an ad: synth plucks, bloops, notification tones, success chimes, cartoon bounces.

Previs first: see and hear it before anything renders
Demo: _shared/previs/studio

    
Added 2026-10-06 after five films showed that most critic notes could have been caught before a render. The video-director-previs skill plans the film as one director and publishes a playable page; the engine only builds what you approve there.

    
    
      
  • A playable animatic with Sarah's lines, the music bed (ducked under the voice) and the foley, on the measured beat grid.
    • 
      
  • Three options, A/B/C, on every decision: direction, music, motion feel, pacing, and per scene the transition, camera, voice line and each sound. The director's pick is marked; any swap replays at once.
    • 
      
  • Pause on a frame or drag across the timeline, press N, and write what you want. Claude changes only that, logs it, and republishes the same link.
    • 
      
  • Approve in the page. Claude freezes approved-video-spec.json, writes SHOTLIST.md from it, and the engine builds exactly that.
    • 
      
  • Cost: one session, no workflows or critic agents, voice on Fish's free model. The first sample is the fictional Crumb app film (9:16, 21 s).

What five films taught

    
The notes fresh critics gave round after round in our test films, and the fixes that passed. The prompts above already ask for these; check stills for them before round 1.

    

      
Carry, don't swap
    
        
  • No one-frame swaps and no empty boxes: morph the container (size, place and radius together) while old content leaves upward and new content rises in, 2 frames of overlap.
    • 
        
  • Start each shot from the exact last frame of the one before, camera transform included.
    • 
        
  • Make the carry literal: PULSE's gauge only worked once the arc was drawn on the ring itself, and SHIFT's red row became line 42.
    • 
        
  • Groups move as one rigid stack; landed cards draw on top and nothing crosses in flight.
    • 
      

      
Text that reads on a phone
    
        
  • UI text under 3 % of the frame failed every time. Push the camera in; don't enlarge the fonts.
    • 
        
  • At most two text levels, and never a line alone on empty paper.
    • 
        
  • Mask exits leave glyph slivers: keep them under 2 frames, give masks descender room, and start serif rises at least 200 % below.
    • 
        
  • The CTA is the most legible line and reads for 1.5 s before the press.
    • 
      

      
Camera and composition
    
        
  • A third of the frame empty for more than 1 s caps the composition score, dark plinths and macros included.
    • 
        
  • A locked camera for a whole act reads as dead; a camera that follows a falling object turns the fall into a lift. Cut on the beat into a held hero frame instead.
    • 
        
  • The press must visibly cause the effect: about 4 % squash and a shadow drop, held, then the UI reacts. The cursor tip sits at the button's edge, never on the label.
    • 
        
  • A full-frame veil eases out before a cut; switched off, it reads as a flash.
    • 
      

      
Sound
    
        
  • Recorded foley, airy whooshes, and the impact as the loudest moment: step the bed down into it, and keep it at least 2 LU over the loudest voice line.
    • 
        
  • Measure the tempo from the kick onsets: the Flowboard bed needed a comb fit (122.0), Cat Walk is 129.98 not 129.2, and Bell Beats is 120 where the tracker said 96.
    • 
        
  • Measure render-vs-mix lag by cross-correlation, not nearest onset; that hid a 43 ms and a 77 ms error. Score hits on an SFX-only stem.
    • 
        
  • A 4× oversampled limiter drops the last 0.15 s: pad the master (apad, atrim) before muxing.
    • 
      

      
Render contract in practice
    
        
  • Seek-anywhere renders leak state: reset every element every frame, and never set a child to visibility: visible (it beats a hidden parent; use inherit).
    • 
        
  • Sub-pixel glyphs paint differently depending on the previous frame: round text offsets to whole pixels and grow an end card with font-size, not a transform.
    • 
        
  • three.js: a transparent shadow catcher needs depthWrite: false; a clipping plane at the desk lets an object rise out of the page.
    • 
        
  • motion-reel's C.seg, sp and spHit take beats, never seconds.
    • 
      

      
Budget and process
    
        
  • Plan for 5 rounds: Flowboard needed 9 to reach SHIP; capped films ended at 7–8 and got a self-checked polish pass, reported as unscored.
    • 
        
  • A port to a second engine is cheap: PULSE moved to Diffusion in about 30 minutes with four engine-specific changes.
    • 
        
  • Delete PNG frame dumps after the mux (SHIFT's 836 frames were about 2 GB) and keep one render running at a time.
    •

The prompt's six parts (from the HyperFrames prompting guide)

    

      
RouteWhich skill or workflow, and where to work.

      
SpecLength, size and frame rate, plus the mood (or a full style).

      
BeatsWhat happens when, second by second.

      
CopyThe exact on-screen words.

      
TechniqueExact names: animations, blocks, sounds, music, fonts, icons.

      
NegativesWhat must not happen, plus the review steps.
